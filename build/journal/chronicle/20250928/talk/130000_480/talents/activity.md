At the board Mistress Ansell called the jaw a quince and named Master Yaxley at a shilling. You said after Thursday, and you meant the king. She told you a face like a pudding would serve you both right. Verbeke offered a thread, a door, and a friend willing to shut it. Alse left, and came back with sops. You said Saturday's thirty-four shillings was the best since Whitsun, and that you would give half of it to chew. You did not name the half as a sum. The Mayor's letter stays out of what you will write.
