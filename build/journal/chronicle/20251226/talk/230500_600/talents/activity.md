In the yeomen's buttery, over hippocras, the night was told and not played again. You had gone in first on the line, in the paper crown and the old velvet, and you had not looked at the state, and you had seen the white staff and the stillness in the chair. She laughed once, at Pope's Armado, from deep in the chest. Kemp's Costard said what was set down and three lines over, to her face, and her hand lifted. Sincler came on in black at the last, and the hall stayed quiet through Tu-whit to-who until the chair stirred. Bryan said she kept her seat to the songs, as good as a purse and likelier. You refused the likeness as a sum. My lord clapped your shoulder hard enough to rattle what teeth you have left, and left without a word. Nothing was paid. Nick had held under the note.
