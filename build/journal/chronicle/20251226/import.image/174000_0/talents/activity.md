At half past five the players' room was plain. A few tallow candles, a small fire, a rolled part, a gilt crown set aside, and someone's hands finishing a fastening. Through the door the hall showed a hundred white wax tapers over sweet-flag rushes, and the corner of a canopy of state with nobody under it. The players waiting in the service passage are this room, not the morning's towers. Your hand was on the papers. Your face was not.
