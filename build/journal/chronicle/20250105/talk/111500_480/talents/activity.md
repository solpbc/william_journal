You stood in the porch of St Helen's after morning prayer. You had spent the sermon counting twenty pounds that is not yours: shoes, your father's small debts, a horse at Lent. Master Thwaite gave you one sentence about the unjust steward, and you kept only the half of it. Mistress Ansell named a servant of Crosby Place under her breath, and said she had done no such thing when you told her her voice had dropped. Sir John Spencer left from three rows ahead and did not speak to you. The loaf is still a penny and a finger short, which she had already measured on Friday.
