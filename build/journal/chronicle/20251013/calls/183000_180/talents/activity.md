You spoke to Gilbert from Great St Helen's and gave him joy of the day. He was at his lodging in St Bride's. The money had gone down safe by the carrier of the second. A wool-man at the Bell on Saturday had brought that word, and a whole cheese of your mother's make, half of it yours. He said you could not chew, and that he would eat your half and call it his birthday. The frame raised in August is thatched. Your father says it is the first thing a player ever held up. You told him the tooth comes out tomorrow at ten, at twelve pence. He would have done it for six with the shop pliers. You asked him to keep you the rind. The cheese stays with him.
