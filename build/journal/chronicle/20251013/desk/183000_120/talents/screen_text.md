## 00:00 · The glass

Gilbert? Will. From Great St Helen's, with a face like a pudding. God give you joy of the day.
Brother. Gilbert. From my lodging in St Bride's. Twenty-nine, if the almanac is honest.
Then joy of it. I can say the word. I cannot chew a feast.
The money went down safe by the carrier of the second. A Stratford wool-man at the Bell on Saturday brought the word back, and a cheese with it. Mother's make. A whole one. The note says half is yours.
