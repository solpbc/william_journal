At the door the gowns were already in. Margery said every third man had asked when the king is played, and that she had said Thursday next. They shouted Remuneration at Kemp. A gentleman of Gray's Inn gave you the opening of Gaunt's England and asked if it would be printed. You said it was the company's, and sent him to the book-keeper. Abel told him to stand off the book, and looked at him as at a burglar. A gentleman of the Middle Temple had York's line about Hereford's rights, and meant to argue a dead man's will at a moot. You had not thought the line a lawyer's. The next king is Thursday the sixteenth. The Rose's bill was The Grecian Comedy. You did not go. You went up as Navarre, and the jaw did the vowels.
