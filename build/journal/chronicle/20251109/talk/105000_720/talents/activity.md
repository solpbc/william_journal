In the south porch after morning prayer, Master Thwaite had turned the glass twice, and Sir John Spencer had gone out with no sword-bearer and without speaking. Mistress Ansell said she had not heard you walk in the night. You told her the dagger was in and the two fathers were still outside. She took your back for Tuesday's stores after a quarter past ten. Alse said the loaf was a full thumb short of the notch and still a penny. You did not dip the pen.
