## 03:00 · Four lines, struck

See how the casement frames her leaning cheeke,
The leaded diamond catching at her sleeue,
And all the carued stone of her fathers wall
Growne soft as orchard turfe beneath her hand.

## 06:00 · What the yard can use

Nurse. From behind. Not seen.
The whisper is for the yard to lean in.
The rest may be said across the rail.
