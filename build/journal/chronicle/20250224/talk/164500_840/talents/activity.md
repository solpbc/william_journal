After the play Margery Pell called the house two-thirds of Thursday's, and louder, and gave no sum. The Inns had called "Remuneration!" before the line, and the gallery had taken up the long Latin word with Costard. Kemp had added thirteen lines. Garnish put the two days at twenty-four, and said there was no song. Kemp said they had laughed at his lines and that he had counted. You said the hush on the messenger was not an accident, and you owned the lines that brought a drifting yard back. Burbage asked whether the ending had worked. You told him what the yard did, not what you had meant. A lawyer at the door asked who made the play, and Burbage pointed with his thumb. James Burbage wants you at the Theatre at ten of the clock in the morning, not at nine in the yard.
