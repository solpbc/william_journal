Verbeke came home early by the back wall, his cap in his hand. Mistress Ansell acted before the talk could start. She put him in the lumber garret over your chamber with a jug and a pot. She swore the household on her Bible, you first. If any ask, the back room is let to a man from Norwich. She said he was from Norwich tonight, and that he had always had a Norwich face. You said it. Alse said it. He said it. She would not have the latch changed. Nobody came.
