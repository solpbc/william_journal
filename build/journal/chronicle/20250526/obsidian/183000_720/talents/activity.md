You wrote one sheet to Susanna and to Anne. You asked to be told the fault plainly, said the Master of the Revels had done you good, and asked whether a queen would learn bad news from gardeners. Love to Judith and Hamnet went in the same line. You told Anne the king is two-thirds made and that you came in a line late as Navarre. You asked which one thing the house should wait to buy, struck your own answer, and left a blot. The thimble was 2s 6d and went on the fifteenth. The sheet goes on the fifth of June. You promised to hear Susanna read in August.
