At the Split Hazel, before the play, Kemp told the hamper, the stool, the line and the glove, and Alice made him move his elbows before she allowed the end. You enjoyed it. Then, in the same voice, he asked whether the Queen's twenty pounds had come. You said it is expected. She said expected money has eaten several dinners in this house and never paid for one. Kemp laughed loudest and paid for his own. You paid fourpence, and you did not put the twenty pounds in your purse.
