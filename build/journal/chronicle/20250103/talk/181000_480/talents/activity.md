You paid threepence and sat to salt ling, a penny loaf and small beer. Mistress Ansell laid the loaf against the notch and found it short by the width of a finger. She asked what you were walking about to on Tuesday night. You told her: four gentlemen swear to study three years and see no woman, and four ladies arrive. She said they are forsworn before supper, and she would not give a pin for a play that begins in a broken oath. Verbeke asked whether the ladies are punished too. You have no sentence for them. Alse asked who does the washing for three years. You wrote washing. The cat was on your stool when you got up.
