Walking home from the Cross Keys under a half moon, you let the play end on the laugh and then refused that ending. A man comes in, in black, from her father's court, says half a line, and she ends it. You tried it three ways, each shorter. They do not marry today. A twelvemonth, earned where you cannot see. You said Heminges will call it no ending, and that it is the only ending. You stopped on a doorstep. The frost was on the pump.
