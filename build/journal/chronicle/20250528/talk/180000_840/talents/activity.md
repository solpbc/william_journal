The clerk had left a precept from Crosby Place, to be read on Sunday next: no blue starch in ruffs. Mistress Ansell had it by heart. She said starch is good wheat boiled to vanity, and blue is vanity dyed. You asked if the Mayor had found the dearth in our collars. She said you may laugh, and that he is a hard man and right about ruffs. Alse owns one band starched blue, bought at Whitsun last. She was allowed to wash it white. At the table the loaf was a finger and a half short, and a penny.
