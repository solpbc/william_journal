Wednesday 21 May.
Board, iij d. The loaf a penny, a finger and a half short.

The Flint sheet, this afternoon.
The king is come down. The lords look at his hands. He looks at the ground.

Heard yesterday, at the bridge foot. Six pound, and I never argue the jig.
They have spent heavily. The money was not wasted. I am not pricing their house.

A looking-glass against a giant. Not on this sheet. The room is not written.
