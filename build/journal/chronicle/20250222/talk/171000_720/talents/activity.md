The week's share came to thirty shillings, the best since Christmas, in a yard the snow had thinned. Margery was right to the penny. Garnish read the margin: Kemp's interest, Thursday, eleven lines, and no song. Kemp asked the interest on Burbage and was told there is no such column. You said thirty shillings, and nothing clever. Edith's eight shillings stayed under the candlestick, unpaid. The watchet stayed unpaid. Heminges asked, half in jest, for a Love's Labour's Won. You said you have not written it, and will not.
