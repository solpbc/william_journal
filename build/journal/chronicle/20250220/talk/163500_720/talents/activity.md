In the tiring-parlour the boxes held seven pound eleven shillings. Margery said the yard went out quiet, and that she had never known a comedy do it. Garnish had ticked eleven lines of Kemp's and would not round them. Burbage had ridden Berowne until the black came in. Pope had stopped the play twice. Nick and Arthur had sent the Muscovites back, and Nick had governed the last scene. Sincler's half-line put the laugh out. You had gone on first in a paper crown, and you did not explain the quiet.
