A young man of the Inner Temple came in to praise Berowne, and then said Southwell was tried at Westminster today before the Lord Chief Justice, for treason, and condemned. He said the priest had given his age as thirty-three, and that he was a poet. You were not at that trial. You asked, courteously, when it was to be, and you were told tomorrow. You rubbed your forefinger against your thumb. Bryan turned the talk to the unpaid gown. The table went on eating. You did not make a likeness, and you did not go to see.
