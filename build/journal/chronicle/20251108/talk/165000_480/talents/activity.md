By candle, after Titus had ended without you, Heminges counted thirty-four shillings as the share. He said it had gone four. Last Saturday was thirty-five, and that sum is not this week's. The hired men's ten shillings were not taken out of the thirty-four. No coffer total was read. Next week is Errors on Monday, with you opening, the Two Gentlemen on Tuesday, and the king on Wednesday. The Rose title was Wise Man. There was no receipt. No fee was taken out of the share. You promised no errand and no unwritten line.
