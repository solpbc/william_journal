Heminges called from Aldermanbury. The court is at Whitehall because the Queen came up yesterday. My lord of Huntingdon is dead in the north, and she would tell the Countess herself. You had already heard from Kettleby's boy that she goes back Monday and that the players are looked for at Richmond. His tilt-boat is Davy Gannet's, for Wednesday's flood, with a deposit down and no sum spoken. If she does not go Monday, you follow her, and he named a second deposit without a figure.
