At the reckoning Heminges would not rule a line under the week. You let the fourth time be written, and you refused a share. Three Richmond nights are looked for and not received. Eleven pair of hose were called drying, not money. The deposit and the second deposit stayed unspoken figures. Two cloaks stay out until Innocents' night. The sixty shillings were a sending on Thursday, not a balance. The new moon was given no hour. The coffer was not counted.
