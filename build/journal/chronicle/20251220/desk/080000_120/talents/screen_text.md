## 00:00 · The glass

Will? John. From Aldermanbury, with eleven pair of hose drying before the fire.
Eleven pair will dry. Say the rest.
The court is at Whitehall. The Queen came up from Richmond yesterday of a sudden. My lord of Huntingdon is dead in the north, and she would break it to the Countess herself.
I had it from Kettleby's boy an hour since. She goes back Monday. The players are looked for at Richmond as appointed.
