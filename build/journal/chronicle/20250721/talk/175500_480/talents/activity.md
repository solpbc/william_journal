At the Bell, Hobbins put Anne's letter in your hand. You broke it in the yard. The fire was on Tuesday the fifteenth, at the north end beyond the Rother Market, about twenty houses and barns. Nobody in the house was hurt. Sadler's cousin lost his malt-barn, and Quiney is writing for a new brief before winter. The forty houses were an alehouse story from Wendover. She wrote not to come for the fire, and to come for harvest if you come. Her hand was steady.
