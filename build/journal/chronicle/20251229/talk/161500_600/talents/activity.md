Mistress Ansell met you on the stair with a jug in her hand. The chamber had been aired. She had not been in the coffer. Your letters were where you left them. The latch had shut the first time, and she will not take money for it. Gib was on the Chaucer, moved off Field's Holinshed. No player had slept on the floor. Supper at the house is at six. You said you would put my lord's ten shillings behind both locks and not count the rest. You did not ask her to name the parish day by day.
