The seven sharers were at the Bull, and nobody had proposed it. There was roast capon, and lamb's-wool with the apples bursting. Heminges set the book open on two leaves and would not let the purse and the three nights share one. Kemp told the button at length. Five pearls, a short candle, rosemary, and the yeomen's tapers done in spoons. Nothing was found. You said the story was a lie if it found the button. It did not. You left by four, for the two hundred yards home. Your ten shillings were still uncounted against the rest.
