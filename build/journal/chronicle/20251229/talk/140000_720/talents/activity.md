You were at Old Swan by two, off the tilt-boat. You did not tell the middle of the river. Gannet had three pieces of news, and you kept one: Twelfth Day, which Kettleby had already brought. There was no ice, and the Queen had not paid you in the boat. Nick asked what a queen does when she is a man, and you told him he learns to stand differently, and that he has time. He did not sing. Kemp wants the ass's head by Candlemas, with the ears a hand longer than the head. The baskets had gone on to Shoreditch in a cart. Heminges did not name a house.
