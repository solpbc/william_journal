Kettleby came to the Feathers among the baskets, before the boat, and said my lord is content. The purse was my lord's own, not the Chamber's: four pounds, for hose. Heminges divided it on a barrel-head into eight parts of ten shillings and did not read the names. He put that purse on one leaf and the Queen's three nights on another, expected, not received. An office is still empty. You did not argue. You are looked for again at Twelfth Day. Two borrowed cloaks went back to a yeoman, counted, and he did not speak. Your ten shillings stayed in your purse.
