From London, the xxx of December 1595.

Anne. We played three nights and were liked. The Queen laughed at Pope, once, aloud. We shall be paid when an office is filled. You asked me a thing at Shottery brook and I answered it ill. I work in London. I live where you are. The rest is arithmetic. It is nine pounds more than at New Year, after all that went down by the carrier. I have asked nobody the price of any house. I will not, until the sum is not laughable. Tell me when it loses another pane.

Susanna. Thy groat was for spending foolishly. If the book is in thy hand, it has no riddle, and I will not set one on it now. Write me the two faults if thou hast found them.

Judith. Thy groat was for spending foolishly. If the sheet is in the kitchen, the tune at the head must be sung, or it is not read. Sing it once. I am not naming the tune.

Hamnet. Thy groat was for spending foolishly. The ball is for the yard, and not for a speech. I have not asked anyone at court about a sword. I am not coming home to see the yard.

God keep thee.
Will.
