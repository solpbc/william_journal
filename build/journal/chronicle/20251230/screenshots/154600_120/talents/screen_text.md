## 00:00 · Looked at Gresham's plain tomb

The porch of St Helen's, from a man's height, looking in. Sir Thomas Gresham's great chest-tomb stands in the nave, a plain mass of stone. The two naves run side by side, the nuns' and the parish's. The light in the church is thin and grey. The close beyond the door is already dim. No hand is on the stone. No face, and no shadow of a man.
