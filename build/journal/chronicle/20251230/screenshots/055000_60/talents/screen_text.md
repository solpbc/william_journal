## 00:00 · Candle and frost

The chamber before daylight. One tallow candle on the trestle desk, the flame small and steady. Frost ferns on the west panes, and the close beyond them still black. The brass inkhorn and the round pounce-box sit where they were left. The grey cloak hangs on the peg by the door. The coffer at the bed's foot is shut. No face, and no shadow on the boards.
