## 02:00 · The foot, again

She sees a weaver in an ass's head, singing to show he is not afraid.
The workmen at their rehearsal in the wood. The weaver corrects them all.
The bellows-mender pipes, and the voice will have to be a girl's.
Most radiant Pyramus, most lilly white of hue,
He goes off into the brake a man. He comes back not.
The queen wakes among her flowers.
What Angell wakes me from my flowry bed?
