## 06:00 · The place, and not the day in figures

From London, the xxx of December 1595.
Anne. We played three nights and were liked. The Queen laughed at Pope, once, aloud.
We shall be paid when an office is filled.
You asked me a thing at Shottery brook and I answered it ill. I work in London. I live where you are. The rest is arithmetic.
It is nine pounds more than at New Year, after all that went down by the carrier.
Susanna. Thy groat was for spending foolishly.
God keep thee. Will.
