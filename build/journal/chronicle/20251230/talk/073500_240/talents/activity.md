Alse brought water that had gone cold on the second turning of the stair, and she would not heat it again. You told her to leave the letter: not to sand it, and not to carry it. Mistress Ansell, with a jug in her hands, said she would call once if you were still at work at dinner. You said once was enough. Alse had tried the mended latch and it had not stuck. The sheet stayed on the board.
