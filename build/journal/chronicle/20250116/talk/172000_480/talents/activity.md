Gilbert had brought the parcel from the house. You paid Hobbins sixpence for it and twopence for the letter, eightpence in all. He has six horses. The ruts are iron, and he walked the last mile in. He would not turn the inward bags out until the horses had drunk. Nothing from Stratford was put in your hand. You did not ask for a letter inward. Gilbert goes on to St Bride's after the horses drink.
