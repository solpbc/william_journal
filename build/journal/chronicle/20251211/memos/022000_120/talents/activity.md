In the dark hour you said his sentence over as you would a cue. You asked whether "it can be done" meant that a man can be made to pity a king put down, or that a king can be put down. You called them the same four words. You had looked at the hand, not the face. You tried the sentence as praise, as warning, and as a habit of saying nothing in a full sentence. You did not choose. You said that he saw the three leaves, that Hunsdon's daughter saw them, and that the Master cut them. Yesterday the lovers played a second time to a house nearly as full, and you said the Prologue with a steady heart. You stopped on Sleep.
