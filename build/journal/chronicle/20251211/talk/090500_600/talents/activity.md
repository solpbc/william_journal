Burbage asked by daylight, with Bryan there, whether Sir Robert was pleased. You said a powerful man's face is not a verdict, and that you have decided not to read it. Bryan reported what he saw. The steward was courteous. The purse was in Heminges's hand before the last speech, and not after. Lady Hoby thanked Nicholas and Arthur by name. Sir Robert rose when the women rose, and not before. You would not translate any of that into pleasure. The Queen's entrance in the garden was late. The shortened groom scene held, played on a floor six paces wide. The public book stays cut. The three leaves are not going back into it.
