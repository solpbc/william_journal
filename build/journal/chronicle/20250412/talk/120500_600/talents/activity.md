At her table Mistress Ansell asked whether you had given Anne her opinion of the forsworn gentlemen. You had. Anne had said the ladies were right to make them wait, and a twelvemonth was short. That silenced her for a herring. The loaf was a finger and a half short of the notch, and it was not put on a pan. Verbeke asked after the corn. You said it was up and thin, and you named no price. Alse asked if the children were like you. You gave her the three sentences. Mistress Ansell said they were well, and she took the threepence. The coffer was not counted again. Verbeke kept his name.
