After the Shrew, which you did not play, the tiring-house door shut. You gave them Kettleby word for word. John said twice that if you are sent for you go, and that a company which asks the Queen for money down is not sent for twice. The cost is the boat, the lodging, and the boys' meat for five days, out of a stock that holds six pound. Kemp stopped on being first, and told the grocer to be first. George will stand with John. You were second on the Queen's paper once. Tom said you were paid before Easter. No sum was written.
