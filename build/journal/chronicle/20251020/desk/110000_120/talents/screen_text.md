## 00:00 · The glass

Master Shakespeare? Ambrose Kettleby. From Somerset House, among the hampers.
Master Kettleby. I hear you. I am at the Theatre, and the yard is quieter than your hampers.
The court removes to Richmond today, and my lord with it. Master Heminges's question has reached me by three hands, or I should not have known to call.
Then I am glad of the three hands. Who signs, now Sir Thomas is dead?
