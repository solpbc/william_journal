At the day's end Raye brought the rest. The morning bill had been the second Caesar, and he had not seen a sum. It is their last. The Admiral's men are packing hampers, no bill goes up tomorrow, and nobody over there will say why. Kemp said they have run. Heminges said or been told, and that the whole Bankside has nowhere to go of an afternoon but here. Margery said she would as soon they had somewhere. The justices' padlocks were a rumour of one more tumult, and not a decision. Nothing came back from home. Your letter had said nothing of butter or of prisons. You did not choose a reason in the room.
