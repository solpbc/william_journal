Mistress Ansell came up because two candles were lit at three. You told her the porch was gone from the window. She said the sixpence is the firing, and she refused a third candle. Alse had shut the street door against the fog. You left Gib on the Chaucer. You sent the loaf back downstairs. You said you had two lines you do not trust. She told you to come down when you were hungry, and she did not climb the stair again.
