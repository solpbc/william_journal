You bought a quire of Rochelle crown paper at the trade price, fourpence halfpenny, out of the purse. Field showed you a forme with one turned letter and a dropped line: the forme unlocked, the line lifted, the page justified again, a sheet pulled. He said it before you could. "Thou changest a word and some poor devil copies three pages. Here it's my journeyman's morning." Jacqueline, with an armful of quires, said that if both of you had finished discussing paper, one of you might move it. You moved the pile. The talk got less grand and more useful. You did not rule the coffer again.
