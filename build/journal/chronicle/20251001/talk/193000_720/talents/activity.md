Gilbert came after the shop shut and took the Michaelmas money to the Bell, sixty shillings sewn in canvas, for tomorrow's carrier. Four lines go with it: the king is played Thursday, and more when you know whether anybody came. You do not go yourself. Tomorrow at about a quarter to three you will be dying at Ely House. Alse, at the door with the washing, heard "this England" and asked whether he is sorry to leave it or sorry for it. You said both. She told you to say it sorrier at the end. Mistress Ansell called her down from the stair. Not after eight. Gilbert, going, said that if it fails there is always ribbon.
