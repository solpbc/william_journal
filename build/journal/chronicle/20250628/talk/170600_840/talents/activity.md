With the door shut, John said the Mayor has written to the Lord Treasurer and will blame the playhouses next. James said let him, he is the Lord Chamberlain's man and he will open his own doors, and that sentence was not taken as the vote. John wants it written that you played old comedies and kept the peace. Bryan would send to Kettleby at Greenwich before the Mayor's letter gets there. Margery said the yard is dry tinder, and that she has stood that stair nine years. Kemp had no jest. You said the only safe house in London tomorrow is a shut one, and that it is Sunday. No order has shut the house. You agreed to meet Monday and see what Monday is. The chests take a second iron. The licensed books go to Aldermanbury with John. Garnish carries the parts. John set the July roads as a question, and nobody answered. Richard was present and did not decide. You did not send the king with the licensed books.
