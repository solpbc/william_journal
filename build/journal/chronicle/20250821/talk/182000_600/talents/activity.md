Gilbert was on the mounting-block. He said you look like Father. The walking was folly, the barn was vanity, and the twenty shillings was right. You paid Hobbins the two shillings for the pack and the saddle. You gave Gilbert the gloves your mother sent. Then the three weeks, in turns: the Spaniard three days in Cornwall and gone, parishes counting pikes, the marshal's men off the gates since the first week of August, the Queen at Whitehall on Monday and into Surrey this morning, bread no better, bills up at the Rose and the Theatre for Monday, Bartholomew Fair cried for Sunday, and Gilbert behind a stall whether he likes it. Your father owes Sadler. He had told you nothing. You did not pay it. You took a shared bed for twopence, with a man from Coventry, and you did not walk the last mile.
