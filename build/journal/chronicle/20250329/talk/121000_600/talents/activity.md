You were the tenth, on a stool from the shop. Joan asked about paint, and you said some do. Your father counted the gloves three stitches to the inch too few, and said they smell like a duchess, and Anne put them on. Edmund asked how Master Burbage dies. You showed it with a spoon and told him to finish school. Hamnet kept to the hinge. Judith did your father's walk once, and was not rebuked. Richard passed the salt and said nothing. The food was pottage, salt fish, and new bread.
