At the shop Richard Field sold you a quire of Rochelle paper for fourpence halfpenny at trade. He asked after his Holinshed, the second volume, and you had not brought it. He had the Lambeth news. Nine articles. Some chosen before the world was made, and some not, and a man cannot move himself from one list to the other. He approved the clarity. You answered with measure twice and cut once, and you asked the price of crown paper. He was not deceived, and he did not name a price. You paid the fourpence halfpenny. You said you were not writing it down.
