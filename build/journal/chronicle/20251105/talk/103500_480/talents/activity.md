Paul's Walk was louder than the stalls. You heard that on Monday at Richmond the Queen showed Essex a book printed over sea, with his name at the front, and that he came out pale and has kept his chamber. Nobody had seen the book. One man said it reckons up how kings have been put by, with examples. You stood with Daniel's third book open and did not turn the leaf. You did not name your own play, and you did not say how many leaves it has.
