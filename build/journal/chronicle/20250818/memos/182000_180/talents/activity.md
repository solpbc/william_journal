In the yard, among the horses, you let the inn's dog keep the stool. You called the back a creditor. You had been put up as far as Shipston and had carried nothing. You meant to keep a sentence from the morning and remembered the left strap instead, and you did not shorten it. You said you are too old to walk to Warwickshire. You guessed a house at forty pound and at a hundred, and you took the guess back. You do not know. You marked the friar, at first light, with one basket. Then you stopped.
