At the bridge the string was six horses and the spare, with gloves, cheeses and a town's letters under the cloths, and a bell on the leader. Richard had handed the parcel up and said nothing. Hobbins said it was light this time, and no calls at Oxford, four days, eight when the way is foul. He put you on the spare as far as Shipston, ten miles, carrying nothing. You said you were not glad of it. The two shillings stays for the Bell. You are not turning aside for the old stones.
