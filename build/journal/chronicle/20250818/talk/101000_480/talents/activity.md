At Shipston you got down off the spare. You were glad of the ten miles and you said you were not. From here you walk, at two and a half miles in the hour, which suits the back. Hobbins priced the harvest: oats a penny the peck over last month, and hay not to be had. You are not buying either. The night is an inn at Chipping Norton, twenty-one miles in all. The old stones are on the right at the rise, and you are not turning aside.
