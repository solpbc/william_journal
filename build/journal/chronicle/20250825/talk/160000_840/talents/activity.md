In the tiring-house Margery Pell counted fifty-two shillings, prentices let off for the fair and their masters after them, and not one lawyer. The yard had been full by the second trumpet. You had walked on as Navarre, and the oath of three years had a new sound. Kemp had stored a month. Garnish ticked fourteen lines and broke a pen, and you left the fourteen in the margin. The half-wages had held. All five hired men answered. Heminges put them back on ten shillings, and said Raye may bank on both sides of the water.
