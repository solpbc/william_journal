Cuthbert, from Holywell, said Allen rode in from Essex and named four-and-twenty pound a year where the company pays fourteen, for a short extension, with a clause about the house after five years that he would not explain. Two years less a month remain. James says he will pull the house down. Cuthbert wants the timber asked first, and the sharers told before James tells it his own way on Monday. You asked what a new play is worth to a landlord. Nothing, he said, and to his father a year. You said you will tell James you have a king coming. He said to tell him it is long. Nothing was agreed.
