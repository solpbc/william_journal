The sheet in your hand is dated the seventeenth of February 1594. It holds the groats, the ballad at the wash, the whistle, the odd penny, the cough gone, and the horse. Under those, in a different ink, is a reckoning at the whittawer's, fourteen shillings and sixpence, which need not trouble you. You had already put that sentence away in the yard. You did not answer it, and you did not add the sum to any account of your own.
