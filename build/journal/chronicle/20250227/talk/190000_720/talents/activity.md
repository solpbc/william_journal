Back from the Bell you found a joiner's man in the chamber, sent by Mistress Ansell, mending the stool that had rocked since Christmas. Under the short leg were three leaves of the comedy, cut in January. You said they were not valuable where they were. Mistress Ansell said they were the only words in the room that had ever held anything up. She paid the man threepence from her own purse. The stool stands level on Anne's cushion. You kept the wedge. You told them the cough was gone, and you did not read them the rest of the letter.
