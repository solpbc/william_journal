At the window, at dusk, you said you had heard the bells, and that this was all you knew and all you would say you knew. You had five, and no name. You had butter at threepence, a pillory, and a court that called it war. You remembered a gentleman at Clerkenwell a fortnight since, and a line you wrote on a Saturday and thought was only a line. You stopped at enough. You did not describe a thing you had not seen.
