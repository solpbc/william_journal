From London, the xxvj of October 1595.

Anne.

I sat near Paul's with Master Daniel, and his printed queen had gone cold marble beside a boy in a garden.
    Struck. A story for strangers. Not for this house.

I hear by your letter that the brook at Shottery has been undammed. Thank Hamnet from me. Bid him not begin upon the Avon.

Christmas will likely keep me at court, and not at home. My lord's gentleman says we shall be sent for. I shall know more when my lord says so. I do not promise a surprise. I do not promise a ride at Twelfth Night that I cannot make.

Commend me to my father and my mother, and to your mother, and to the girls. The boy has the brook. That is his portion.

Will.
