Tuesday. 1594.

Field. A forme on the press. No book
in the hand. He would have the day it
comes home. I have not got the day.
I will ask in Lent. I have not written
it in the almanac, and I will not.

The owl. One stop. It held. No word.

The packet is still on the desk.
Thursday. The groats are not yet sewn.
