John called from the mercer's in Cheapside. The Princess's gown is watchet taffeta and copper lace that will pass for gold at ten paces. The mercer named fifty shillings and will take half down. The stock has eleven shillings in it till Saturday. You asked whether France could be poorer. John said not when the King of Navarre goes in his own old velvet. You took the fifty on credit against the Queen's twenty pounds, whenever that comes. The half was not paid. Nothing came from your purse, and nothing came out of the stock today. Nick was beside John, being measured. The sleeves must be long, because he has grown. You said God keep you both.
