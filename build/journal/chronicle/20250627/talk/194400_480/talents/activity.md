You found the frame in the lane before the Mayor's gate, a hundred yards from your bed. It was empty, and nobody was near. Mistress Ansell was at her window. She looked a long minute and said that is not how you ask for bread. She shut the shutter and barred it. The bar was on the window, not on the street door. Alse and Verbeke stayed with the house and out of the street. Sir John did not speak. You were not in the crowd.
