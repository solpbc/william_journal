From the boat the Rose went by with its flagstaff bare. The new timber is green oak. James said it will twist, and that it is bigger than his. It is wider than the Rose and wider than the Theatre. Cuthbert said the Lord Mayor wrote to the Lord Treasurer against this house last November, and there it stands, on ground the City cannot touch. Davy said it is Langley's, a goldsmith building a playhouse, who will gild the groundlings. You know a goldsmith, and letters written against him. You did not land.
