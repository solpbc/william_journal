The share was seventeen shillings, received. You wrote no total. Last Saturday's fourteen was not added. The five pound agreed on Thursday was not paid out of this share. John set next week: Monday and Wednesday Two Gentlemen, and you play; Tuesday rehearsal at nine and Love's Labour's Lost, and you play; Friday Love's Labour's Lost, and you play; Saturday Errors, and you play. Thursday was not set. James will not rest until he has seen the goldsmith's timber. You are going on the river. You will not land.
