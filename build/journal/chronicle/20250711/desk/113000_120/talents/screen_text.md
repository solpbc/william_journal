## 00:00 · The glass

Master Florio? William Shakespeare, from Bishopsgate. I'd ask you of a town.
Verona? I'll tell you Verona. But you'll put a sea-coast on it, I know you poets.
A river bent round the city like an arm. An old Roman playing-place of stone, bigger than any in London.
The lords of the place are buried not in a church but beside one, in the open street, in tombs like stone tents.
