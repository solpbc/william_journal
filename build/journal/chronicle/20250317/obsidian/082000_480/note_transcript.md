the xvij of March 1594. Before I go out.

The sword, if he will give it. For the boy. Not this week into the boy's hand.
A riddle-book, for a girl who reads.
Ribands, three colours. A pair of perfumed gloves.
A bay, from Wednesday week. xij d the day, and his fodder. Nothing in hand.
The letter stays. It has no day.
