You wrapped the soap and the blue thread with Mistress Ansell and Alse. You could not name the third thing. Mistress Ansell knows what the kind with two ends is, and she said your wife asked you. She would not say the name. Gilbert is to carry the parcel to the Bell for Thursday, at sixpence, with twopence if the question goes with it. Alse tied the cloth and could not letter it. The parcel is less handsome than you meant, and more likely to arrive.
