You stood in the porch of St Helen's after morning prayer with Mistress Ansell, Master Thwaite, and Gillis Verbeke. The sermon had run to two turnings of the glass and to fourteenthly. Sir John Spencer left from three rows ahead and did not speak. The penny loaf is a finger short. The sixpence for firing and washing runs till Lady Day. You were well. Saturday's two sneezes were not this cold.
