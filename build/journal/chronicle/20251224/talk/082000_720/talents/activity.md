On the first of the flood, Gannet's tilt-boat took the company, six oars and himself at the stern. The paper went in first and driest, under the hoops, and the rolls went in oiled cloth. Garnish stayed on the chest of three books and sixty rolls. Gannet cast off with three pieces of news: the Queen at Windsor, ice at Kew, and a peace with Spain. You told him it was three again, and you did not correct him. You had it from yesterday that the court went up to Richmond, which is not Windsor. You had no peace to put in the boat. The frost on the gunwale was not news from Kew. You left his version on the water. The scarlet coats stayed folded on the basket. Nick was in the boat and did not sing.
