Ralph Raye had paid Henslowe twelve pence on his own debt and seen yesterday's play, Long Meg, set against the thirtieth. By that book today is the thirty-first of April. The sums were right to the farthing. You said a man may lose a day, and nobody loses a shilling by mistake. Kemp wanted a wage, then offered a thirty-second of May for sixpence, and was not paid it. Pope would have played twice. Heminges kept any sum of that house off the table. Your part was not named.
