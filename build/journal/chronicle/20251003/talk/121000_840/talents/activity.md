Fish day at the Jackdaw. Ling for the company, buttered eggs for you, and the ordinary sixpence. Richard, still in the king, gave the slow tapster the amazed couplet, and the tapster bent the knee and did not answer. Agnes said kings pay the same as carters, and sooner. Ralph Raye said the Rose's new play had a fair yard and a restless one, with half of them asking the way to Shoreditch. He had no sum, and you did not ask for one. Kemp said you eat like a nun and fill a house like a bear-baiting. You said, courteously, that you had rather it were like a wedding. The jaw is no better for yesterday's fame.
