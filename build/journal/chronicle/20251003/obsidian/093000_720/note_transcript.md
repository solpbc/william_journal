A father in a hurry. He does not ask her. He tells the mother the day, and the day is Thursday, and Thursday is soon enough that the house must run.
She has already heard the cousin is dead, and by whose hand. I had written her a speech that explains to the yard a feeling the yard has already seen her have. That speech is the one I cut. Eleven lines, gone. One turn, instead, and fewer words defending it.
The margin still says to trust the boy. I am not copying the words fair. I am obeying them.
No window at dawn. No cup in her hand. No tomb, and no families fetched to stand in one. The night she fears is not this page. This page is a man naming a day, and a girl given less room to apologise for knowing her own mind.
