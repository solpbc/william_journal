Alse brought the loaf in two fingers short and said she had paid a penny. You named Egeon at two and the Bell at six. The two pounds were still in the book. You said you would take them at five, and not before. Mistress Ansell told you to hear the answer before you gave the money in the street. You said that if the answer is yes he writes the line, and if it is no the two pounds come home. Dinner is at noon if you are in. You did not pay the brief at the table.
