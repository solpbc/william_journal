After the close, John Heminges left the boxes shut until Saturday. You said you had a supper at six and did not want a sum first. Kemp said the jig was eight minutes and that the tally was not his interest today. Heminges said the tally stays shut. You told John you would speak to him in the morning, and you did not say the day out loud. He told you not to bring the boxes to the Bell.
