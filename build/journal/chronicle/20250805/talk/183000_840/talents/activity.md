Quiney came to the house because you had not gone to the shop, and he brought Sturley. You said the maltsters should be made to sell. Sturley asked what you had paid, and you had paid none. Anne gave the town's side and did not rescue the sentence. Quiney named two-and-twenty shillings the quarter, and thirty by Christmas, and your father enjoyed him. Two gentlemen of the county are to certify both fires in one brief. Their names were not spoken. You said you would give what you can give, and that your lord's ear is kept for plays.
