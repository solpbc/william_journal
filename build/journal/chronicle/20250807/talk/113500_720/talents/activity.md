At the Sadlers' bakehouse, with the penny maslin coming off the peel, Quiney asked two pounds for his own malt-house to Michaelmas, not for the town. You said yes before Anne got a word in. Judith Sadler stopped the hand and made the word be loan: a pound by Martinmas or soon after, the rest by Lady Day, no use. Hamnet Sadler called it the first honest bargain in his shop this year, his own included. Judith promised the three children mutton and a pie on Sunday. In the street Anne said that is two pounds you know where it is. The twenty shillings for the timber stayed a different sum.
