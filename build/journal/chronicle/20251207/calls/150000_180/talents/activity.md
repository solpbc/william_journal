Kettleby called from Somerset House on the Sabbath and asked pardon for himself, not for his lord. Sir Edward Hoby has written this day to bid the company to supper on Tuesday at Canon Row, after supper, with the king play. It is not at court. It is a gentleman's parlour, and the gentleman is my lord's daughter's husband. Sir Edward desires the play whole. You repeated the word. You are to come by water at five. Twelve sit at table, in a small room. You asked whether my lord knows whose guest it is. Kettleby said my lord knows whose house it is.
