Before church Mistress Ansell gave you the verdict she had prepared in the night. The girl should have minded her mother, and the mother the girl. The friar was a meddler, the nurse wanted whipping, and the old men were fools. She had something in her eye at the end, and that was all she would say of it. Verbeke asked whether the two houses were friends after. She said for the length of a speech. Alse asked if they were boys, even the one that dies. You told her he is fifteen, lodges in Aldermanbury, and ate two pies after. She will not go again. The loaf was not put to the notch.
