George Bryan came to the foot of the stair, sent by Heminges, and Mistress Ansell let him as far as the third step. You did not talk about what the guest wants to see, or why. You talked about bodies, paper, candles and time. Nine players and two boys. A floor the size of a counting-house. A screen for a tiring-house, and a stool for Garnish behind it. Candles off the players' feet. The tide serves at five and will not serve at midnight. The three leaves are in Garnish's chest and have not been spoken since June. K. Richard in the letter is your king and nobody else's. You did not say more than that. Bryan, going, told you to bow to the chair by the fire, whoever is in it.
