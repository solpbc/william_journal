Master Shakespeare? Kettleby. From Somerset House, and on the Sabbath, for which I ask your pardon and not my lord's.

My lord's son-in-law, Sir Edward Hoby, keeps a supper on Tuesday at his house in Canon Row, for a guest he is particular to please. He has written this day to bid you.
