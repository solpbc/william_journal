Over Sunday mutton at Holywell, the table had Derby's wedding at Greenwich, the Queen there, the match put off a week while Tilney's office sewed. Bryan gave a great-house night as masque, banquet, and the hall cleared at midnight. Richard asked who plays. Cuthbert said Derby has his own men. You said a wedding wants a play as a feast wants a fool, something light to get the company to bed. James said a wedding wants paying for. Kemp called a wedding an excellent occasion for paying players who have already been paid. Bryan called that an unusual definition of excellent, and said it was the match Southampton was said to have paid dearly to refuse. Nothing was written down.
