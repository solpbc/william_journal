You tried the Capulet wedding morning with Tom, Kemp, and Nick. Tom read the father plain and flat, down to the spices, and the plainness was the danger. Kemp found a pause nobody had written, and Nick laughed without being asked. You wrote the pause in. Kemp said a pause lets a man put a whole life in it. You said you saw it, and you did not promise more. You kept the jaw tender, and you named no one for the afternoon king.
