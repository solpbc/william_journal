## 00:00 · The glass

Will? Dick Burbage. From Holywell, and I heard you were in.
Dick. I am in. The last mile was this morning, with the pack on my own shoulder, for the look of it.
The marshal's men are off the gates. The Rose's men are back, and they open Monday, and so do we, the same afternoon, and let the town choose.
Monday. I heard the bills at the Bell, from Gilbert, off a post in Fleet Street.
