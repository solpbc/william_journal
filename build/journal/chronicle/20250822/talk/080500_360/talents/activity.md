Mistress Ansell handed you a basket of washing before she asked anything about the journey. Then the letters, on the shelf under Gib. Then dinner, at noon. Then the quantity of road on your shoes. You had carried your own pack the mile and a half, for the look of it, after nine hours at the Bell, the soundest since Stratford. You would not let Alse take the pack, and you would not shorten the strap on the stair. The welcome was the house's work.
