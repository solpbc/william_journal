At dinner she took the journey as a plot: a fire, a walk, a barn, a back. She asked if the man in it is the wiser. You said he has seen a house he means to buy. She asked with what. You said that is the fourth act. You did not name a price. The loaf is still a full thumb short of the notch. Verbeke said the looms are quiet and nobody has shouted at him since Lammas. You did not ask him to say more than that.
