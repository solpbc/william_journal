On the step you put thirty shillings in her lap and said it was over the quarter's. She said it was, counted it as from anyone, and tied it in her apron. You told her the locked sum to the shilling, twenty pound and eight, and what a share may bring between Bartholomew and Christmas if the Mayor lets you alone. You named no weekly rate. She asked what it was for. You said a house, here. She told you not to promise one. You said not with twenty pound, and that you had not looked. Your mother set a pan down and did not speak.
