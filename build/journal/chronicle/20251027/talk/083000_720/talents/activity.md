A boy in Southampton's livery left a folio at the street door and did not come up the stair. The slip in Florio's hand says lent, not given, and who lends, loses, and prove the proverb false. Mistress Ansell carried the book up to feel the weight. It goes on the board and not in the coffer. She will fill the horn at twopence when the Monday copper cools. You opened it for the Knight's Tale and gave yourself until the bell for nine. The loaf is still a penny and a thumb short. You did not start a page of your own.
