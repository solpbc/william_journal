## 00:00 · The glass

Will? Richard Field. From the Blackfriars.
Richard. I am at the lodging. Go on.
Harrison means to put the Venus out again, and small this time. Octavo. Cheaper paper, more copies, a book for a pocket. And not a penny in it for thee, as thou knowest. The copy's his.
I am glad to be thought portable. A book that fits a pocket will outlive a book that needs a table, and the penny was never the point of that one.
