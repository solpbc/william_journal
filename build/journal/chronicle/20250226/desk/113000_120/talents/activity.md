Richard Field called from the Blackfriars. Harrison will print Venus again, small, in octavo, and not a penny in it for you. You said you were glad to be thought portable. He asked for a third poem and said the press stands idle on Fridays. He does not hold with the comedy, and he asked to borrow the book when Garnish can spare it. He will lay by two quires of Rochelle crown at fourpence halfpenny. You did not pay. He asked after your father. You said the last word was mending, and that tomorrow's carrier is the next news. You ended on God keep thee, and he answered and thee.
