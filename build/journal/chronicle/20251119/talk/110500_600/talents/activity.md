At the end Richard said the lover is all haste, and that he is seven-and-twenty. You told him to play it at a run. Nick said he had marked the roll and had nothing to ask. Kemp's count is nine-and-twenty lines, a fan, and half a song he is not let finish. You asked him to keep Saturday supper free, for something that is not in this play. You stayed on you. Nothing was copied. This afternoon's Shrew is not you.
