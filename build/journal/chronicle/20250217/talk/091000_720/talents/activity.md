At the Cross Keys you rehearsed the comedy with the column beside Costard still open. Garnish read no total. Kemp wanted another pass at remuneration, and you told him Thursday may add and this morning may not. Burbage was told not to licence him. The watchet gown is still fifty shillings unpaid. Phillips kept Sam's song for tomorrow, and you left the ladies' refusal where you marked it on Sunday.
