Kemp read the fair roll and said he can no longer make up what is set down. You told him to make something new at Easter, and that you would then have a long play. He threatened three minutes with a turnip if the cut stands. Burbage spoke a king's speech to the empty galleries, heard them give it back, and said the house likes a king. He asked for an English king whose crown burns his forehead, to wash Tamburlaine out of London's mouth, and not the hunchback again. Pope said the cap is the cue, and that nobody has won. The visible cue is Kemp's cap off on the ninth line. Garnish will not enter the turnip.
