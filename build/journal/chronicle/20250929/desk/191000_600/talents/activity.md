After evening prayer you ruled the coffer at twenty-three pound ten. The sixty shillings for Stratford are inside that sum, sewn in canvas apart, for Thursday's carrier. You entered the shares since Bartholomew as 26, 31, 33, 29 and 34 shillings, and you did not add them. The walk, the barn, the thirty shillings to your wife and the cloves are lines already in the book. Quiney's forty stays expected. You wrote no remainder.
