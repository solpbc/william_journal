You paced the chamber with oil of cloves on the gum. Your head went to the constable at Grendon, to the cloth-workers' old arguments, to Golding's lovers speaking through a wall, and then to one figure who would play the lover and the lady and the lion, solemn, with an ass's head. You laughed into the shawl until the jaw stopped you. You marked it to sleep. You did not make a page of it.
