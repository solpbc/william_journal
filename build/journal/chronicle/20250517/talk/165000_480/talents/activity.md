Heminges named your share as forty shillings, against eight-and-twenty last Saturday, and called it the spring's best. You said the chest was not counted. James said nobody was to spend the sheet on the lease, and he did not price a beam. You said nothing was agreed on Monday and that you were not agreeing it now. Heminges said the sheet is the share, not the lease. Nothing of Allen was written on it. Kemp took the same forty and no extra for the dancing.
