Florio spoke from my lord's house in Holborn, and the house was in black. Sir Thomas Heneage died this day at the Savoy. He was Vice-Chamberlain and Treasurer of the Chamber, and husband these eighteen months to my lord's mother. My lord is with her. There is a remedy for everything but death. You sent your duty and asked nothing. Then you asked who signs. A widow must first cast up the dead man's accounts. After that, who knows. He has not forgotten the Chaucer, and he will send it when the house is quieter. You did not pretend to have lost a friend. Nor did he.
