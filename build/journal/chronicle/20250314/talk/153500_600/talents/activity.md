At the Bishopsgate post Kemp set ten days of Lent and a coat of paint against your consciences, and you told him a conscience needs no elm boards. Raye, back from the Bankside, counted three hundred and twenty-five elm boards at twenty-four shillings, with lime-tubs beside them, and would not bring a receipt with the count. No second bill goes up tomorrow. Phillips was pleased, then admitted your own house spends in a pause as well. Both companies are idle. Tomorrow the Queen pays. You did not ask the day's taking, and you were not told it.
