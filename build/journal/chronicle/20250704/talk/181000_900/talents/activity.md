You came out again after you had been home, and found the proclamation nailed at Bishopsgate. The ink still smells. Gillis read it twice, slower than you. It says nothing of strangers. You named apprentices, vagrants, and any company gathered. He called that a forgetting, and the best thing a law can do. Two citizens argued whether a wedding counts, and neither had read past the heading. You did not tell them about a playhouse. Sir Thomas Wilford is named provost marshal. The sheet was given at Greenwich this fourth of July. Mistress Ansell came as far as the lane's mouth and would not have a knot. A mounted man looked, and you went in under the arch one at a time. If asked, Gillis is from Norwich.
