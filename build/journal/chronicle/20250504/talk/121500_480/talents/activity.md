Field said grace long and carved short, and you ate a hen in a French pot while the apprentices stayed at their catechism below. He told you the Lives out of Plutarch are coming off in sheets, and he asked you to come see them hang. He asked after the chronicle and let it go. You said last night's share was four-and-thirty, and you did not bring a purse. Edmund's letter sits until the carrier of the fifteenth. The comedy is to be back before Tuesday. No price was spoken.
