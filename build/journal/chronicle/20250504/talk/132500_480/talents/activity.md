Jacqueline said you make the Princess's people speak French as an Englishman dreams it, and she mended two words with her own pen, beau to bien and sire to madame. Field said you are wasted on players, as your friend, and then, without a pause, to leave the book and he would read it with the door shut. He told you the court went to Greenwich yesterday, which you had already heard. By the cheese the home vowels were back in your mouth. The book stays until before Tuesday. No third word was mended.
