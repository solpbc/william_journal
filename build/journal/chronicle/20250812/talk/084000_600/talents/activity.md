On the second bay you had the foot of a post with Richard. He lifted from the legs. You lifted from the back, and something in the small of it let go like a snapped lute-string. You did not fall. You stood with your hands on the post while the frame was braced, because it could not be put down again. Your father, from a ladder, asked if you were praying or lifting, and then he saw your face. Anne had been at the edge with a jug since eight. She said nothing kind. You started for home between Richard and Sadler, and you did not stay for the ale.
