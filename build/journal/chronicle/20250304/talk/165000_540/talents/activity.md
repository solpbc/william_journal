After the third playing Garnish gave the tally as thirty-five lines in three days, and one whole song. You told him to say it and not to copy the lines into the book today. The company drank my lord's health and did not add his age. Heminges had the trestles stacked, and said they were the inn's. You heard a harder, hungrier edge in the lads' voices than in a cheap-bread year, and you did not answer them.
