You paid the other baker forty-one shillings and had the page ruled. Seventeen shillings were left in the belt, which would not reach the shoemaker's forty-four shillings and sixpence. You unpicked the canvas, took forty-two shillings, and left eighteen in the cloth. The fourteen shillings and sixpence that remained paid the whittawer for skins. Each man was asked to say nothing at Henley Street. You did not carry a name home.
