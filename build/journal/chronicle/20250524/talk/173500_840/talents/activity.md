At the Jackdaw, dinner was sixpence. You told Burbage they have a god in a lion's skin and that he shall have a looking-glass. You said the king has given up the crown, sends for a glass, looks, and breaks it. He asked for it, and you said when you have written how he gets there. Heminges said a glass is four shillings and that he will break one every time. He named no Rose sum. Garnish gave Kemp's interest as six days, nineteen lines, and a noise like a goose. Kemp was not there. You said he has not seen the book. You did not buy the glass.
