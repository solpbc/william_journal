At the Old Swan you paid Davy Gannet a penny. He said Raleigh had gone for gold, the Spaniard was in Kent, and it would rain. He had the going as forty ships and the Queen's blessing, and he had seen salt beef loaded below the Bridge. A sailor said the men of Guiana keep their heads in their chests. You said you had met such men in the City. You did not count the ships. It was not raining.
