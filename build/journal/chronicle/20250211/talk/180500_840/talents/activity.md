At the Split Hazel supper was sixpence. A scarred gunner spread a greased chart of the Spanish Main and swore that every kitchen pot in Guiana was beaten gold. He had Davy's forty ships and the Queen's blessing. Kemp asked him, with great respect, why he was in Bishopsgate. Bryan said that anyone who truly knew the road to riches would go quietly. You said the loaf at home was a finger short, and that you had not counted the ships. Alice let the talk stay while it was still talk.
