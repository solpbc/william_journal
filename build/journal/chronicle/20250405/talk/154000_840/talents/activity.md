You told her the comedy is off the stage, that the Queen's twenty pounds was the company's and not your wage, and that the Lord Mayor has never spoken to you. You gave her the canvas sewn, eighteen shillings and not sixty, and you named the three shops inside the five pounds. She weighed it unopened and named malt, corn, and shoes, one pair fewer. She said it is the one leather. You did not name a house.
