My lord had left the jig. Nick came in on the note, not under it, because he was happy. The voice dropped out and came back a fifth lower. He stopped, considered, and bowed. The hall laughed, kindly, and by Bryan's account so did the chair. Arthur took the next verse as if it had been set. Behind the traverse Nick said it was gone, and you told him it had come, and that Sly shall teach him to fence. There was no second try, and no song left for the boat. Heminges entered nothing.
