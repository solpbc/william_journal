Back at the inn, nobody sang. You left the fifth unnamed on paper and left Sly for a later morning. Bryan would not tell the chair's laugh a second time. Heminges still had no sum, and he still would not write the three nights into the book by starlight. Kemp's button story stayed held for the inn down the river. The two cloaks stayed for morning. You took the middle of the bed you share with Heminges and Bryan.
