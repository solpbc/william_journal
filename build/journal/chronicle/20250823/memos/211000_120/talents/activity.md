You started from Anne's order, that when you write you should say something. You have a list, and a list is not a letter. The something is the friar saying Susanna's verdict and not being heeded, and it is not sealed. You did not put a sum on the house. You did not shorten the strap. Tomorrow is the fair, and Monday is the opening, and neither is a part of today's page. You stopped before it became the letter.
