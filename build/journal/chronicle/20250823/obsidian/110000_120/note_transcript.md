Fryer, at dawn, one basket. The herb that heals and the herb that kills, and he knows which.

He says what S. said. She is too quick to say yes. Saying it does not mend it.

An old man. Little heeded. Wisely and slow, they stumble that run fast.

The friends hunt him. The Nurse is long about the word. Married by afternoon.

Not a parting. Not a death. The moon stays off this page. The ladder stays off it.
