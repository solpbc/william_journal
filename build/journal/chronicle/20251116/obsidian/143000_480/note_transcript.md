Anne.

The wash stands. Fourpence the week, through the winter, out of the Michaelmas money, as thou hast paid it. I had no useful answer from a hundred miles off, and I should have given thee a worse one.

The Christmas money, when it is sent, is to be laid as thou wilt see it used. So much for the house. So much for the shop's debts. So much thou mayst spend, and not write first.

Does the children's string-and-spool engine still work, or hast thou forbidden it.

Will.

the xvj of November 1595.
