The column is what you have already promised. Twenty shillings for Mistress Ansell's Christmas quarter, not paid today. Sixty shillings for home, not sent. The hose my lord demanded, with no price. The journey up the river, with no fare. A brief for Stratford, with no sum. Two pounds lent to Richard Quiney on the seventh of August are not back. A house by the chapel is an address and no price. There is no total at the foot.
