16 Novemb. 1595, before candles.

Spoken for already, and not the sum in the coffer.

Item, to Mistress Ansell, the Christmas quarter, xx s, not this day.
Item, to Stratford, the Christmas quarter, lx s, not sent.
Item, hose my lord demanded, spoken for, no price.
Item, the journey up the river, no fare.
Item, a brief for Stratford, which Richard Quiney will ask, no sum.

Out since August, and not yet come back.
Of Richard Quiney, lent the vijth of August, ij li.

A house by the chapel. An address. No price.
