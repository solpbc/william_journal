You began a letter to Anne and did not send it. You told her Thursday's packet had gone with Hobbins, groats and all. You told the new comedy at length and did not write the end. You told her of fifteen barges and the Prince of Purpoole, and you saw that she would need the thing explained. You did not answer her question. You named no day for coming. You dated the leaf the 9th of February 1594 and left it on the desk.
