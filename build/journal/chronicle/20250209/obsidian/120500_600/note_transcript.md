Anne.

The packet went with Hobbins on Thursday. The groats were in it.
This leaf is not going with anyone.

I have tried to tell thee the new comedy, and I have told it as I
would tell a company, which is too much of it. A king and three
lords swear to study, and shut women out. A princess comes with
three ladies. There is a clown with a letter, a page, a
schoolmaster, and a Spaniard who is no Spaniard. They are masked
and then they are known. I have not come to the end of it. The
end is the part worth having, and I am not writing the end today.

Fifteen barges came up the river on the first of the month, with
streamers, for the Prince of Purpoole. That is a Christmas lord
of Gray's Inn, made of students, and not a prince thou wouldst
owe a courtesy. I have written it as if thou hadst been on the
hill. Thou wast in Stratford. It will take a second page to make
the first page plain, and I am not writing the second.

Kiss Susanna. Kiss Hamnet and Judith. Hamnet is ten since
Candlemas. I have eaten. The loaf is a finger short of the notch.

I am leaving this on the desk.

London, the ix of February 1594.
W. S.
