After the comedy you stayed in the tiring-house. The yard had been the biggest since Easter Monday, more apprentices than sweethearts, loud before the play and in it. Kemp had fed it. Garnish stopped counting at fifteen, and the hush at the messenger had come late and broken early. Margery emptied her box without her usual line. She said the apprentices were thick as flies, and that they laughed like men who mean to laugh at something else after. Burbage shrugged it off. You did not. Kemp had danced the jig of the slippers, and the yard would not let him go. You did not mend the hush.
