After the Shrew you reckoned a thin yard, apprentices with nothing to do, which was what Margery had said in the morning. The flag had been up. You had come by the fields. Phillips and Pope had come by boat, not by the Bridge. John named no sum. You named no share. You told them fourpence at Leadenhall, two hundred yards from home, and a loaf a thumb short. The aldermen's blame, which John had heard on Thursday, was said again, and the afternoon had not given the Mayor his sentence. You went home.
