John called from Aldermanbury, and that street was quiet. Margery had told him the yard would be thin, apprentices with nothing to do. He would play rather than have the Mayor write that you feared your own audience. You agreed to an old comedy with no crowd and no crown. He said the Shrew, and you agreed. The flag went up as usual. You were to come by the fields, not by the gate. Phillips and Pope were to come by boat, not by the Bridge, and he had already sent that. He asked after your Fleming. You said a man from Norwich lodged here. He had heard on Thursday that the aldermen were already blaming stage-plays for drawing apprentices together.
