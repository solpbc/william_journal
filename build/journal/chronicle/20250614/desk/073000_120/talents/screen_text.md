## 00:00 · The glass

Will? John Heminges. From Aldermanbury, and all quiet here.
John. Quiet here. What is the afternoon?
I have sent to Holywell and to Margery Pell. The— the yard will be thin, she says, and what there is will be 'prentices with nothing to do. I say we play. If we shut, the Mayor writes that we feared our own audience.
We play. An old comedy. Nothing with a crowd in it, and nothing with a crown.
