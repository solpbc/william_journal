After the Shrew, which you were not in, two Gray's Inn men brought wine to the parlour door and told the twenty-eighth of December. The hall was thronged, the Templars' ambassador left in dudgeon, the dancing was ruined, and a company of base and common fellows played. Kemp asked for that in writing and offered to have been the sorcerer. Heminges's book says Greenwich in the afternoon and Gray's Inn that night. Kemp says the night before. Burbage remembers the cold on the river, and what a clerk will make of two houses in one night. You did not enter the hall. You promised him the fifth act at the Jackdaw on Saturday, after the reckoning. He has not heard it.
