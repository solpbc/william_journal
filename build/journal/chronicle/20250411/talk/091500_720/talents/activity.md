The first rehearsal on the big stage was the comedy for Easter Monday. The yard was three times the Cross Keys. Garnish had a new plot by the stage door. Cowley missed Dull's entrance three times because the cue was a Latin tag he could not hear from behind the arras. Nick was sound and did not shout. Arthur's hooks had split and were left. You said you would take the roll home. No bill was up. Larkham was in the yard and did not speak.
