On the empty stage, with frost still on the yard rail, Nick read the vial once to himself and then spoke the two lines. You said it was very good. He called Nurse, and the voice cracked into a man's note. Nobody laughed. Arthur, on a hamper, had nothing to add and did not offer the part. Nick finished a third lower, without the call. He asked if she would be long. You said two acts, and that she sleeps through some of it. Phillips said to send him mornings, that it can be nursed and cannot be mended. No hour was named. You told Garnish to leave the sheet. You did not say, in the room, that you would refuse to cut her.
