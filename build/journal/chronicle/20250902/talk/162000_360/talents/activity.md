After the play you gave Bryan and Heminges the call. My lord will come unlooked-for on the second of October. The Lord Treasurer cannot stir hand nor foot. Bryan said what Kettleby had not said: an old man who cannot hold a pen, two younger men waiting to hold it, and a play about a weak king and his favourites about to walk out between them. Heminges said you play it as licensed. Nobody added anything.
