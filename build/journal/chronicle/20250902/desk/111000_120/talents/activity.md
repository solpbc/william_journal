Ambrose Kettleby called from Somerset House, sent up for his lord's linen and letters. My lord is with the court in Surrey. He would know that the house opened quietly, and when the new king is to be seen. He will come unlooked-for. You gave Thursday the second of October, and Kettleby wrote it down. The Lord Treasurer cannot stir hand nor foot, nor feed himself. Every paper that wants his hand is waiting on his foot. My lord bids you keep an orderly house, and he softens it. You said the orderliest in Middlesex. He said that is the county he should be anxious for.
