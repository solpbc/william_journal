## 00:00 · The glass

Master Shakespeare? Ambrose Kettleby. From Somerset House, where I am sent up for my lord's linen and his letters.
Master Kettleby. How does my lord in Surrey?
Sound enough to ask after his players. He has heard the house is open and would know two things: that it opened quietly, and when the new king is to be seen.
It opened quietly, sir. The new king is Thursday the second of October, if you will have a day.
