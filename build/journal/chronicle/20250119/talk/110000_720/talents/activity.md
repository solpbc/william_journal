You kept the bed. Mistress Ansell put the posset in your hands and told you she had been to church for the house, because you had walked in a frost at ten at night. Gilbert came as far as the chair with Anne's letter from Thursday's carrier. The children want shoes in one month, your father's cough is mild and cheerful, and she would know about Lent. He told you to answer the last one first. You did not answer it. Sixpence and twopence were Thursday's, already paid.
