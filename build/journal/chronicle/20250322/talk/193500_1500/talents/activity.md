Morley brought you into the house in the lane off Bishopsgate, which is the house with the lit window of Twelfth Eve. His balletts are five voices, at the printer's this year. He would not name what the strings cost. A woman at the virginals played a strain exact, then the same strain faster, as if laughing at it. You knew the hand and did not ask her name. He did not make you known. A man of the parish asked for the air and did not supply a name either. Nothing passed but the music. She did not speak.
