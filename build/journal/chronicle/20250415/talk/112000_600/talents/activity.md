You bought Daniel's book for two shillings, and you called it the dearest since Golding. You had stood in it four times since March, and today you were still standing when you paid. Harrison repeated that you might read it and might not wear it out. Drayton said you would not find a laugh in four books. You talked about five minutes of home and of sonnets. You would not give him a number, and you did not envy Daniel. You sent nothing else out of London.
