At the queen's garden, Sam asked why the book tells him to look frightened when nobody has told his lady anything yet. You began from the whole play. Phillips stopped you: answer from his entrance, because Sam has read his roll and not the play. You told Sam he has seen his lady not sleep, and that this is enough. He did his three lines from the door, and it was right. Nick, waiting as the queen, looked at his shoes and said a queen knows only what is in her roll. You said the correction, from the door and not the book, and that you would write it on the tables.
