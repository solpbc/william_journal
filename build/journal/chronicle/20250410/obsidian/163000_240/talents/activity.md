You unlocked both locks and counted. With the four shillings you brought back, the coffer was £19 6s. The journey went down as four lines: horse, fodder and inns, 38s; carried home, 60s; for your father, 58s more; £7 16s out of the coffer since Lady Day. The leaf is dated the tenth of April 1595. Anne's list lay beside the book and was still unread aloud. You did not start another page. You were asleep by eight.
