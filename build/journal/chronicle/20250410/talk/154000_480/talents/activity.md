On the stair Mistress Ansell had an aired chamber, a dull parish of two banns and one cracked pane, and Heminges's note for Friday at nine. The hired bag was to leave the passage tonight. Alse had failed to move Gib off Field's Holinshed. You told the cat the book was Master Field's. You unpacked Anne's list before your own notes, and you did not read the list aloud. Verbeke had kept his room and his own name.
