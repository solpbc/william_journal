You spent the morning on a property chair of state, painted to pass for oak and gilt, with a weak joint where a king sits down too hard. James mended it with one peg, pale in the old wood, and kept it light enough for the boy who must carry it off. Sam's cleats would have worked only if the chair never left the stage. Larkham wrote that it was mended, and not to be sat on by Master Kemp. The trap was left for another day. You were going home by eleven.
