the xviij of March 1594.

Florio. From my lord's house in Holborn.
He bids me say he is glad you prosper. I say it as he said it.
Nothing more. I did not ask.
One proverb. I think it is wrong. He says it is only English.
Not copied out. Not sent to a printer.
The chair is mended. Not to be sat on by Master Kemp.
