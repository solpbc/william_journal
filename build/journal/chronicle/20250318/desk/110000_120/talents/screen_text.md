Master Shakespeare? Florio. Resolute John Florio, from my lord's house in Holborn.
Master Florio. Five weeks is a long quiet to break at eleven.
The paper you left at the lodge. My lord had it. He was at court for the Shrovetide running, and he has it by him still.
He bids me say he is glad you prosper. I say it as he said it.
