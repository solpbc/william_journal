Florio called from my lord's house in Holborn, five weeks after the lodge. My lord had the paper. He was at court for the Shrovetide running, and he has it by him still. Florio said he is glad you prosper, and that he said it as it was said. Nothing more was offered, and you did not ask. He gave you peregrinate, perambulate, and walk, and the proverb. You said you thought one proverb wrong. He said it is not wrong, it is only English, and he asked you to send it to him before a printer. You did not copy it out.
