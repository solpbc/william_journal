John Heminges counted twelve pound thirteen in the boxes, the year's best house, above the eleven pound six of Richard II on the 2nd of October. The share was forty shillings. He said eight times forty shillings is sixteen pound, and that sixteen pound does not come out of twelve pound thirteen, so he would not divide the box into the share. You agreed there would be no third figure. The hired men's ten shillings stayed a wage, and Larkham's extra copying stayed unpriced. Margery Pell said she had no line, and that she never watches a play. A church hat with a girl beside her had sat the lower gallery and gone down the stair. Kemp had added one line and no more.
