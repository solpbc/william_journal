From behind the arras you had the afternoon in your ear. Pope's Nurse had stopped the play three times. Phillips's Mercutio had been laughed with, and then not. Kemp had said what was set down, and one line more, and the fan was on the boards. The sun was gone before four. When Romeo called for light it was brought. Nick had held under every note. You went on as the Prince and spoke the last line, and nobody moved at first. In a house of two thousand not one nut was cracked through the vault.
