The children played Saturday night's orchard scene at the bedside, to an audience of one lying flat. Susanna was the girl, on a stool, and she read clear. Hamnet was the boy, at a gallop, and he stopped at the wings to ask for a ladder or a friend. Judith was Madam in three sizes, and she was also the moon, with a candle, and she would not be talked out of it. You laughed, and it hurt, and you laughed. Susanna said the girl is too quick to say yes. You answered with the line in which she says so herself, and you changed nothing. Anne, from the door, asked if this was the work. You said that was it. She said it was better than she had thought.
