At home you wrote the afternoon in two halves. The useful half was where the laugh starts, how attention comes back, and what a body can carry. The other half was one sentence, that Alleyn is easy because the part is smaller than he is and the yard cannot tell. You were ashamed of that sentence because he had been cordial, and you left it on the page. You noted the post on the left, the penny, the day, and the full moon. You did not write a receipt.
