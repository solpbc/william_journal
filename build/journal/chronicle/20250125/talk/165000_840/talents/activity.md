After Two Gents you set next week's afternoons and did not give out a part. Monday Titus, not you. Tuesday the Shrew, you, and the reading at Dick's, with Abel reading and no roll in a hand. Wednesday the old Hamlet, you. Thursday Errors, you, and a rehearsal at nine. Friday the Shrew, not you. Saturday Errors, you. No share was named. The coffer was not opened. The hired men's ten shillings was named and not paid out. Supper tomorrow is at the Burbages'. You are not sent to Greenwich.
