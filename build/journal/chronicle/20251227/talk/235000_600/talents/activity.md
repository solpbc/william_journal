Still in the hall, you said you had spoken the prologue between the degrees, and that indoors every hour is night. Pope's Nurse had done what my lord said a nurse would do, and he would not repeat the sentence. Nick had stayed grave and low, held to the dagger, under every note, and Burbage had not looked away. The end was a rustle, one cough, and the chair rising, not the Theatre's hush. A gold button set with five pearls was gone from the kirtle since the play began. Sam had searched the stage-front and under the trestles for half an hour and had not found it. Nothing had glinted. Kemp said, aside, that a little gold thing may raise a bigger search than a missing player ever did. Heminges told him to keep it until the inn, down the river, and not to tell it at the Feathers. You did not find the button. You were still in the hall.
