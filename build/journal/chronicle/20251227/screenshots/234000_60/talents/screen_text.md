## 00:00 · The search, and nothing glinting

The hall floor after the play, seen looking down. Rushes, sweet-flag, and sprigs of dried rosemary, trodden and scattered. A boy of twelve is on his knees, his back three-quarters turned, parting the rushes with one hand and holding a short candle low in the other. The light is a small warm pool. At the edge of the pool, a yeoman's scarlet hose and shoe. Beyond that, carved wainscot goes into the dark. Nothing glints. Nothing is lifted out of the rushes.
