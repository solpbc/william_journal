At the window you said what had reached you from Southwark market. About three hundred apprentices had taken the butter, asked at fivepence, and sold it at threepence, and had paid the money over. Five hundred pounds, by one telling. You said they had paid, and that they would be whipped as thieves, and you asked who was whipping the women who had asked fivepence. You did not leave the house. The sheets were still on the board.
