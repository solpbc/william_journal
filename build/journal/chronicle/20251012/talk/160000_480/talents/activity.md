Supper was peas boiled to nothing, a sop of bread in warm ale, and a baked apple. You complained of the texture of each, and Mistress Ansell said the food had already yielded more than you had. Alse began a riddle about a barber-surgeon's charge and was stopped before the answer. Master Verbeke finished it in his own tongue and would not translate. You laughed, held the jaw, and said that laughing was not the thing that hurt. Evening prayer had already been said at the table. She told you to be in bed by eight. The loaf was a penny and a full thumb short of the notch. Board was threepence, and the hall candle was the firing sixpence.
