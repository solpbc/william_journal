You paid twopence for the Monday horn and threepence to sit at the board. Mistress Ansell set the penny loaf against the notch. It wants a full thumb, and the price is still a penny. If anyone asks, Gillis is from Norwich. She asked what the morning was. You told her a king who talks, and that you are not playing him this week. Dick still has the forty leaves. You did not open a book.
