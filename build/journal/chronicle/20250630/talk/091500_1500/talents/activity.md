You sat at James Burbage's long table with the fellows, Kemp not yet in. The streets up had been swept and silent, and the gate had looked at every face. John said the almanac says no play, and that the afternoon bill was struck this morning. You began the forty leaves. At the hollow crown you read the two lines, and Dick's hand went flat on the table and stayed. You did not take the hand away. The harder page is still ahead of you. Nothing has been agreed yet except to hear the king out.
