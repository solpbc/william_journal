After Errors, in which you played Egeon, you asked for the flag and the book. John says the flag took under three hundred after two dark days, and the yard sat still for the whole tale. Margery took nine shillings from old men and country folk, and she had never had a house so quiet. Dick had held the forty leaves since noon yesterday, wrapped in a shirt, and he gave them to Garnish. Garnish said he is not your cousin, and that the leaves want ruling. He has six days. John's man carries the fair book to Clerkenwell on Monday. Seven shillings is set by for the fee and is not entered as spent. You are walking home by the same road.
