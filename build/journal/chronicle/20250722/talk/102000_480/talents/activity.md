Nick stopped at the girl's entrance and did not speak her verse. He said she knows nothing of the boy, and of the man only a name and a face across a hall, and that she is careful, not shy. You wrote it down. Kemp played the servant straight and did not ask for a song. Burbage said the boy had read too many sonnets, and you said he had. Pope liked the old man better. You did not let Kemp see that you were pleased.
