After the play, Margery brought word from the twopenny gallery. Five men are condemned at the Guildhall. The charge is not riot. It is high treason, levying war against the Queen. George Bryan gave Nick the old explanation, that a number gathered to pull down even a pillory may be called levying war. Kemp had no jest. Nobody knows the day. You told John you go into Warwickshire on Monday, and when he asked on what, you said on these. You could hold the letter and the trial, and think about neither.
