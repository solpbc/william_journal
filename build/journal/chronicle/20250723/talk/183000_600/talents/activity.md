You read Anne's letter at the table. The fire was Tuesday the fifteenth, at the north end, about twenty houses, and none of yours was touched. Mistress Ansell said twice in two years, asked what they do in that town, and when you said they malt she said they dry it too near the thatch. The loaf was still a penny, a thumb short of the notch. Verbeke said a grace longer than Sunday's, in his own tongue, and did not put the rest into English. Alse asked about tomorrow. Mistress Ansell spoke of the pease and gave no ruling, which you have not known her do. Hobbins goes at first light and will be there four days before you.
