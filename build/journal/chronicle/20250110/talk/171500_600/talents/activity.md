You came down for a roll and found Edith with the mended doublet and eight shillings still owing for a prince's sleeves. You praised the seam. She took the praise and did not let it settle the bill. Heminges named Lady Day as the latest he can promise. Kemp said a prince who cannot pay for his sleeves should keep his arms inside his coat. She did not laugh until you had written Edith viij s. owing. Lady Day. It is not your debt, and it was not paid.
