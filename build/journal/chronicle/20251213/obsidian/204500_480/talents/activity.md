You turned the private leaves once and did not count them. There were no titles, numbers, or names on them. You read first lines only. The weary one begins "Weary with toyle, I hast me to my bed,". The one against painted beauty begins "In the ould age blacke was not counted faire". October stays a night the jaw would not let you sleep, and the lilies stay a line Master Burby also has. You did not write those two out. You squared the sheets and tied them with tape from the property basket. They went to the bottom of the coffer, under the canvas and under the account books of two years. Gib was put off them. No one was named.
