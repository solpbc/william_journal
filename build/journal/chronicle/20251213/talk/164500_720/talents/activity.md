Heminges reckoned the week as thirty-seven shillings for the playing, and twelve shillings and sixpence from Sir Edward beside it, not folded in. Five pounds was the purse. Four shillings was the glass, and it was not taken off the five or off the twelve and six. You did not make a total. You did not count the coffer. Quiney's pound stays out. Last Saturday's forty was not written again. The Queen's three nights were not entered. The rent was not paid from this table. You said the two figures back. No Rose receipt was invented.
