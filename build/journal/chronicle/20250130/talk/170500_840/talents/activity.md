After Errors, with the almanac open, John counted five playing weeks to Lent on the fifth of March, twenty-eight afternoons, and he wants four of them for the comedy. Richard wants three full weeks for Berowne, for he never stops talking. You fixed Thursday the twentieth of February, a term-less day, and trusted the Inns to come. Kemp asked what jig follows, and was told a comedy needs none. That was the first small cloud. You stop on Shrove Tuesday, the fourth of March, rather than play into Lent and tempt the Mayor. Pope said the Rose will play till they're stopped. John said the Rose has no mayor sleeping next door to its poet. No share was read out. The coffer was not opened.
