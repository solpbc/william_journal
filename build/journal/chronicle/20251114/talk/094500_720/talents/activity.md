On the empty stage Phillips played two measures of a minor Italian pavan and stopped. You counted sixteen bars for old Capulet's welcome and for Tybalt knowing a Montague's voice under the mask, and then the bass viol down so the fourteen lines float. Burbage and Nick spoke at half voice, palm to palm, and stopped when the music stopped. Phillips said nobody in the yard will hear the hautboys, and everybody will hear the pilgrims. Nick stayed under the note the whole way. The parts are not out. You did not copy the fourteen lines onto the margin.
