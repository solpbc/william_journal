At the corner of Bishopsgate Street the bonfire was going and the steeples were ringing. Mistress Ansell let Alse out to it. She said that is how you light a fire, for something. Alse asked who fell, and whether the Queen was there. You said you were too far down to count a fall, and that the Queen was a stillness behind glass. You did not tell the rest. You did not say why you had gone. When the log fell in, she took you both inside.
