James told Allen's figure himself. Four-and-twenty pound a year, where the company pays fourteen, for a term of two years less a month, with a clause about the house after five years that Allen would not explain. You said you have a king coming and that it is long. You did not curse Allen, and you have not seen him. Nothing was agreed. Heminges kept the figure off any sheet of money taken. You played Navarre after. The chest was not in the room.
