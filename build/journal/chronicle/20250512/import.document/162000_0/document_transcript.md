MEMORANDUM OF LEASE HEADS - THE THEATRE GROUND,
HOLYWELL
Minutes brought from Cuthbert Burbage (10 May) and read at the Theatre, Monday
12 May 1595
Lessor: Giles Allen, gentleman, of Haseleigh in Essex. Lessee: James Burbage,
joiner and player, of Holywell, Shoreditch. Present term: twenty-one years
from 13 April 1576, expiring April 1597 (two years less a month remaining) at
the old reserved rent of 14 pounds by the year.
Heads demanded by Master Allen for any new covenant:
1. Annual Rent. The yearly rent for the parcel of ground, barns, and playhouse
yard to be raised from 14 pounds to 24 pounds per annum, payable at the four
usual quarter-days.
2. Repairs and Upkeep. Lessee to covenant to bear all charges upon the timber
frame, galleries, tiled roof, and boundary wall out of the company’s own
purse.
3. Clause Touching the House After Five Years. Master Allen demands a further
clause touching the playhouse itself after five years of any renewed term. He
has neither explained the words of this clause nor delivered a fair copy to be
read.
4. Timber Ownership. Under the original 1576 indenture, the playhouse frame
was erected at James Burbage’s own cost with covenant to take down and remove
the timber before expiry if no new term be sealed.
Minute entered at the Theatre, 12 May 1595:
Present: James Burbage, Richard Burbage, John Heminges, Thomas Pope, William
Shakespeare. No figure agreed, no term accepted, and no sum entered in the
company book. The five-year clause remains unread.
