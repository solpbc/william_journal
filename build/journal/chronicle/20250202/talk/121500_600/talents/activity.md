At dinner the penny loaf was a finger short of the notch, as on Friday. You said the twins are ten and that the letter waits for Thursday. Alse offered to run it to the Bell. You refused. Verbeke took the heel. He is from Flanders, and his loom is in Norton Folgate. The meat was threepence. Firing and washing stay sixpence a week until Lady Day. No second loaf was bought. The pump in the close had run.
