At the reckoning John refused to call a hope a coin. Pell said Wednesday's Titus had a full stair, that you were not on it, and that she brought no figure. Edith's eight shillings for the prince's sleeves were written and unpaid, and nobody paid them from the stock or from your purse. The gown's fifty shillings were unpaid, on credit against the Queen's twenty pounds, which have not come. Half down would have been twenty-five shillings, and it was not paid. You did not turn Tuesday's eleven shillings into a new stock. John would not count the coffer or name a share. Your twopence to the carrier stayed out of the company book. Kemp was not set down beside any of it. Nothing was bought.
