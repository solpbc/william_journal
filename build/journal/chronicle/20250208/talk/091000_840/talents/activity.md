At nine, and not before, you rehearsed at the Cross Keys. Garnish had Thursday's two stops in the book. Nick had walked the speech and did not speak it out. Phillips sent him to eat. You walked the old book for the Seven Deadly Sins. Kemp was civil. You did not open a tally, and you did not quote the new play. The gown's fifty shillings stayed unpaid, and this room did not settle them. Nothing was bought. The new comedy stayed off the yard.
