At the White Greyhound, Harrison told you, as of a third party, that Cuthbert Burby entered a play of King Edward the Third this morning. The scenes of a king and a countess are in your manner because they are yours, made three or four years ago for another company, for a fee. You asked if Burby had asked leave. He said, of whom. Sixpence to the Hall buys the title its place. The title carries no company, no hands, and no name. You named lilies that fester, and you made no claim.
