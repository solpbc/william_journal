You helped clear the winter house at the Cross Keys. Three apparel baskets and the property hamper went to the cart, and the inn's trestles stayed stacked. Garnish kept the books in his arms. The ostlers threw Sam his cues from the loft, having heard the comedy forty times. Heminges paid thirteen shillings and fourpence on the sill. The cart is eightpence, to be paid at the Theatre door. The host said he would look for you at Michaelmas, if my lord writes again.
