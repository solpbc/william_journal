In the hall you gave Anne Mistress Ansell's opinion in the landlady's words: four young men swear off women and keep it till dinner. You told the rest of the plot, the messenger and the year, and you did not sing the song. Anne said the ladies were right to make them wait, and a twelvemonth was short. You said you would carry that sentence back word for word. You did not write a line of the play.
