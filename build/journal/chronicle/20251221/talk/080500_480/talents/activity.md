Before church, women of the parish came to the step for the gooding. Mistress Ansell gave wheat-meal and halfpence by name. Alse carried the bag and counted aloud. Margaret's loaf was short by two fingers and a thumb, at the same penny. You offered the bag and were refused. She said the door was hers and the church box was yours. She had had eleven before you came out, and twelve by the time the neck of the bag was shut.
