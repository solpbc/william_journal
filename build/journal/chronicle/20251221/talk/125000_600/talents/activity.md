After sermon you ate Jacqueline's goose at Blackfriars. Field asked nothing about the second volume, and you told him that the not-asking was worse. Jacqueline said a man going to court should take a second shirt, and she made it two shirts, not a speech about one. You said the chronicle stays in the room. You still had Monday as the day the Queen goes, and Wednesday as the boat, if Monday holds. There was enough at the table for three.
