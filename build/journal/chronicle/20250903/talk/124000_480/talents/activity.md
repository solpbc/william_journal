At dinner you gave Mistress Ansell the friar at dawn with his basket, and the line from the road. She approved him until you said he marries them the same afternoon. She said that without their fathers' leave he is no better than the Nurse, and that it will end in a churchyard. You looked at her and said nothing. She measured the loaf as a full thumb short of the notch, at a penny. Gib was asleep on Brooke. You said the chronicle was still in the room under its cloth, and that you were not sending it back.
