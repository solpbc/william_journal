At dinner she counted three candles to the socket on Tuesday, and asked if it was the lovers again. You said Tuesday was the tooth, and the lovers were this morning, and they burned one. They parted at sunrise. She said they had more sense than you. There is no lark in Great St Helen's in October, only rooks, and the girl had lied about the nightingale. The loaf is still a penny and a full thumb short. Board was threepence. Twelve pence for the tooth is Tuesday, and unpaid. You are in the Roman at two.
