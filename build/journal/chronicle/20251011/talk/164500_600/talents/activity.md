After Titus, John named your share as thirty-eight shillings. Last week was forty-five. You did not ask for a tale to explain the difference, and you did not count the coffer. You were in Titus today, and off in the fourth, as on Monday and Wednesday. The king was Tuesday, and the next is Thursday the sixteenth. The hired men's ten stays settled. The glass stayed locked. Thomas had read 1 Hercules on the Rose bill. You did not go. You are writing the share and not a total.
