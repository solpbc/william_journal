Nick asked for a clearer lead in the orchard. He had gone in a breath early, twice, where Romeo's last word and his own first come too close. Burbage said it had gone, and the yard had wept. You backed the boy. A fraction of doubt spreads like a crack through a pane, and the orchard is all pane. The lead is changed by two words. He comes in on the second of them, and not on the breath before it. The words are not said into the room. Larkham marked the book and both rolls, with his small sign, before a rival memory of the old cue could be carried off. Burbage tried it and said it was better. Phillips called Nick to the lute and told him to keep Saturday's note. The rest of the morning was the king Shoreditch already has. The three leaves stayed in the chest.
