Between the rent and the play you went down to Old Swan in the sleet. You held the canvas and Gannet knotted it over the tilt-boat's hoops. He confirmed Wednesday's flood, six oars and himself. He also had it that she will not go to Richmond at all, that the river freezes by Friday, and that the Spaniard is coming. You told him three stories made him wrong by a third, and you did not yet know which third. Your cloak was soaked on the weather side.
