At the end of the play Margery had four shillings and tenpence from a yard of some sixty, and she would have given the pennies back to send them home. It was the Theatre's last play of the year. Heminges had word from Somerset House that the Queen has put off Richmond until tomorrow, for the weather. He said she has sense, and that the tilt-boat stands. He would not make a share out of the four and tenpence. You did not yet correct Gannet in his hearing. You had tomorrow, which is one of the three, and not the whole of any of them.
