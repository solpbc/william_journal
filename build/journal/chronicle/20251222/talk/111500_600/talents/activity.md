You paid the Christmas quarter three days early, twenty shillings counted twice, because Thursday you will be up the river. Mistress Ansell wrote the receipt once, in her husband's law-hand. The latch, mended this morning by her joiner, shut the first time. She would not take money for it. She promised to air the chamber, leave the coffer unvisited, and keep Gib off the lord's book. You did not count what remained.
