Errors was just done. You had played Egeon and then waited two hours in a cold parlour. Kemp got up on a hamper, set a stool for a chariot, and did Alleyn's line. The room went quiet. Heminges said they will be paid as slowly as we are. Pope said she is keeping two dogs and feeding neither. Kemp roared the line again, and Burbage threw a stuffed glove. Your sheets of Berowne stayed under your arm. You did not read them. The Queen's twenty pounds is still expected, and still not yours to fetch.
