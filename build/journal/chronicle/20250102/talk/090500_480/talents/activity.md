At the morning rehearsal the plot stayed on the door. You are Egeon. You speak, and then you sit two hours while the play goes on without you. Garnish would not have the book mended. Burbage heard that the two hours are the wait and not the speech, and told you to keep your own page under your arm. Kemp kept the laugh. You did not give them the old man's tale, and you did not come early.
