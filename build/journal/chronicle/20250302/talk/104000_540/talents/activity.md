In the porch Master Thwaite turned the sand-glass twice, said he would be brief, and put Ash Wednesday three days off. You paid sixpence for firing and washing till Lady Day. Sir John Spencer sat three rows ahead and did not turn. Mistress Ansell showed the heel of the loaf, a penny and a finger short, and said she would not salt the house ahead of the church. Alse asked which hunger was hers. You left Gib on the Ovid, said you would be in close on nine, and went when the second glass was out.
