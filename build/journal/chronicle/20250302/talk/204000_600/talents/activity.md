At the Bull, close on nine, Nashe was still at war with Harvey, who was not in the room. You told him he was the page, not the pedant and not the braggart. Phillips got one verse out of him. Burbage got a penny for one cup. Heminges said the company had come through its first winter without a quarrel over the purse, and he touched the table. The supper was tenpence a head. You left on the purse.
