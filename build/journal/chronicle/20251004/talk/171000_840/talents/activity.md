After Errors the boxes were opened on the baize. Pennies, half-groats, threepences, a few sixpences, and one clipped shilling Margery swears she never took. Heminges's items were a crown new gilt at three shillings, rosemary and a black pall at fourteen pence, and a looking-glass at four shillings, bought in May and not yet looked in. The galleries this week come to more than a whole house in a June week, with no June figure spoken. The hired men have their ten shillings, a wage, not a deduction from Thursday's eleven pound six. The share is forty-five shillings, the best week. James said a house that takes eleven pound of a Thursday is worth a landlord's envy, and Cuthbert steered him off the lease. Kemp said eleven pound six, and not a line of it his, and wished you would learn to empty a house. Garnish gave him no interest in the king and no principal in the jig. They fixed the king once a week, on a changing day: Tuesday next, then Thursday the sixteenth. Richard said it will be. You took the forty-five and will enter them without a jest.
