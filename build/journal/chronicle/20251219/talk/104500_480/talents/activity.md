At the printing-house door, in a thin snow, James put a carpenter's rule out from under his cloak and swore the secret on a hot gill. The Upper Frater can be had outright for six hundred pounds, come the spring, when the money is found. You said it was a great deal of money. He said it was a great deal of roof. Dick did not speak. You looked at the lancets and did not talk of the neighbours. You promised the sum would not reach Field or a tiring-house. Nothing was bought, and no timber was to be pulled.
