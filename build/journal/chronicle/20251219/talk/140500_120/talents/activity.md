You went to the Theatre for the two o'clock Shrew, though the bill did not mark you to play. At the tiring-house door the gatherer brought the day's other news: the Queen has come up suddenly to Whitehall, and nobody can say why. Heminges said a court does not move in December for nothing. Dick caught your eye, and nobody spoke of a hall. You said you would hear the first act from the door and be home before the candle.
