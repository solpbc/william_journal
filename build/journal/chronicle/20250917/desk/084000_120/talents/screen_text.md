Will? Richard Field. From my own shop, with a book open that I did not print.
Go on. I am not yet in a gown.
It came yesterday in a parcel from Cambridge. A learned thing in quarto. A letter in it from England to her three daughters, the two universities and the Inns of Court, praising their poets by the yard. I was reading it
And on one leaf, in the margin, small, there's a name I know. I'll not tell thee what he calls thee. Come and see it with thine own eyes. Harrison has copies at the Greyhound by this. Tomorrow, ten, at Paul's.
