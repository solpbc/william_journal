Field called from his own shop with a book he did not print. It came from Cambridge yesterday. He will not tell you the word in the margin. Harrison has copies at the Greyhound. Tomorrow at ten, at Paul's. Your jaw is the size of an egg. Woburn burned on Saturday, and a tract is already in the press. You said you would write to Quiney tonight. He asked for his Holinshed, and you ended on God keep thee.
