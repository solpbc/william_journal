You stood by the left-hand post. Alleyn filled the house. Henslowe stayed at the door with his lips moving, and neither of them spoke to you. It is a lost play, and the title is what you have: an English king of that name, a yard that knew before the first trumpet how it comes out, and cheering for a man who wins. They are the same faces that go quiet for Burbage. They pitied the king who loses and shouted for the king who wins, and they paid a penny either way. You did not bring a plot away with you.
