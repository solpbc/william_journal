You ran Richard the Third from the parts for next week. Cowley entered twice on the wrong lord, because two cues both ended alike, and you changed three cue-words to Lord Hastings, my liege, and madam. Larkham copied both rolls and said nothing. Nick had his words and not the breath before the door. Burbage at half voice was still too big for the parlour. Phillips wanted Cowley to take Egeon. You refused, and you played the tale at two on breath and consonants.
