Heminges shut the bag and named twelve shillings the share. He said last Saturday's bag had not been shut, and that no share had been named then. He said the twenty-fourth of May had been thirty-six, and he did not subtract one from the other. You did not add the twelve to a coffer. Nothing was sent home. He had no Rose figure beside it. The house had played The Two Gentlemen at two, and you were not in it. The twelve neither grew nor shrank for that. The Monday holiday stayed the comedy already set.
