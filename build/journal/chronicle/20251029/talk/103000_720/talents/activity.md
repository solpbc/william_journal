At Old Swan you stood with half the parish for a mayor who does not live among you. Gannet, on his oars, said the new man would pull down the playhouses by Christmas, that the old one was to be an earl, and that the Spaniard was in the Downs. A passenger asked to follow the barge and was told to follow in his own boat or swim. That is the line you kept. Kemp took his hat off toward Bishopsgate and bade the absent mayor sweeten. Mistress Ansell said Sir John went out as he came in, liking nobody, and that he kept his own door. You did not take his going for a victory. A woman's dark head was turned away, laughing, and you did not look long enough to know who stood beside her. You are Egeon at two.
