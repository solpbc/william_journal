The loaf was a penny, and two fingers short of the notch. Mistress Ansell measured it and did not raise the price. You did not open the coffer. You did not untie what is at the bottom. Gib had been on Field's chronicle and she had moved him. Your letters were still where you left them. Supper is at six. The rent is already paid. You called the shortfall a measure, not a grievance.
