You packed two shirts, the hose, the tables, and a quire, and you rolled the lath sword in the grey cloak. The children's things were four separate knots. Mistress Ansell asked whether your wife knows the plot. You said not yet, and that you would tell her the opinion. Alse has never been ten miles. Warwickshire is a hundred. Brooke stays on purpose. Holinshed stays under a cloth, against Gib. The sixty shillings stay in the canvas until tomorrow. The bay is not taken. The long page stays face down.
