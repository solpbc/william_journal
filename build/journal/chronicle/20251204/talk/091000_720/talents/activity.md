At the copying pass you mended the one vault cue two men had heard differently, and you kept the fire on the word light. You let go a better word for the Friar and a turn in the Nurse's lament. Sam, holding the ink, said you had obeyed the rule mostly applied to boys. Heminges did not praise you. Larkham's boy took four lines to the Bell with the letter of the 16th and the six lines of the 24th. The four lines say the lovers are played on Saturday. No sum was set for the copying.
