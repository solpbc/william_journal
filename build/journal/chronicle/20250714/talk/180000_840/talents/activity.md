She had heard you walking. You told her the two houses, the old quarrel, the servants, and that the Prince promises death for the next. She said he was the only sensible man in it, and not the chief part. You said you are to play him. She said speak up. The loaf wanted a full thumb. The penny stayed a penny. Verbeke had you tie the knot four times, and gave you thread, shuttle, knot. Alse tied it once and was told not to look pleased.
