John called from Aldermanbury after going through the book with Garnish. You have written four ladies and a dairymaid, and he has three boys, and one of the three is the page. You offered to cut the dairymaid. Kemp has already seen her, so she stays. Two boys out of Pope's parish at three shillings the week each stand as Maria and Katharine, and are not paid tonight. Sincler, in black, is the messenger, since he needs no padding to look like bad news. John takes the schoolmaster. His wife says Nick is growing through his sleeves. He said the Princess wants a gown, and he is not buying it tonight.
