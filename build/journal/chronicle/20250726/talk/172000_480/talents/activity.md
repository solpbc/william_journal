Heminges paid the hired men the week owing and the first of the half-wages in hand, five shillings. The ledger line is dark till Bartholomew, and no Saturday share is written beside it. Garnish locked the books and the parts in the chest with the second iron, and the swan came down. You said you walk on Monday and save eighteen shillings of horse-hire. Burbage offered to keep the Verona sheets. You said they go where you go. He told you to mind the rain. You said there is no rain this summer.
