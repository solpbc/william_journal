Before the jig, Margery brought the court post. On Wednesday, at six in the morning, four galleys put men ashore in Mount's Bay. Mousehole is burned, and Newlyn, and Penzance, and the church at Paul on the hill. Kemp asked if it was four galleys or forty. Bryan said if it were forty you would not have played to the end, and that galleys burn and go and cannot lie on that coast. Pope danced the old woman's part and got his laugh. By the jig the yard had made it forty galleys and Plymouth taken. You kept the four places, and left Plymouth in the yard. The play was the best Saturday of the month, and the part you played is not named here.
