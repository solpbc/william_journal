From nine until eleven the company rehearsed the old Knave and did not have the book. Cowley dried four times in the same place and apologised to Garnish, Heminges, Pope, and you. Kemp was not come; he was at his slippers. The tireman had padded Pope to a barrel and left Sincler in a doublet that would fit a rake. Pope said he needs none, that he is the before and Pope is the after. Garnish stopped prompting and read the play aloud. Heminges looked at the sun. You got all eight lines right and were thanked without kindness. They broke at eleven no better. The Rose has Hercules again.
