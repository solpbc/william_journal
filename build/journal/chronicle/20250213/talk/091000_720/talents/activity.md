At rehearsal the K was still only a letter. Kemp was sharp and still said you. You asked again for a line, not a number, and you did not get one. Burbage called for the Errors cues. The Rose had a Siege of London. You did not go to read the bill, and you did not cross. Phillips kept the songs as they were and did not fetch Nick. You did not move a third stop. Kemp kept the watchet gown out of the room. Nothing was bought.
