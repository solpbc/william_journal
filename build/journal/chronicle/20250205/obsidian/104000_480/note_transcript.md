Wednesday. 1594. At the desk, not at the play.

The bill said Mahomet. Ralph says a head,
and nothing worth the powder.
London is quick with thunder.
A face it keeps.

Titus is at the Cross Keys at two.
I am not in it. The play goes on.

[struck, and not to be filed]
If noise were fame, the post would be a book.
[the line crossed. the sheet is not
a leaf. it does not go in the folder.]

The folder has two leaves. It had two
yesterday. It has two now. No name on
either. Not today.

The packet is still tied. Tomorrow,
after the play. Not at noon.
