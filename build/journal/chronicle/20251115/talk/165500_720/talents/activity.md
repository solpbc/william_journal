At the reckoning Heminges would not read a sum. Thursday's seven shillings is already entered, and he will not enter it twice. Monday the house is dark. Tuesday you are Gaunt. Wednesday morning the lovers are read at Holywell. The company has not read them. Wednesday's Shrew is not you. Thursday Navarre, Friday the Shrew, Saturday Two Gentlemen, and you are in all three. The three court nights stand as they were said: the Navarre comedy, the lovers, and something old to laugh at. Not the king. You did not choose the old play. Kemp stayed on you. The tally-book was not closed. No court money has come. You did not open a new total.
