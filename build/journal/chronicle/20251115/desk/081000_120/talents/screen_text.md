## 00:00 · The glass

Loving countryman? Richard Quiney. From the Bell in Carter Lane, these two days, and my boots not yet dry.
Richard. Tell me the errand. I am not coming to Carter Lane before noon.
The town's business. A brief for a collection through the shires, for the relief of Stratford after its fires. The certified schedules of loss are in my saddlebag, for the Lord Keeper's men.
And have the Lord Keeper's men seen them?
