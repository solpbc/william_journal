Tuesday night. Till ten, and no further.

Item, cloves, ij d.

A pennyworth on the way home, and another for the night. That is the line. The coffer stays shut. I am not ruling a new total under a toothache.

The tooth has had its scene. This page will not be about a tooth. The street will not move because a man is in pain, and I will not let the pain choose the line.

So I stop. This is stubbornness. It is not a scene.
