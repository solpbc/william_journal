25 October 1595. A loose leaf. The red bundle stays tied, and this is not it.

The parents, when they come in.
The wedding meats, which have already been called for.
The Nurse at the bed-curtain. She has the noise. The girl sleeps.

Not written, and not to be written as if they were this page:
the tomb,
the letter that does not arrive,
a song at the door,
any page of a new play.

She has not been found yet. This leaf only says who will have the noise when she is.
