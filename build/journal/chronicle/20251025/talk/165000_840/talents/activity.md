After Two Gentlemen, which you played, the sharers reckoned the week. Your part was thirty-eight shillings, a share. You will not write a coffer figure under it. Wednesday's eightpence stays where it was entered. The hired men stay at ten shillings. Christmas stays a blank. Tuesday's Shrew does not include you, and no man was named in your place. The king stands for Thursday week. You are not opening Monday again. Phillips said the mornings have not begun. You are going home.
