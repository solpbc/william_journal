Still in a duke's gown from Richard the Third, you took the call from Somerset House. You are sent for three nights at Richmond: St Stephen's, St John's, and Innocents'. He wants the Navarre comedy, the tragedy of two lovers, and something old to laugh at with a cup. He refused the king, because a book has come out of Antwerp with Essex's name on it. He stood at the back of yesterday's house like a carter, and Burbage made him weep. Heneage's office is not filled. You will be paid slow. Nothing was paid. He wants clean hose, and he did not say whose heel. You did not choose the old play.
