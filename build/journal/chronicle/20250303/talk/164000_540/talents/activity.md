After the Two Gentlemen, Bryan gave the court's Shrovetide as heard of: Whitehall since Saturday, a tilt, a tourney yesterday, and tonight a masque of Proteus and a rock of adamant. You said nobody was asked. Kemp set the scholars playing for nothing against a penny. Bryan had the warrant for December's twenty pounds drawn and moving, and my lord's word to stop cleanly on Shrove Tuesday and hand the Mayor no stick. You did not fetch the money. You left the old count where it was.
