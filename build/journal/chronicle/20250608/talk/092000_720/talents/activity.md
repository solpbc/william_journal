At St Helen's you counted two more servants at the Mayor's pew than at Easter. Master Thwaite preached the gift of tongues and set the glass down after one turning. He then read a portion of the Homily against Disobedience and Wilful Rebellion, and you heard it out. In the north aisle she did not turn, until the second lesson, once, toward the south, and away. You lost the place in the psalm. Nothing was said. She was gone by the north porch, and you did not meet her at the door.
