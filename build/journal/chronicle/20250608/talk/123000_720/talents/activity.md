You dined at the Fields' with the shop shut, on lamb and the first peas. Richard had read the comedy with the door shut. He called it wanton, and the best-made thing he had read that was not in Latin, and he asked for the copy. You said it was Garnish's and the company's. He said a Millington would one day have it from a hired man for forty pence. Jacqueline said he was right and you were right. You refused the Holinshed. You said the king was not finished, and nothing more. They talked of thin corn, and whether the bailiff would stint the maltsters. Jacqueline asked if the streets were quiet by Bishopsgate. You said quiet enough, and you told them nothing of Friday.
