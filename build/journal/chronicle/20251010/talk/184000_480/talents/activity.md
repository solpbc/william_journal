The first hot bran went on at home. Two nights, salt water, Tuesday at ten, twelve pence unpaid. She kept the twelve pence apart from Monday's sixpence. You had asked whether it could wait till Lent, and he had ended it. The loaf is still a penny and a full thumb short. Alse left clove-wool in a saucer by the inkhorn. You did not write. You went to him yourself. She had not sent you.
