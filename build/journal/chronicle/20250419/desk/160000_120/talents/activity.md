Heminges called from Aldermanbury with a house full of boys. Sam had a cold and was being kept dumb by the fire with honey. Arthur was learning the owl too well, as if it were a judgment. You said the owl may be a judgment and the cuckoo cannot. You agreed: Sam if he can, Arthur if he must, and no word to Sam. Asked whether the king goes forward, you said he is talking. He wished you Easter joy.
