## 00:00 · The glass

Will? John Heminges. From Aldermanbury, with a house full of boys.
John. I heard him this morning. He croaked like a rook.
Sam Latchet has a cold in the head. On Monday he is to sing the cuckoo to the biggest house of the spring. Phillips has him by the fire with honey, and is keeping him dumb.
Dumb is right. I did not give the word to anyone else at the yard.
