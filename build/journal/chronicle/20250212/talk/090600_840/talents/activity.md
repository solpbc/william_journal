In the third week of rehearsal Kemp played the remuneration with three farthings. He weighed them, bit one, and tried to buy Sam. Sam refused. Each pass was two lines longer than the last, and nobody made a total. Kemp said the groundlings never paid a penny to hear what was set down. You asked, very courteously, which of the new lines were better, so that you might write them. Garnish wrote a K at the head of a margin and did not write a figure under it. Burbage took Berowne's speech on love to the stable wall. You said Hilary term ended today and that you were not going to Westminster. Pope said the gowns will thin.
