In Paul's Walk the tale was a third smaller. The phrase was with little riches. Stones that may be ore, a river as wide as a sea, a golden city one nation further up, and men whose heads grow beneath their shoulders. You asked if anyone had spoken with a man off the ship. Nobody had. The court had had it yesterday. Drayton had looked for his own name on the Cambridge leaf first. You had seen it on Thursday and had not bought the book. Of Raleigh he said eight months, and a story. You said it was a living. You bought the quire for fivepence. The stall-keeper was not a man you named.
