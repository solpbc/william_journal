You were at the Theatre by ten with the scarf on the left side. Kemp used the name and called Monday his, Sunday having been church. Heminges had you down for Navarre at two and would not have Raleigh in the book. You told them the river's gold and Paul's little riches, and the fivepence. Burbage told you the scarf comes off for the oath. Kemp was not offered a roll. The plot does not carry the name. Abel scraped it on Saturday.
