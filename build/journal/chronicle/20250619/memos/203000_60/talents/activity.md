At the window you said the play at the Theatre was thin and that you were a duke in it. You did not name the play. You said what your father used to tell of this day, the trades on waggons and Herod in the street, all of it gone before you could see it. He tells it as a man tells a fair he was at. You said players are now a thing the Mayor writes letters about. Same trade. Different day. You named no town.
