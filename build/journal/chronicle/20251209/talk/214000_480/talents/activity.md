The steward put a purse in Heminges's hand before the last speech, and not after it. Five pounds. Twelve shillings and sixpence the share. The glass was four shillings, and Heminges did not take it off the five. Lady Hoby thanked Nicholas and Arthur by name. Sir Robert said it was very well penned, and that he had not thought to be made to pity him, and that he would remember that it can be done. You looked at the hand, not the face, and you did not decide the sentence. Lady Hoby ended it: supper, the king has had his say. He rose when the women rose, and not before. Nobody asked the players anything. No errand came of it. The tide was gone. The boys and the baskets went in the cart.
