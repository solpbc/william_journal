Lady Hoby had the candles off the players' floor and would not wait on the gentlemen for them. The floor was six paces by four. A screen stood at one end, and a stool for the book behind it. Marchpane and hippocras were going as you came in. Twelve had sat at table. My lord was not among them. Sir Edward asked for King Richard whole, the parliament and all, after the marchpane, and he presented you himself. Sir Robert was in the chair. You bowed and did not explain. You did not move anyone to make Gaunt's death more convenient.
