You played Gaunt for the sixth time, to a full house on a raw day, and the jaw held. You were done in the second act. Margery reported gowns, citizens, two bishops' men, and an old gentleman in a frieze cloak at the back of the twopenny room who paid for three. He struck the rail with his fist, and she took it for clapping. Heminges guessed a country justice up for the term. Nobody carried the guess further. The Rose bill was Barnardo. You did not cross, and you do not know their receipt.
