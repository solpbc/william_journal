Margery Pell had the morning already, and her figure was two hundred, from talk on the stair, not from a count of coats in the road. Cowley had a boy's account: apprentices, one man, a gate, and no count. Larkham would not invent a third figure. The only sentence Cowley and Larkham both accepted was that people were running toward the noise. Cowley proposed going down to see which story was true. You would not. You had seen the open gate, the wrenched hasp, the cap and the backs, and not a man brought out. Nobody went.
