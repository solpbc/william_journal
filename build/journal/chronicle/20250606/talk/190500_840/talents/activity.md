At supper Verbeke had the looms' version: a silkweaver, like himself, and English. He said so it is not yet us, and he cut his bread smaller. You told the table what you had seen, and you did not describe a rescue. The Mayor and the dearth stayed what the water-bearer had called talk. Mistress Ansell kept Verbeke under his own name, at his own work, in his own bed, and kept the household at the table. Alse stopped at one gate. Nobody went back.
