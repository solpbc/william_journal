Will. You went off bent and I would know you are come straight. The barn has its roof on. Your father told the Cross you raised it single-handed, and Sadler let him. The children are brown as nuts and have quarrelled since Thursday over your girl at the window. Susanna says she said yes too quick. Judith says she did right, a man under a window will not wait for ever. Hamnet says the boy should have brought a ladder, and rope. I walked by that house on market day. Another pane is gone; I did not mend it. Send the Michaelmas money when it falls and not before. I had rather you ate. Your wife, Anne.
