At the puppet booth a wooden king lost his crown to a wooden devil, and had it set on again every quarter-hour. Burbage stood through it three times. A cut-purse was taken a yard from Arthur, who gave the arrest back in four voices. Kemp danced for nothing in a ring of two hundred, and bowed when he saw you. Gilbert, behind a stall of points and hat-bands, affected not to know any players. From the fourth hour the back complained. You bought three gingerbread men before you thought, one with gilt, and stood with them in your hand.
