She took you to Leadenhall because you had offered an opinion on what the winter was costing. You said four shillings for a side of bacon, and she would not write the number. At each stall she told you that you were no use at a price. You carried a side of bacon, suet, a firkin of salt, onions on a string, and a cheese. You paid nothing toward the stores. The firing stays the sixpence. The candles at fourpence are your own. You gave your back.
