At the Theatre James Burbage put his knife two inches into a spongy oak post laid in 1576. The lease is twenty-one years, and the ground falls back to Giles Allen on the thirteenth of April, 1597. Allen is slow to renew. Cuthbert asked who would pay the carpenter. You named no price. You gave two mornings in March and left the law to the family. You did not talk about any later building.
