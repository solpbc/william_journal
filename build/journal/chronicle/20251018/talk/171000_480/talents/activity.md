The hall candle was already burning. She had lit it at five, and it is the firing sixpence. You ate broth. The jaw is still tender, and you did not call it quiet. You read nothing. The share of thirty-six shillings is unspent. Shoreditch mud is on the hem, and you told Alse to leave it until it is dry. You are for evening prayer, and then bed, with the cat at your feet, before the Bow bell.
