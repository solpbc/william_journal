You waited in the passage and came for the reckoning only. The share was thirty-six shillings. You did not go into the yard. Thursday's house had filled three galleries, and Pope's Gaunt drew no laugh on this England. Heminges would not say the boxes were better without you. Your line had reached Aldermanbury. Heminges and Bryan will wait on my lord's people at Somerset House within the week, so the Christmas money is not stranded with a dead man's clerks. They did not name a man in the passage. You did not spend the thirty-six on the way. The sun was down before five. There is no inn-yard this winter, and the Theatre will be played cold.
