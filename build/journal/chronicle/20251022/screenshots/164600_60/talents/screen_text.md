## 00:00 · Torchlight at Crosby Place gate

The tall stone oriel and dark timber gatehouse of Crosby Place seen from the corner of Great St Helen's lane at dusk on the evening of the new moon: one high mullioned window lit with yellow candlelight in the stone bay above, a link-boy's pitch torch smoking in the iron cresset by the shut gate below, the cobbles wet and dark. No hand and no face.
