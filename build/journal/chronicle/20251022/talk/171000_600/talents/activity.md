At supper you ate broth and the toasted crabs that were already in the house. You did not put a price on them. Threepence stayed the dinner's bill, and this was not a second one. You gave the house no title and no sorrow. Gib stayed by the hearth. Mistress Ansell kept the hall candle and told you again about the one flame upstairs. Alse said the latch had stuck, and you left it unmended. You did not tell them of the earl.
