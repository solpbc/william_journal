At night you said Southwell was hanged in the morning, and that you did not go and had never been going. You had three accounts that did not match, and you believed the one in which the people cried out to let him be dead first. You called him a poet and refused to make that a likeness with the play. You are thirty and he was thirty-three. You did not say your father's night prayer. You came back to the messenger, who is to say almost nothing. The pen in your hand had no ink. You stopped.
