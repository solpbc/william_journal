Before dinner. The spring note is written. The winter note is written. I am not copying either of them onto this leaf a second time. They are in the foul papers, and they are enough.

The door, after them, is one line:

The wordes of Mercurie are harsh after the songes of Apollo.

I tried the last scene pleading. I tried it plain. The plain refusal is the livelier. It is no safer at the box. I am keeping the plain one. Abel may have the book after Richard. He may not have it before. No part leaves this table today.
