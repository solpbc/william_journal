After Richard the Third you put forty-odd clean leaves into Garnish's hands. He read standing and found Katharine and Rosaline with the same lover. You chose. Rosaline keeps Berowne and Katharine keeps Dumaine. Heminges wrote one line, Navarre ended this day. Pope asked whether a reward was the point, and you kept the plain refusal. The seven shillings are Friday's, and they are the company's. No part was given out.
