Anne.

Gilbert bid me set thy question first. Thou asked whether
I mean to come at Lent, or only to say so. I mean to come.

The playing stops on Shrove Tuesday. There is the Queen's
money to be fetched and divided, which may be mid-March.
I will ride, and be with thee and the children before
Mid-Lent Sunday. I will not name a day I cannot keep.

Thursday the new comedy is played. Four young men swear
a fast and shut the door on women. A princess comes with
three ladies, and the oath does not hold. They go disguised
and are known. A messenger brings a death, and the ladies
will not have them yet. The ladies have the better of it.

This is the shorter letter. It goes with the carrier on
the twenty-seventh.

God keep thee.
From London, the xvj of February 1594.
Thy husband, W. S.
