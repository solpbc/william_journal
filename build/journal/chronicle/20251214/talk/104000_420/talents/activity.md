After the sermon you told Mistress Ansell, in the porch, that you will not be at her table on Christmas Day. You are looked for at Richmond. The letter is unsealed and goes Thursday with Hobbins. You named the russet she chose, and that it is paid for, and you did not name Joan's. She will not bake on a rumour, and she will not say her playhouse sentence again. Master Thwaite had reached fourteenthly and would not give you the rest of it in a porch. He did not ask what the letter said.
