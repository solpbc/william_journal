Heminges stopped at the foot of the stair with a custard from his wife in a cloth. Mistress Ansell stood on the second step. You came down in a nightcap. The king is billed for tomorrow, the Inns have written it down, and Pope will play Gaunt. He has nothing till the garden in the third act. You said he will die at Ely House and rise a gardener. Heminges said he will get a laugh on this England. You said he will not. The roll stayed with Pope. Mistress Pope had said he was near weeping at it. Garnish has cut the long cue, and you did not take a length for it. The Rose has something of a woman and a wonder. You said to let them. You have a king and a custard. She said you were to have the custard and no more talk. You do not know tomorrow's laugh.
