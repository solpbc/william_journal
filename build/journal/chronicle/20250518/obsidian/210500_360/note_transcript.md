Sunday 18 May.
Board at Mistress Ansell's, iij d.
The loaf, a penny, a finger and a half short. She would not measure it again.

At the Split Hazel. Alice's bill, ij s. viij d. and the jug. I have not written a half.

Tom came from Paul's with a stationer in his teeth. Will mended him into a salt-fish on a stall, weeping brine when a beadle coughs, and Tom took the fish home. I said little, except to keep the wheel turning. Tom had not the money, and he admired the paper.
