After Titus, in the parlour, Burbage said your voice had held. The cold is gone. Kemp was civil and did not ask for a new laugh. You and Burbage counted seventeen days to Thursday the twentieth for Berowne, which is not three weeks. Heminges will take Nick to a Cheapside mercer tomorrow and call you when the man has named a price. He did not name one today. The Queen's twenty pounds are still not had. Edith's eight shillings stay unpaid, and they are not your purse. You mean to ask Field in the morning about a chronicle for Lent, and not to take it yet.
