With no morning rehearsal you stayed at the lodging while Mistress Ansell boiled Monday's ink from galls, copperas, and gum. Your horn was half full. It is twopence the horn when it wants filling. You told them Titus is at two, and that you would take the threepence board. The penny loaf was still a finger short. Gib was on the book. Gillis was already at his loom in Norton Folgate. The Candlemas packet stays until Thursday.
