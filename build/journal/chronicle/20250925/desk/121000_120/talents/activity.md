John Heminges called from Aldermanbury with the tailor standing by. Tomorrow is the full rehearsal in the apparel. The crown from Harry the Sixth is new gilt at four shillings. The Master has saved the company a looking-glass broke every time. Nick has grown two fingers since Candlemas, and the gown is let down for eighteenpence, with no more cloth. He eats like two, and he sings in the yard of a morning. John heard the thick voice and said to have the tooth out before Thursday, because Gaunt must be heard at the back. You said after Thursday. Asked for Love's Labour's Won, you said when this king is buried.
