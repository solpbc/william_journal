## 00:00 · The glass

Will? John Heminges. From Aldermanbury, with the tailor standing by.
John. I am at the Theatre. Say the items.
Tomorrow is the full rehearsal, in the apparel. Item, the crown. The old one from Harry the Sixth is gone green at the points. I have had it new gilt, four shillings.
Item, a looking-glass to be broke every time we play him, which the Master has saved us, and I'll not pretend to grieve.
