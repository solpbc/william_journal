You whispered the night down. The street bar is down, and the only candle is on the boards behind the bed. You repeated the boy's thousand, and you still cannot see them. The sound comes and goes. Friday's gallows is a hundred yards off, over butter and a loaf. Gillis knew before you did what a crowd says to a stranger. If they come up Bishopsgate they come for the Mayor, and they would pass this door. You told yourself to remember the dark, and to begin the two houses in the street, among the serving-men. You let it go by. You opened no book.
