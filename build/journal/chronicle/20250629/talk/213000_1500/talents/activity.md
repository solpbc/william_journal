About nine a sound began to the south-east that was not bells. A boy cried a thousand on Tower Hill, with warders among them. You saw none of it. You and Mistress Ansell dropped the street-door bar and spoke of it as an ordinary night, tired of the heat. She will have no light in the front. She asked whether the upstairs candle was remembered. It is yours, and it is the only light in the house. Gillis went up to the garret without a word. Alse is to sleep in her mistress's chamber. You said you would bar your own door.
