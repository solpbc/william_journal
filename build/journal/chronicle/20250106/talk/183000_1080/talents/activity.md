After Errors you went up to Holywell for the cake. The bean fell to Nick, who has played queens since he was eleven and was king for the first time. He set Kemp a paternoster, Heminges no Item till morning, and Sam the last slice. Kemp lasted about half the prayer, named the Admiral's men at Greenwich, and did not mind them. James said twenty-one years, and two to run. Cuthbert moved the jug. Heminges gave eleven pound eight shillings to the sharers' side, without a drum or a trumpet, then said Item and was fined a slice for Sam. Sam did not speak. Mistress Burbage did not speak.
