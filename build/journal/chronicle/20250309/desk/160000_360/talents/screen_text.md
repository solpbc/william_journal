## 00:00 · Anne's letter of 27 February, open on the board

Will. The groats came nine days late and were spent inside the hour. Judith bought a ballad she cannot read and made her sister say it over until she had it. She sings it at the wash. Hamnet bought a whistle and lent the odd penny to a boy who has not paid, which makes him your father's grandson. Your father's cough is gone. Come, then. I shall believe the horse when I hear it.

## 02:20 · The lower lines, a different ink

Your father has a little reckoning at the whittawer's, fourteen and six, which need not trouble you.
