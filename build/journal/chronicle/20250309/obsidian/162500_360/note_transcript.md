Anne.

I have your letter. The children's news is the better
half of it. Judith's ballad I can hear from here, and
Hamnet's debtor also.

I will talk of the house's needs when I come. What must
be paid before then, you judge. I had rather your
judgment at the door than mine at this distance.

The horse you shall believe when you hear him. I have
not bespoken him.

God keep you and the children.
From London, the first Sunday in Lent,
the ix of March 1594.
Your husband, W. S.
