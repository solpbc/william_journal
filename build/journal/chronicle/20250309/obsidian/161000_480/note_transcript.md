Anne.

Fourteen and six is a sum. A sum that need not trouble me
has already troubled you, or it would not have been set
down in another ink for me to miss.

Pay nothing on my father's account until I come. I will
not have his shop-debts made kind in a postscript, nor
my wife turned messenger for a man who can write his own
name.

From London, the ix of March 1594.
