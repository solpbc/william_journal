At the reckoning John Heminges set your share at twenty-two shillings, a pound and two shillings, in silver. You counted them and were not short. The gown's fifty shillings stayed unpaid, and Edith's eight stayed unpaid. Neither was taken out of the twenty-two. The Queen's twenty pounds were not in the room. The coffer was not brought down. No new stock was written. Your twopence to the carrier stayed out of the company book. The K still had no figure. Margery Pell had no receipt for the Rose's Tasso. Nothing was bought. You did not send the letter with the silver.
