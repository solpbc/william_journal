You found six packhorses steaming in the Bell yard. Hobbins gave the road as oats dear at Oxford, malt dearer at home, and a harvest you could carry in your hat. Anne's letter said the money came, and gave you Susanna, Judith, and the brook at Shottery. You left her jokes unanswered. Quiney is for London at Martinmas. You did not repay the two pound. The letter back is twopence. The fourth line is still to be written on the corner of the table.
