With the children left in Joan's charge, you and Anne walked the field path toward Shottery. She named who held the close before her father, which hedge her brother laid, and a stile where a cousin broke an arm at seven. You began to turn the stile into a tale, heard yourself, and stopped. She noticed. You talked of the carrier on the eighteenth, the horses' pace, and what you will carry: the pack, the sheets, and the inkhorn. In the yard Bartholomew said the wheat was thin, and said to leave it. After that you walked without talking, and the silence was neither a quarrel nor a peace.
