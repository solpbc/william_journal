On the grass at the town end of the bridge you made three bark boats with elder-twig masts, one each. The twins raced them in the shallows, where the water was a hand deep. Hamnet's would not sail straight, and he said it was built crooked. Judith named hers after the ballad and sang it, and she did not give the words. Susanna read the two lovers and the wall, and asked why they did not simply use the door. You said the book does not say. You told them nothing, and they learned nothing. You left toward the middle of the afternoon.
