Dinner was at the bakehouse by the Cross. Hamnet Sadler set a penny loaf on the board, unasked, and said he was not the villain of a small loaf. You said that in Southwark they blamed the butter-wives, and whipped the boys who said so. Judith Sadler asked what they blame you for in London, and she laughed when you said the Lord Mayor blames you for the boys. Over the cheese, Tuesday was settled: the frame at seven, twelve men, ale after at the cousin's charge. Anne looked at your hands, then at Sadler's, and said nothing at all.
