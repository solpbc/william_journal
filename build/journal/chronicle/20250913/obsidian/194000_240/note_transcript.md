Heminges sends to Kettleby tonight. Monday stands as billed. No order has come. That is the reason, and it is not a better one.

Kemp said tour. The room did not agree. The Mayor writes of June, and the answer is a king put down. The cut stays out. I am not making a new sum of a good Saturday.
