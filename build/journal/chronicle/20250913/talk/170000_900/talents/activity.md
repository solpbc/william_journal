With the door shut and the boys gone, Cuthbert told the hearsay again and Bryan made him separate the letter from the clerk. James had eighty-four and two years on the ground, and did not march. Heminges said a letter asking is not an order granted, and that the reckoning is thirty-three shillings a share. Kemp said tour. The room did not agree to tour. You said the Mayor writes of June and a king is to be put down, and that my lord should hear it from you before the board. Heminges sends to Kettleby tonight. Monday is as billed. There is to be no talk of it at the Jackdaw.
