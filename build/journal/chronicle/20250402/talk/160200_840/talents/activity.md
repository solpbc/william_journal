Your father was cutting fine gloves by eye. He said Gilbert had been counting, and he named the other baker at thirty shillings, the shoemaker under two pounds, and the skins at a few shillings. You did not correct him, and you did not put the ticket on the bench. He showed a yellowed scrap once, the pattern from his year as bailiff, and said they would have made him a gentleman once, and then wool fell. You said a player is a gentleman three hours every afternoon. Your mother remembered the colour of the ink and would not name it. The scrap went back. Nothing was filed, asked, or promised.
