At the reckoning Heminges would not name a share. Last Saturday was thirty shillings, the best since Christmas, and he left it there. Margery Pell would not turn Monday's two-thirds into a purse. Garnish read twenty-four lines, with nothing added and no song. Kemp kept you, and did not offer a song. Edith's eight shillings stayed unpaid. The watchet stayed at fifty shillings unpaid. The Queen's twenty pounds was not in the room. Monday's bill is The Two Gentlemen of Verona at two of the clock. You did not take a figure home.
