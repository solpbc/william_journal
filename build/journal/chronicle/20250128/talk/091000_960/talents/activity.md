At the long table, Abel brought seventeen parts copied in six days. You had said on Saturday that Abel reads. This morning you read the whole book, every voice, and you read Costard badly so Kemp would mend him. Pope tried Armado's first letter, lost the third word, and the table was helpless. Kemp unrolled his part against Richard's on the floor, and neither called a winner. Augustine asked for the songs by Friday. You promised a pointing, not a new copy. Nick asked whether the Princess weeps after the news of her father. You said she governs, and he marked his roll. The gown was repeated as John's words from last night. John was not in the room.
