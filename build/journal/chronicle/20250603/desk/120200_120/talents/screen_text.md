## 00:00 · Prices

Prices. Tuesday. Leadenhall. bought nothing.
A hungry man, an angry man.
Hunger breaks stone walls.
The loaf goes by the Mayor's weight and the belly by God's.
the penny loaf is still a penny. shrunk on the pan. not measured.
the talk is Star Chamber, the present scarcity, justices sent home to keep hospitality. I was not in the chamber. I do not know it.
