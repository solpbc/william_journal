You stood by the meal-sacks and bought nothing. A man in an old soldier's coat said the talk was that the Council had spoken in Star Chamber of the present scarcity and was sending justices home. He said he had not been in the chamber. You heard three proverbs. The penny loaves on the assize-pan were shrunk. You did not measure them. Beadles stood about watching knots of idle silk-weavers and men in old soldiers' coats.
