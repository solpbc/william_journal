Mistress Ansell said it was no comedy, that you had walked like a man following a hearse and then stood. You said he was dying, and that you are, in the second act. She called that the most sensible thing you have yet done in a play. The loaf is still a finger and a half short. The board was three pence. You were not cast, and you did not open the third act. Verbeke stayed at the stair and kept his own name.
