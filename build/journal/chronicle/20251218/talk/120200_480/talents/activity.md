Hobbins read the pack back before he corded it. The quarter's sixty shillings went in canvas, with the sugar and the raisins, the prunes and the ballad, the grey kerchief with no verse, the oranges, the sugar-loaf, the cinnamon, the holland, the russet, the book with no riddles, the ballad-sheet, the ball, and three groats. He would not sing the tune in the yard. Anne's letter came south. You heard two faults, the street, the sword, and the porch, and you did not add a line to Sunday's letter. You read the cheaper pane twice and laughed with your shoulders. Nobody shouted down the yard. You did not count a coffer.
