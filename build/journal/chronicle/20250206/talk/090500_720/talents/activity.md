In the cold parlour Phillips had Nick walk the Princess's first long speech, not stand rigid to show he could. The speech itself is not set down. Sam copied the walk and reached the door before the sentence ended. You heard that your own pointing had put the breath in the wrong place, and you changed two stops on the roll and no word. Nick's relief was practical. The middle of it was no longer a test. Phillips said a boy has a pint of breath, and not to spend it all on the first line.
