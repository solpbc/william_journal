Thursday. 1594.

Two groats, sewn into the paper.
One, and one. The string is mine.
The outside is still blank. I will
write it when I am looking at him,
and not before, or I shall write a
speech.

Hobbins after the play. Not now.
The noon yard at the Bell is not
the hour I am keeping.

Two stops, moved. The boy kept
the breath. I will not move a third.
