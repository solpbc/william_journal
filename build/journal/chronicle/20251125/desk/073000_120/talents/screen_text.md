## 00:00 · The glass

John? Will. From Great St Helen's. God give you joy of your day.
Will. I am at Aldermanbury. Say it, and then we will have the business, since I am awake.
I am not making you a speech before breakfast. Take the day by the item, if that is how you take it.
A wife. A full cradle. A house that does not smoke. A company eleven pound richer of a Thursday. That is the joy. I will not have it in a lump.
