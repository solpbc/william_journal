At the morning rehearsal the book on the peg was the cut book of Richard. You are Gaunt: the dying, the garden, and the counsel, and no longer part than that. Three leaves stay under the book. You are not playing a deposition, and you are not feeding Burbage a speech out of the chest. Abel Garnish marked the cut plot and refused a page at the door. John kept the tally shut. Nick was not called. Nobody is to startle him, and Arthur's hems were not this morning's work. You promised again that tomorrow's naming would have no surprise from you. The chest stayed shut.
