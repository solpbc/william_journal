At Wycombe you had walked every mile. Hobbins had offered the spare at the foot of the hill and you had refused, and by the top the back was better going than sitting. In March you had ridden the other way like a man who had been paid. He priced the houses he knows: a mortgaged farm, an inn changing hands at Michaelmas, and a widow at Wheatley who will not take forty pound. That forty is hers. You had opened your mouth for the house by the chapel, and you asked the price of oats at Wycombe instead. The answer took the rest of the talk, and it did not leave you a figure you may repeat. You did not buy. You did not move her price onto the chapel.
