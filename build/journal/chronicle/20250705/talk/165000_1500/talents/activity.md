The reckoning is ten shillings. You were not at Two Gentlemen, and the book will say so. Margery calls it a better stair than Tuesday and not her box. Kemp wants the roads. James wants his doors open and does not get a vote. George will not answer a two-day-old paper by leaving, not this hour. John sets the question at Monday and refuses a new total. You take the ten as received. You tell them you are going home by Bishopsgate Street, and you do not tell them why. Nothing about the country is decided.
