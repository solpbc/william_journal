You paid a penny at the door and stood in the yard, left of the stage, by a post you could lean on. A fair house, not a great one. Henslowe was at the stair-foot with his eyes on the boxes. Alleyn, the tallest man on either bank, played the first Edward in gilded mail under the throne that was hung there in June. Drums, a pageant, blows given and taken from outside. This king never doubts. He filled the house like water in a jug, and the yard loved it.
