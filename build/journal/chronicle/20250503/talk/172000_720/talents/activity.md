Heminges named the share at thirty-four shillings, against thirty-eight last Saturday, and did not open the coffer. You gave no total. Bryan said the court went down to Greenwich today, with my lord and Kettleby, and that he had it from the household. You said there is no summons before Michaelmas. The licensed book is out, and not a loss. Monday is Errors, and you took no roll. You are enclosing nothing to Edmund.
