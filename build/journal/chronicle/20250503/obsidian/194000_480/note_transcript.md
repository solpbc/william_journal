Brother.

You are fifteen today. Finish school.

Latin is the only property a player never has to hire. If at seventeen you still want to hear how Master Burbage dies, come and see it for a penny, like a Christian.

I enclose nothing. The sword's first owner sends his commendations to its second.

The court went down to Greenwich today, and with it my lord, and Kettleby, and any chance of a summons before Michaelmas.

This goes by the carrier of the fifteenth.

Your brother, W. S.
