At supper Mistress Ansell remembered the bells rung through this night for the souls, and how that was stopped. She did not say she missed them. She said her husband has been dead six years at Hallowtide, and that she boils his ink every Monday by his receipt, which is remembering enough for a Christian woman. Alse said the horn is the old horn, and that she boils the new on Monday. Verbeke said that in Ghent they remembered both ways in one street, and it went ill. He went out by the north door. Master Thwaite heard him and did not take it up. You said nothing, and you were asked nothing.
