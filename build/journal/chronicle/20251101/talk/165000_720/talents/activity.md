After Errors, in which you played Egeon, the sharers reckoned the week. Your part was thirty-five shillings, received. You will not write a coffer under it. Last Saturday was thirty-eight, and this one is not a quarrel with that one. Wednesday's eightpence stays entered. The hired men stay at ten shillings. Christmas stays a blank, with no date and no sum. Monday is the Navarre comedy, and you are in it. Thursday is the king again, and you are in it. Phillips said yesterday's morning is not done and not priced. You did not show the page.
