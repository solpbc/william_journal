Burbage had lost the joins between the lists and the farewell to Gaunt, and he sounded like three different men. You asked for a plain reading before anybody changed a word. The joins came back the second time through. At the lists at Coventry you stooped to shift the armour-basket out of Sly's way, and the back answered. You played Gaunt the rest of the morning from a stool. Burbage said that was the best thing in it.
