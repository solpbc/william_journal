Garnish gave out the rolls as he came to each name. Burbage needed none. Phillips took Bolingbroke, Heminges York, Bryan Northumberland, Sly Mowbray in the lists, Pope a gardener of eighteen lines and an apricock tree, and Sincler the Groom. Kemp stood with his hand out. There was none for him. You told him there is no place where a man could laugh and the king still stand. He would have had a laugh at the deposing. The Master has cut it. Kemp said that he and the Master are served alike, and he took the jig as a groat in settlement of a pound.
