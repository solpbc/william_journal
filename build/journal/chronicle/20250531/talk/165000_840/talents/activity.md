John would not name a share while the bag was unshut. You recalled last Saturday as six-and-thirty and did not ask him to better it. Pope said Titus went and that you were not in it. You said you were at the board, and the bill was left as a bill. The Rose's bill for today is marked paid, the Revels' fee, and John would not add a sum. Burbage asked after the glass. You said the line is struck and none is bought. You left without a figure.
