You spoke Gaunt, and "This royall throne of Kings" landed in a hush. You were dead by the middle of the second act and stood at the tiring-house door after. Burbage sat upon the ground to tell sad stories. In the fourth act the crown changed heads between two speeches, and the Abbot said "A wofull Pageant haue we here beheld" of a pageant the house was not let behold. You did not play the deposition.
