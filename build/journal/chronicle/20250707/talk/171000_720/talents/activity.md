After a half yard, with the door shut, the sharers sat on upturned chests. Kemp argued for the road. George put a constable on every mile. James wanted the house earning. Richard wanted three clear weeks. John's sheet carried it, and you voted with him. Last day is Saturday the twenty-sixth. The house opens again on Monday the twenty-fifth of August. The question comes back on the seventeenth. Augustine sat the chest and did not speak. Kemp was against.
