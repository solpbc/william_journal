You were walking Navarre's oath when Alse came up with the washing and stayed in the door. You gave her three years' study, one meal a day, and no woman within a mile of the court. She asked who is to cook. Mistress Ansell called from below that Alse was not to be encouraged after eight. You laughed with your shoulders. Alse had never seen a play, and she found the hole before the scene does.
