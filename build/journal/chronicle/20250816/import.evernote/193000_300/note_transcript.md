## Chapel Street Walk — The Brick and Timber House
Source: Chapel Street Walk — The Brick and Timber House.md

16 August 1595, evening at Henley Street.

Walked down Chapel Street in Stratford this afternoon with Anne and my brother Richard, looking across at the corner house by the Guild Chapel. Brick and timber, three storeys high, a broken pane upstairs, and nettles standing at the gate. Richard tapped the wall with his stick and judged the framing sound under the neglect. Anne stood longer looking past the gate into the ground behind it and said she knew what she would do with the garden.

I asked no one in the street whose the house is, nor what it would cost. Walking back toward Henley Street I asked Anne for three years. She answered that she will give me till she believes me.
