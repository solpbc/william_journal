Anne's short letter gave the three of them equal room. Hamnet outran the Sadler boy to the bridge and back, and in his own line he has built a water-wheel in the gutter. Judith says that is not what it is called, and she has a second ballad. Susanna has found a fault in your last and will tell you of it herself. The letter she means is the one already in her hand. Nothing in it is illness.
