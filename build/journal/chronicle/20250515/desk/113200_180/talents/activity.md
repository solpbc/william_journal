You marked the thimble's paper for Susanna, not before the Monday after Rogation Sunday, and that day is the twenty-sixth. Two letters at twopence and the parcel at sixpence came to tenpence, which is the standing rate and not a new one. Edmund's letter went as it was written. Gilbert's parcel came back with you. Hobbins handed up Anne's short letter. You were due to speak Egeon at two.
