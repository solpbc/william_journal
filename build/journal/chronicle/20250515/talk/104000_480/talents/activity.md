Field sold you the Plutarch at fourteen shillings unbound, and asked his Holinshed home by Whitsun. You paid the fourteen and promised nothing about Whitsun. A turned letter was ringed, and his thumb was the one on the ring. You asked what becomes of an error once enough copies have escaped. He said it acquires company. The next sheet was Theseus, with an Amazon queen. You said a few lines would stay and that you were not starting a play from them. He said it is market day at home, and he never passes a Thursday but he knows it. You carried the sheets off flat. Jacqueline told you to move the dripping paper. You did not promise a day for the chronicle.
