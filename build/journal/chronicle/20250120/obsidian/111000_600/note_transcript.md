I wrote this, and I struck it.

The hedge is loud before I have a bird to put in it.

That is not a spring. That is a man who wants the song finished because the afternoon is empty. The owl is not in my ear yet. I will not hang a winter on a spring I do not believe. The door at the end of the comedy is still open. I will shut it tomorrow, before dinner, or I will not shut it at all. I will not shut it today to be rid of it.
