At the end of an hour and a quarter he said what he had found. A prince who is obeyed, a friar who means well, two children who die of not minding their elders, and nothing that touches the state or any person living. You asked if it was nothing. He said he could find something if you were lonely for it. He signed the last leaf. He told you the other play keeps within its bounds in Shoreditch, and to let it. Heminges counted out seven shillings. After Tilney had turned to the painted paper, still in the room, you said you had been cut twice and never so slighted. Heminges said a clean licence is the only kind he can afford. You did not put the three leaves back.
