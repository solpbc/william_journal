At the post by the Bull you read the Rose's new title with Phillips. It is Barnardo and Fiammetta, with no author's name. He had it from Raye that the piece is two lovers and a jealous old husband, and that the Admiral's tireman has been buying Italian sleeves. You laughed. Your own two Italian lovers are in the red-tied bundle, and the vault of their deaths is still to write. You let the other house go first. You are not cast. You went back toward the desk. You wrote down no sum.
