Gilbert called from St Bride's with the shop shut, speaking for nothing. Richard is twenty-one and neither of you has sent. He read three shops twice: the other baker forty-one shillings, the shoemaker forty-four and six, the whittawer fourteen and six. He called it near five pound and did not ask you to pay. You asked which baker stands at the church porch. He did not know. You said you are for home in a fortnight.
