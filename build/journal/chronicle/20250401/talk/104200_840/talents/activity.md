Susanna named the cooper's plot and what was saved, Judith shouted what the street had shouted, and Hamnet had a nail that had bent in the heat. The carpenter said fire makes work. Anne came from the baker's with a new leather bucket and said it makes debt. The twins disputed the handle. When Susanna said you had answered a question nobody asked, you took the bucket yourself. Anne said you would go home by the Guild Chapel.
