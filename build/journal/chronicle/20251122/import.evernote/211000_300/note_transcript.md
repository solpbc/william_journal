## Mechanicals Casting — Notes After the Jackdaw
Source: Mechanicals Casting — Notes After the Jackdaw.md

22 November 1595, late at Great St Helen's after the Jackdaw.

Read the three casting sheets to Will Kemp across the table. He stood up from the bench and roared Ercles' vein so loud Agnes dropped a jug. Then he sat down again, read the three leaves through a second time, and said, "Thou'st put me in, thou villain." When I told him aloud about the ass's head — which is still spoken, not yet on paper — he laughed till he spilled his ale and Agnes charged us double.

Working points before writing the rehearsal scene: the three sheets stop cleanly after the casting; keep the other five tradesmen plain, earnest, and dead serious about their parts so Bottom's ambition carries against honest gravity rather than cheap mockery.
