You laid three sheets by Kemp's trencher and went on with your eggs. He read them standing. Six tradesmen meet to cast a play. The weaver would be the lover, the lady, and the lion. At the tyrant's line he roared once, and Agnes Tipping dropped a jug. He sat and read the three pages again. He said the weaver would play the whole of it and be loved, that it was himself, and he said thou. It was the first thou. You said you had put in the best of him. You told him that in the third act the man is given an ass's head and a queen falls in love with him, and that she would not have him otherwise. He laughed and spilled, and he was charged double. Dinner is sixpence. You did not name a second figure, and you did not name a day for the rest of the play. Garnish closed his book on the tally. Burbage stayed on you. The play is not finished. The head was spoken. It is not on the sheets.
