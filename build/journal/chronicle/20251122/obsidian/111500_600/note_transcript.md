Three sheets. Not finished. The xxij of November 1595.

Six tradesmen, met to cast a play for a duke's wedding.

The weaver. Is all our company heere?

He would be the lover. He would be the lady. Then he will have this, and no more of it is written fair:

let mee play the Lyon to

The line where a man may stop:

This is Ercles vaine, a tyrants vaine

The other five are names and blanks. Nothing after. The head is not on these sheets.
