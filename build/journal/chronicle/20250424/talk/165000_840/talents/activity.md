After Titus you stood in the tiring-house door. Margery Pell said the butchers' men had clapped the cook. The property hand stayed a bladder until Saturday. The hired men were ten shillings apiece, and you did not multiply it. Raye had the baskets. A boy went to St Bride's and brought back Gilbert's answer: he says aye, and you owe him a cup. You did not go to the Bell. Gilbert was not there.
