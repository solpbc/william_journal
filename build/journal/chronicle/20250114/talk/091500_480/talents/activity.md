You rehearsed at the Cross Keys and would not come off the afternoon. Garnish held the count at six entrances and did not read the scene. You denied the throat once. Kemp mentioned it once, and said he is first on any paper that lists the company. Heminges said Edith's eight shillings waits till Lady Day, and that the Queen's twenty pound is not had and not yours. Nothing was paid. Phillips told Sam to breathe before the word. The winter doublet is too tight. You played at two.
