Mistress Ansell asked if the king was still dying, and you said the second act is shut and that the third was begun yesterday on half a sheet, and not told at this table. The loaf was a penny and a finger and a half short of the notch, and not put on a pan. Board was threepence because you sat. She told you not to defend the trade, to answer in Anne's order, and to send the seamstress's part of the sleeve. You left the barn as the line that needs an answer. She gave no verdict on a king she had not been told.
