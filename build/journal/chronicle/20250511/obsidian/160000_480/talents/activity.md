You wrote home on a clean sheet and did not defend the trade. You answered in her order: the children equally, that you are well and eating, the loaf at a penny and a finger and a half short, and the chamber still yours. At the bottom you sent the seamstress's part of Burbage's sleeve. You left the barn as a line for her to answer. You named no house and no illness. The sheet is to go on Thursday with the thimble.
