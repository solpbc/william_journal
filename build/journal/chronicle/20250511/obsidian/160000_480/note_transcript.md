Anne.

I will take the house's questions in the order you asked them. I will not fill the sheet with a defence of the trade.

The children are as I last had word, and I have no newer word of one than of another.
I am well. I eat at the house.
The loaf is a penny, and a finger and a half short of the notch.
The chamber is still mine.

Burbage split a seam at the elbow in a gesture he holds essential. The seamstress had him make it again, slowly, and showed with her two hands how much cloth it takes. He made it a third time, smaller, and liked it better. She told me a change of direction is cheaper than a change of sleeve, if a man tells her before the cutting.

Whether the Sadlers' cousin still means to build his barn this summer.

From London, this 11 of May, 1595.

Thy Will.
