You were carried on in a furred gown with the beard chalked white, and you spoke this England round the clove. It went out to the empty galleries and came back. You were washed of the chalk and stood at the door for the rest. The Master's cut showed like a drawn tooth. The parting in the street held: off at Flint a king, on in a London street a prisoner, and between them plume-pluckt Richard. Burbage loses a crown behind a curtain every day. You called it the law. He called it a hole. Nick's Queen was exact in the gown let down the two fingers since Candlemas. On the highest line there was a grain that was not there at Easter, and you called it a grain. Phillips looked up from tuning, and down, and would not name it. In the garden the Queen stepped into the shadow of the trees to overhear, as a girl in Warwickshire held she must. Pope's gardener got the only laugh, from the hired men. Kemp, with an apple, said it wants a dog. Over the water it is Faustus. You stayed.
