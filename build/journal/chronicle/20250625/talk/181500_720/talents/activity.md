She had heard no walking for a fortnight, and she asked what you had done. You told the house the whole, since it is finished: a king who thinks the name is the thing, a cousin wronged who comes back and takes everything, a crown handed across, a prison, a coffin. She heard it with her knife down. The cousin reigns, uneasily. She called that indigestion, and a bad example, and she would not show it this month of all months. Verbeke asked who speaks at the end. A groom, and the man who had him killed. Alse asked why he broke the glass. It told him the truth, and she said it was a good glass and he should have kept it. The fellows have not heard it. Kemp was not in the room.
