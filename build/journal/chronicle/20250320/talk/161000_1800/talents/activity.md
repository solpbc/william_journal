In the tiring-house, with the baskets still roped from the Cross Keys, Heminges paid Edith eight shillings out of the stock's share, five days before Lady Day. He said the stock paid a debt, which is not the same as a man drawing his share. Edith folded her account before she took the compliment on the January sleeve. He said the prince's sleeves are paid for at last. You struck the owing line. For Easter she is to let out the Princess's gown and let down Arthur's hems. Nothing came from your purse.
