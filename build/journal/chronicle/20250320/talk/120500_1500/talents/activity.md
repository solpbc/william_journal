Hobbins brought six horses and your mother's hose. You gave him four lines and twopence. You leave on Wednesday the twenty-sixth, by Oxford, and you look to be at the bridge on Friday by dusk. He leaves Saturday and is there two days before you. Gilbert said they would have time to sweep, and he sent a paper of pins, not a parcel and not at sixpence. You kept sixty shillings in the canvas. Hobbins gave the road: drying in this wind, foul beyond Wycombe, the river up at Shipston, twelve hours to Wycombe at his pace, and nine on the bay. The long letter did not go.
