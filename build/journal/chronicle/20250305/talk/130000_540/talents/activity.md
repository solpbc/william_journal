At the cold Theatre Ralph Raye showed a Rose bill for Seleo and Olympo and said the flag was up over the Bankside. He would not give a figure from a book he did not see. Kemp said you stop like Christians while they take three pound. Heminges said the Mayor counts which house played in Lent, and that a season may have a name without every tradesman stopping at once. You said Henslowe will be shut within a fortnight. You did not cross the river, and you named no total in your own book.
