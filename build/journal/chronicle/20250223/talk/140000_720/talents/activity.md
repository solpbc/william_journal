After dinner Mistress Ansell was one spoon short. She accused nobody, and she wondered which caller had a taste for pewter. You asked which caller could make a profit from one. Alse found it among the ink-things, black to the neck, used for the galls and the copperas. Mistress Ansell said it had been put there to be found. She set it on the board beside the ink-pot. She said the house had an ordinary quarrel, and she did not make it into Friday.
