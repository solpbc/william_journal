On the Moorfields path, with the wind behind you, you tried the names in your mouth. Romeus, then Romeo in three beats that open at the end. Juliet, Montague, Capulet, Capel. Mercutio is a cold hand in Brooke and nothing else, and you said a man could be made out of a cold hand. A laundress's cart passed. Tybalt is a cat's name. You stopped. The king at home, with his uncles, will not wait. You spoke no line of any play.
