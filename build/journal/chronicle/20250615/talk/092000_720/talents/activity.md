You stood Trinity Sunday at St Helen's. Halberds were at the Mayor's pew, and they had not been there on Whitsunday. You did not count them. Sir John sat behind them and did not speak. Master Thwaite preached that the powers that be are ordained of God, that masters are to keep their servants in, and whoso resisteth. He turned the glass once and set it down. Verbeke came with the house, because not to come would be noticed. Mistress Ansell took you out by your own door.
