Before church you sat at the board. The loaf was still a thumb short of the notch, and still a penny. Yesterday's butter had been fourpence, where the constable stood, and Mistress Ansell would not have it improved. Verbeke was coming to church with the house, because not to come would be noticed. If he were asked, the answer was Norwich. You did not spend that sentence where nobody had asked it. You carried no book to the pew.
