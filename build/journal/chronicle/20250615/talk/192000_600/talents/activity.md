After dark a neighbour stood on the step and would not come in. He had heard that apprentices went against the Counter in the Poultry and took the prisoners out. He had not seen it. Verbeke had been at his loom. Mistress Ansell kept the bar in her hand. She said yesterday butter, today a prison, and that they had left off asking. You said yesterday's butter was fourpence. She sent the neighbour away, barred the door, and sent you up on one candle.
