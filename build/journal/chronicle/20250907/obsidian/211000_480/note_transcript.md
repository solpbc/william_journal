Sunday night.

The square fills, and empties, and fills. The friend is already gone off the page, since yesterday, and the street does not know it yet. The Prince comes in. He has the square. He does not have a speech from me tonight.

I will not write him a sermon. The noise has stopped. What comes in after a stopped noise is a man with the ground to himself, and that is enough for this leaf.

Phillips is not on it. I am not telling him from this page.
