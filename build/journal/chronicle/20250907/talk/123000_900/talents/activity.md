It was thou on the stair, the first sit-down at Blackfriars since Whitsun. Field asked for his Holinshed, the second volume, and you told him a cat keeps it. He said that is not what a book is for. Jacqueline asked after your wife as one householder after another, and did not translate the pottage. You gave the streets. When the great house by the chapel went by, he looked up and did not ask.
