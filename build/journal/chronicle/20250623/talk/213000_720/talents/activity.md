Kemp and Phillips had walked you home from the play and stayed. The parish fire was in the street, birch over the doors, bread and ale at the better houses. The watch was doubled and went by in harness, and the constables had their eyes on every knot of boys. Nobody leapt. Phillips heard a rhythm in the bells and tapped it on the doorpost. Kemp pointed out a boy supervising grown men who did not know they were supervised. Mistress Ansell stayed twenty minutes and took Alse in. Verbeke stayed at the door of the close. It was warm and still. You did not tell Kemp about the king.
