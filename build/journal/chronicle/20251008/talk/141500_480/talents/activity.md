Richard Cowley saw the cheek and offered to carry the old Roman, as he had in January. You refused. John said the tooth had been at you since the ninth of September, four weeks as of yesterday, and that twelve pence at a barber would be cheaper than a mumbled king. He did not send you. Humphrey had read Crack Me on the Rose bill and had not crossed. You kept the gown, and you were for the fourth act again.
