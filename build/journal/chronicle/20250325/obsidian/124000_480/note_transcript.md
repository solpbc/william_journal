Lady Day. the xxv of Marche 1595.

Item pd to Mrs Ansell, the quarter's chamber, by noone ...... xx s
Item a horne of her inke ................................... ij d
Item a quire of paper ...................................... v d
Told in the coffer this day ......................... xxvij li ij s

Whereof lx s in canvas, and v li for the road and my fathers shops, into the belt.
Remayning under both locks .... xix li ij s

The firing sixpence ends this day until Michaelmas.
The long page stays on the board, face down. It is not in the belt.
