You ruled the line and headed the page 1595. Under it, twenty shillings, a horn of her ink at twopence, a quire at fivepence, and the coffer told at twenty-seven pounds and two shillings. The sevenpence is on the page. It does not change the told sum. The canvas and five pounds go into the belt. Nineteen pounds and two shillings stay under both locks. The long page stays on the board. The sixpence ends until Michaelmas.
