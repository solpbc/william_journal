You paid twenty shillings before noon and she counted them twice. The year has changed and the room has not. Constancy, she said, is one of its virtues. The receipt is in her husband's law-hand. The firing sixpence ends until Michaelmas. She will not let the chamber to a Lenten preacher. After the rent the coffer is twenty-seven pounds and two shillings. The canvas and five pounds go into the belt. Nineteen pounds and two shillings stay under both locks. No shop was paid.
