After a thin Errors, Sly read out a cousin's letter three weeks old. At a place in Monaghan, Tyrone's men had mauled Bagenal's column two days running. Sly did not believe the numbers, and you wrote none down. George said a relief column is the worst place God made, and he kept the cousin's letter apart from what strangers had added. There was talk that Tyrone is to be proclaimed traitor, and talk of pressing men for Ireland before harvest. The cousin said Tyrone was trained in the English manner. Raye said the Rose had a new play, a second part of Caesar, and he brought no receipt. Richard said Rome sells. You said nothing of the unbound Plutarch at home.
