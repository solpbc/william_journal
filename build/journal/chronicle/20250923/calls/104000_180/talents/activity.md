My lord of Southampton called from Holborn, where he is for two nights, with Florio frowning at his elbow. He called poems left for players a desertion. You told him the players pay by the week, and that you do not forget his honour. He is to run at the tilt, and he asked you to come and see him unhorsed. The court is certain he is in love. He asked for fourteen lines to send with a pair of gloves, for a lady he did not name. She mocks him. You told him of the friar. Florio's horse would not wait. You gave no verse, and you promised none.
