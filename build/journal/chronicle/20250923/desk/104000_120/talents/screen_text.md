Master Shakespeare? Southampton. From Holborn, where I am for two nights, with Florio at my elbow frowning at me.
My lord.
They tell me you've left off poems for players. Is it so? I call that a desertion.
The players pay by the week, my lord. A poem is paid once, in honour, which I have had of your lordship and do not forget.
