At the Jackdaw, Ralph Raye brought the Rose news with the dinner. Henslowe has paid seven pounds and two shillings for a throne in the heavens, a chair that comes down out of the roof with a god in it. Yesterday's new play was The Seven Days of the Week, at three pounds ten. You said a glass at four shillings comes down by itself. James Burbage showed the jam with a knife and a crust: the rope will not take a neat turn where it meets the block. Nothing was built, and nobody crossed the river.
