Easter term ended. The almanac lay open on the property-chest. Heminges wanted the comedy for Whit Monday and you gave it, Monday the ninth. He asked for a Labour's Won at Michaelmas, with the same four and the weddings. You said you have a king. He asked how near. You said he is about to be unkinged. Burbage set that for Michaelmas, when the Inns are back. Three weeks of the old stock run from the ninth to Midsummer.
