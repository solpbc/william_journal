Bryan asked whether a play of a king put down will pass the Master. You said it is in the chronicle. Kemp asked what part he has in the king. You said the parts are not cast. Heminges asked how much of the summer's takings may be kept back if playing grows uncertain. Nobody liked the question, and everybody agreed, and nobody named a sum. Trinity term opens on the twentieth, eighteen days off. Heminges shut the almanac on that map.
