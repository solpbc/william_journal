## 00:00 · Fifteen barges

From the slope of Tower Hill, late morning, between the backs of the crowd. Fifteen barges row up on the flood below the Tower, close together, streamers and painted banners over them, trumpeters in the bows. White gun-smoke hangs low on the grey water. Wherries sit in the wake. At the left edge, a big man in a russet cloak, his back to the hill, leaning toward the gentleman in the lead boat as that gentleman steps out. The man's face is not turned. No one looks toward the picture. Richard is the man in the russet cloak.
