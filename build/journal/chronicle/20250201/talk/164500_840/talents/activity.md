After Errors, John set next week and would not put a share on the leaf or open a coffer. Monday Titus, you. Tuesday a rehearsal at nine and Fair Em, you. Wednesday Titus, not you. Thursday a rehearsal at nine and the Seven Deadly Sins, you. Friday Fair Em, not you. Saturday the Seven Deadly Sins, you. The Rose today was the first Caesar. Edith's eight shillings is still owing till Lady Day. The Queen's twenty pound is still not had. The boys' three shillings were not paid. The gown was not bought. The packet is not written, and you set nothing beside the horn. You still do not know the day.
