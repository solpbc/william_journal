The sleet had gone and the morning was hard and bright. Heminges saw both heels and laid out new hose for the whole company, twenty-six shillings out of the stock. Kemp presented his feet and dared a hole to be found. Pope produced the old pair, heel open to a fist, and was satisfied that Greenwich had been his. Garnish packed three books and sixty rolls and sat on the chest. Bryan gave the order of going, and forbade a first word to a yeoman and a hand on a hanging. Sam asked if the Queen is truly there or if it is the chair again. You told him the court went up to Richmond this morning. Nick was there and did not sing. Phillips's four days still held, and Phillips was not in the room.
