You told Mistress Ansell the hour, which is first light, before you go. She had yesterday's news that you go, and not the hour. Alse said it aloud and did not write it. The chamber will be aired, the coffer unvisited, and Gib kept off the lord's book. The chronicle and the pages under the board stay. The twenty shillings are already in her book. You did not count the coffer, and you did not pay for the latch.
