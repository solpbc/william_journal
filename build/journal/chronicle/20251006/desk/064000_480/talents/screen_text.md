## 00:00 · The sheet before the lines

Iul.

## 04:00 · Eleven lines, and the night not come

Come, heavie day, be done, and spend thy light. / My window hath no use for thee till darke. / The clocke is honest, and I would it lied, / And strooke the evening while the streete is noone. / Night, thou art late. I have a word to give / That will not keepe untill thou reach the staire. / Bring him in quiet, and take the lanternes too. / The house is all my mother's till thou come. / I have set the casement, and the boord is cleare. / Be darke enough, and I will do the rest. / A girl is waiting, and the night is not.
