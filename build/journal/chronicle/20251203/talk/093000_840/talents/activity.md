You ran the tragedy in apparel on a frosted stage. Nick came in low, and Burbage kept his eyes on him. You told Pope to leave something of the Nurse for Saturday. For the vault you set the tiring-house door as a mouth and a trestle as a bier, and you said to light them when he says light, and not before. Garnish heard the cressets already up. Burbage heard the fire on the word. You wrote neither as the keeper. Garnish timed it at two hours and fifty minutes. James Burbage said there is a great stone hall in the Blackfriars that would hold a winter, and named no price. Kemp reported that by Henslowe's book Monday was the thirty-first of November.
