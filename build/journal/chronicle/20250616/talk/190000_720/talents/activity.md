Alse came back from the Cheapside conduit with the buckets half full. She had seen a press of apprentices and masterless men in Paul's churchyard at noon, then officers, then running. A woman at the conduit had said the armourers' shops, and another had said Ludgate. You told her to keep what she saw apart from what she heard. You said you had meant to check Daniel's third book at Paul's against a better copy, and that you were not going. Mistress Ansell ruled that nobody in the house goes west of the Stocks again this month, the lodger included. The loaf was still a thumb short of the notch, and still a penny.
