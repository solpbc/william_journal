Upstairs at Field's you had the morning's errands already in your hand. Both shoes new-soled and a heel-piece, ninepence. A little book of fables with woodcuts, fourpence, for the girl who asked in April for no riddles. A paper of London pins, twopence. Field had word by the same carrier that his kin are untouched. He asked if you would walk, a man with a share in a playhouse. You said the horse was dear. He said the horse is always dear, and told you to leave his Holinshed. You said it lies under a cloth, and Mistress Ansell has her eye on it. It stays.
