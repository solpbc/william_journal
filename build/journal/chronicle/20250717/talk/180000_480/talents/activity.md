Anne's sheet: the hay is in and light, the barley stands short, the house is busy. Hamnet has a jay's feather by the Guild Pits and will not say where. Judith has a third ballad by ear. Susanna wrote that who should be whom, and that a queen would hear the gardeners only if she hid, for servants say everything, but not to your face. You laughed with your shoulders. You have nothing to send but a line of thanks. The old letter is still not going.
