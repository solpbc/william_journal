## 00:00 · The glass

Will? John Heminges. From Aldermanbury, with the book open.
John. I am at the Theatre. The book, then.
Yesterday's boxes were the best since Whitsun. Bankside has found the way to Shoreditch while the Rose is dark. Saturday's share will be seventeen shillings, or near it.
Seventeen, or near it. That is Saturday's figure. What is the matter?
