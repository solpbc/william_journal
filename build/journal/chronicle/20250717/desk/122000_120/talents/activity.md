Heminges called with the book open. Yesterday's boxes were the best since Whitsun, and Saturday's share will be seventeen shillings or near it. He proposed half wages for August, five shillings a week for five hired men, five pound for the month, out of the stock. You said pay it. Asked for the Labour's Won at Michaelmas, you said the thirty-first of April, and a king at Michaelmas, cut to the Master's measure. His knees are bad. Nick has had new shoes twice since Easter.
