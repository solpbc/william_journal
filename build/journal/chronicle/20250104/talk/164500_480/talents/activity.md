After Two Gentlemen, Heminges would not give a figure. The gallery is not all in, and he will not strike a share, because Monday still plays and you are the old man again. The holiday boxes wait. Burbage repeated Mistress Ansell's sentence from the close. Kemp kept it as his own: the Mayor will feast a mock-prince and shut the door on a player. The Queen's twenty pounds stayed off the cloth. You will line your own payments at home, and you will not open the coffer.
