You stood at the mouth of the close, a hundred yards off, among citizens' backs. Sir John, a furred gown on the steps, received a law-student king with a straight face and did not speak. Mistress Ansell said he will dine a play-prince at his table and shut the door on a player, and that that is the City. You and Burbage laughed over the Night of Errors. Burbage looked at how the king sat a hired horse. Torch-smoke and the brass of the trumpets hung over the porch. You did not go in.
