Richard Field called from the shop. He had Millington's little book in his hand, and he had heard you saw it. He called it honest presswork on a dishonest copy, and he asked you to be angry at his table on Sunday and not in Paul's. You said you had been curious whose it was, not angry. He said the man who entered it. You said the plays are not yours to print. They are the company's. He asked for Sunday and for the Holinshed. You said Sunday, and not the Holinshed.
