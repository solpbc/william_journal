After the children were in bed you sat with Anne on the low chamomile bench and talked of ordinary things. Susanna's handwriting had been set to a bill for Joan, and it was ruled. The twins' school-shoes were through at both toes. You said shoes could go in the Christmas pack if Anne sent the size, and she said she would not have you guess a kindness from London. Joan will marry when she means to. Anne had been told so in the kitchen. You stayed a little longer because the bench smelled of chamomile.
