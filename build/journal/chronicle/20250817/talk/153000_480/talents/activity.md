In the orchard you gave Edmund two cues and nothing else. Wednesday had been a name, on the floor. Today was the line. He galloped the boy's line about love's light wings, and Susanna said Hamnet had had it on Thursday. You made him wait until she had said the girl's line, and then come in under it. He mentioned Nick. You told him Nick is bound, and that nobody has asked for him. The bargain was two lines, the shop, and the work he already has. He said he would read aloud and stay, and that he did not like it. You did not ask him to like it. The rest of the argument you left for the table.
