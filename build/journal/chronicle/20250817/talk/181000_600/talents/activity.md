Supper was ten at the table, and a cold pie. Judith asked for a ballad with a murder in the Christmas pack. Susanna asked for the answer on too quick, written, so she could keep it. You told her she was right and that you had not mended the girl. Hamnet asked if a player's sword is sharp. You told him it is no sharper than the lath one, and a deal heavier. Edmund asked to come. You told him to finish school, and when he said you had not, you said to look at you, walking home at thirty-one. You said nothing is arranged for him in London, and that admiring a play is not a living. Joan said you might take the back and leave the rest. Your father raised the cup to your lord, and put the twenty shillings and the spine in the same sentence, and the table let it pass for a jest. Your mother put a jar of the bay grease in the pack and did not speak. Richard was there and did not speak. Anne was there and did not speak.
