Hobbins looked in after dinner and set the morning at six, at the bridge. Six horses and a spare. You are to ride the spare as far as Shipston, ten miles, and carry nothing, and he would not hear the back argue. The pack and the saddle are two shillings, to be paid at the Bell. Richard has the pack by the stair. Anne said you had packed before the evening could arrange itself. The inkhorn was not in the pack.
