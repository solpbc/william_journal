## 00:00 · The glass

Master Shakespeare? Cuthbert Burbage. From Holywell.
Master Burbage. I hear you. What is the matter?
My father was up half the night, walking round the playhouse with a lantern. The matter is wood. A timber house in the fields, and a city in this temper.
Two hired men are to sleep in the tiring-house from tonight, at sixpence each. The yard gates are to be chained after the play.
