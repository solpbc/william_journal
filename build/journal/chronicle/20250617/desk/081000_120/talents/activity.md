Cuthbert Burbage called from Holywell. His father had walked the playhouse half the night with a lantern. Two hired men are to sleep in the tiring-house from tonight at sixpence each, and the yard gates are to be chained after the play. Cuthbert would have had the flag down on a trouble day, since a white swan is a thing to throw at. You said keep it up, because a house that hides looks guilty. He said that was his father's opinion. You said it was Heminges's first. He called it three to one.
