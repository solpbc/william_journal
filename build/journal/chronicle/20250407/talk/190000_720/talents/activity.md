After supper each child asked one thing from London. Susanna wanted a book with no riddles. Judith wanted a song nobody in Stratford has. Hamnet asked whether the sword was ever in a real fight, and you told him it had killed the same man four times a year since before he was born. He was satisfied. Edmund asked to come to London and was told that fifteen is for school. Your father sang half a catch, the words he had, and forgot the rest. Anne sent them up. Mary, Joan, and Richard were in the room and did not speak. Seven voices, and no more.
