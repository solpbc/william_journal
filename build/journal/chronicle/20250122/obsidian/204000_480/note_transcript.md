Titus at two. I was in it. I am not going to write the afternoon down as if it had been the morning.

The morning was Paul's. Three stalls, and nothing bought. Venus moves. Lucrece is respected. He has scarce a dozen left, and the lawyers come tomorrow. He asked for a third poem. I told him a comedy. He heard an illness. I will not cure it with a title.

The Walk was a wedding at Greenwich on Sunday. I left it as news. I wrote it down so I would not pretend I had not heard it, and I am not taking it any further than that.

Abel has the book. It is not licensed. No one has a part. The letter is still on her table. I had a free morning. I am not spending the night on a question I have already refused to answer badly.
