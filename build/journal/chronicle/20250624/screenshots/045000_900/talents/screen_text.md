## 00:00 · The dressed close

A narrow close, seen from just outside a doorway under a jettied storey, at a standing man's eye. Every doorway is dressed: green birch over the lintels, fennel and St John's wort, and on one step an earthenware pot of tall white lilies. Over the nearest door a small lamp in a bracket is still burning, the flame pale in the daylight. At the end of the close the church porch stands with nobody in it. A little ash has blown into the kennel. Sparrows in the birch. No people, and no shadow of the man in the doorway.
