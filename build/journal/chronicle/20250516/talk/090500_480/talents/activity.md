Pope had not landed Buckingham's speech when Burbage began the King's answer. You asked him to wait, and not to announce the wait. It was gone nine, and you had them do it twice. Burbage made the pause so large that the waiting itself commanded the stage. The second time, his answer was smaller than the shout he had ready. Pope said the empty yard took it. You said the empty yard changed more than it did under the louder reading. For a moment the King heard something he did not expect. You left the note to be written before you left the house.
