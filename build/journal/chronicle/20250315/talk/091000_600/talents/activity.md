You waited in the outer passage with a tailor and two trumpeters, and you did not see past it into the palace. The clerk read the three names as servants to the Lord Chamberlain, dated the warrant the fifteenth of March 1594, and counted twenty pound in angels and silver. He looked at you. Kemp took the bag. The tide had turned. He would not pay double oars, and no second fare was named. You walked by the Strand and Holborn.
