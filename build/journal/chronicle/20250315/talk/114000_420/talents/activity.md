Heminges had the twenty pound on the cloth and counted it to twenty. Costs were sixteen shillings for the boat and the baskets, thirty-four shillings in rewards, and fifty shillings to the Cheapside mercer, five pound in all. That left fifteen pound, and fifteen pound in eight parts is thirty-seven shillings and sixpence, the eighth part being the stock. Edith's eight shillings is out of that stock on Thursday. Kemp was talked down from dividing the Queen's reward by desert. Augustine Phillips offered him a new jig for Easter, with the slippers, and he took the share. He was first on the paper, and he said so three times. You mean to put your part on a hired horse. George Bryan was in the room and did not speak.
