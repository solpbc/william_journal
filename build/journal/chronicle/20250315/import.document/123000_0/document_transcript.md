TREASURER OF HER MAJESTY’S CHAMBER - WARRANT AND
RECKONING
Dated at Whitehall, xvto Marcij 1594 (15 March 1594/5)
Warrant of payment unto William Kempe, William Shakespeare, and Richard
Burbage, servants to the Lord Chamberlain, upon the Council’s warrant dated at
Whitehall the fifteenth day of March 1594, for two several comedies or
interludes showed by them before Her Majesty in Christmas time last past at
Greenwich, namely upon St Stephen’s Day (26 December) and Innocents’ Day (28
December).
Sum allowed by the Chamber:
- For the two comedies at Greenwich: 13 pounds, 6 shillings, 8 pence.
- By way of Her Majesty’s special reward: 6 pounds, 13 shillings, 4 pence.
- Total received in coin (sixteen angels, four sovereigns, and eight pounds
 in white money): 20 pounds.
Company disbursement cast by John Heminges at James Burbage’s house in
Holywell:
- Boat-hire to Greenwich and return across two days, with carriage of the
 costume baskets: 16 shillings.
- Rewards to five hired men at four shillings each, three boy players at two
 shillings each, and the book-keeper and tireman at four shillings each: 34
 shillings.
- Debt discharged to the mercer in Cheapside for cloth taken up before
 Christmas: 50 shillings.
- Remainder divided into eight equal parts (seven sharers and one part to
 the company stock): 15 pounds, being 37 shillings and 6 pence per part.
 Out of the stock part, 8 shillings set aside for Mistress Edith
 Kettlewood, payable Thursday.
Entered and acknowledged at Holywell, 15 March 1595. Acquittance sealed by
John Heminges on behalf of the fellowship.
