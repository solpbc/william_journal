# Week of Jan 05, 2025

You began the week at church and ended it on the stair, well. On Sunday you lost the sermon on the unjust steward and kept one sentence about goods not yet in the hand. Mistress Ansell named a servant of Crosby Place and denied that she had lowered her voice. You struck a letter to Anne, dated the fifth of January 1594. You did not send it. In a lane off Bishopsgate a pavan was played twice, the second time faster. You did not go in. You do not know the house. A cross sits by the fifth in the almanac. No word sits beside it.

Monday you played to a holiday yard. At Holywell the bean made Nick king for the first time. Kemp lasted about half a paternoster. James said twenty-one years, and two to run, and stopped there. Heminges gave the holiday boxes as eleven pound eight shillings to the sharers' side, without a drum or a trumpet, and then lost a slice for saying Item. That sum is not your share. At the stair Mistress Ansell stood over you while you ate a slice of her own cake. Tuesday you took the second father in the Shrew and left the new page where the names fail. Wednesday, under a half moon, you brought a messenger in black. Thursday you were not in the afternoon Shrew. Two students at the parlour door called the company base and common. Your legs say one day. You promised Burbage the act for Saturday. You did not enter the hall.

Friday you read two titles and no authors, and you do not know what the Rose took. You wrote Edith viij s. owing, Lady Day. You did not pay it. It is not your debt. The Queen's twenty pounds is still not yours and not had. You did not count the coffer. Saturday you played Egeon again and read the act at the Jackdaw as far as it goes. The reckoning had no new share. Burbage was silent a full minute, and then the year was too long for a play. The objection is Berowne's. You did not strike it. The half-line is still empty. There is no song and no last line. On the way home you sneezed twice. The latch sprang when you lifted it softly. You are not ill.
