# Week of Oct 26, 2025

You began the week with a letter for the carrier of the thirteenth. At church the sword went before Sir John for the last Sunday, and the loaf stayed a penny and a thumb short. In the afternoon you struck a supper-story out of the page for Anne, thanked Hamnet for the brook at Shottery, the dam down, and sent the word likely.

Monday a lent Chaucer came to the door. You stood at the Knight's Tale until nine, and wrote one line about a duke and an old moon. Tuesday you read an Italian title at the Bull and went back to the desk. At dinner the loaf was two fingers and more short, once, and she called the answer sound doctrine. By candle you wrote one page. The friar's letter has miscarried.

Wednesday you kept one line from the river and played Egeon. Thursday the mornings began, and you played Gaunt on a whole jaw. Friday you played an old man in Titus. Saturday was one glass, thirty-five shillings received, and supper on the bells and the ink. The one page is still one page. Christmas is still written as expected.
