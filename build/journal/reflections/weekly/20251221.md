# Week of Dec 21, 2025

You stood at a door while meal and halfpence went out by name, and you put two shillings in a church box. A goose came with a second shirt attached to it. Field asked nothing about the second volume, which was worse than an asking. In the dark you thought of where the Prince stands. You paid the Christmas quarter three days early. The latch shut the first time, and the mending was hers.

You lashed a canvas in the sleet and heard three stories. You read the Rose's title and stayed for Errors. Errors went to a thin wet yard, the last at that house for the year, and by dusk she was waiting until tomorrow. On Tuesday the heels were shown, twenty-six shillings went out of the stock, and a boy asked whether the Queen was the chair. You left the chronicle on the shelf and the pages of the wood under the board. Before light on Wednesday you kept nine shillings out and counted the rest at thirty-three pounds. You went up on the flood with Gannet at the stern. Christmas was a strange table, three low notes, and a run you showed with your feet.

On Friday you went in first on the old line. She laughed once. A hand lifted for three lines over what was set down. The owl held the hall until the chair stirred. My lord clapped your shoulder and went out. You refused to write the night down as paid. On Saturday the lovers were ten days short of a month. A boy stayed under the note and held a dagger, and Burbage kept his eyes on him. Another boy searched the rushes for half an hour and came up with rosemary. The remark about the button was put away until an inn down the river. You were still in the hall.
