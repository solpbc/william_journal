# Week of Sep 07, 2025

You stood under the Queen's bells at St Helen's. Mistress Ansell put the ringing down as ale, and as a charge. Alse asked whether every noise must be a charge, and was told that in this parish it is. At fourteenthly the glass was turned. Seven weeks of the Mayor remain. The sword-bearer stood at the pew's end. He did not speak. At Blackfriars it was thou on the stair, the first sit-down since Whitsun. Field wanted his Holinshed, the second volume. You told him a cat keeps it. He looked up at the great house by the chapel. He did not ask. At night the Prince came into the square on the page. Phillips was not on the leaf.

On Monday the loaf was a full thumb short of the same notch, and still a penny. She would not cut another. You played Fair Em at two. The part was not named. The moon was full enough to find the latch. On Tuesday a crust at the Jackdaw started the left tooth. You pointed, for once, at the place you meant. Agnes offered a clove or a hot knitting-needle, and you chose the clove. You played Egeon out of the right side of your mouth. At supper the nether great tooth was black at the neck. She said it will settle when it is in a dish. You wrote Item, cloves, ij d. You did not open the coffer. The back was not thought of once. On Wednesday you woke at one and at three, and you stayed down. The part in The Shrew was not named. The letter stayed sealed.

On Thursday Burbage would not hold the roll. You gave him the long line and told him half the voice. He sat on the bare boards. He said he would play a strong man at being weak, and Phillips bowed. Nick asked if he means it all, and you said that is the part. Kemp was not called. You played Fair Em again. The part was not named. At the Bell, Hobbins took the letter of the last of August, still sealed, and gave you Anne's. She wants the Michaelmas money when it falls, and not before. You did not open the parcel. On Friday the tables took two lines, cords, and a ladder of rope. You were Egeon again from the right side. At the Hazel, Kemp tried the knot with his fingers, his teeth, a fork, and a speech. You would not name the child. Alice ended it in one stroke. The slip, in Susanna's hand, had nothing on it but whom. On Saturday Cuthbert called from a scrivener's in Cheapside with the Mayor's letter as hearsay. You played Navarre to a good Saturday. The share was thirty-three shillings. Kemp said tour. The room did not agree. Heminges sends to Kettleby tonight. Monday stands as billed. The coffer was not opened. It stands at twenty pound fifteen.
