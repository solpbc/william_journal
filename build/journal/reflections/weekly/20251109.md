# Week of Nov 09, 2025

You stood through morning prayer on Sunday. You did not touch the page. The sheet was still stopped after the dagger, and the candle was cold. Sir John went out of his pew. He did not speak. In the porch Mistress Ansell spoke of the quiet night, and she took your back for Tuesday's stores once the page was done. On Monday she filled the horn from the Monday copper, galls, copperas, and gum, at twopence. You opened Errors as Egeon and were done after the opening. At night you wrote the first fourteen lines last, for your own mouth. You did not rule them. You wrote dignitie into the March gap. You did not go to The Disguises.

On Tuesday you brought the two fathers in, gave the Friar forty lines, and ruled the Prince's couplet at a quarter past ten. The account book says Verona ended that day, and a pound of candles at fourpence. At the stair she asked who was punished, and you said all of them. At Leadenhall you said four shillings for a side of bacon. She would not write the number. You carried the side, the suet, a firkin of salt, onions on a string, and a cheese. You paid nothing toward the stores. You played at two, and you stood at evening prayer. On Wednesday you put fifty leaves into Garnish's hands. One woman, the Lady. The man with the fan is to be headed with his own name. Twenty-odd parts, and three boys. He called the two hours a lie, and you told him a sonnet is allowed one. The company did not read the play. At two you were Gaunt. The Rose had the first Tamburlaine. You did not go. The three cut leaves stayed in his chest.

On Thursday Tilney signed the last leaf and left every line standing. He found nothing that touches the state. Heminges counted out seven shillings and entered them once. You told him, after Tilney had turned away, that you had never been so slighted. You did not put the three leaves back. At the Bell, Gilbert had already gone to St Bride's. Anne's letter was on the stair. The winter wash is set at fourpence a week, already paid. You did not answer her. On Friday you read a Rose title. You did not go. You wrote two maids in the wood and stopped before a scene. Phillips stopped after two measures. You counted sixteen bars, and Burbage and Nick spoke at half voice. The fourteen lines stayed a count in the margin. On Saturday Quiney spoke from the Bell of two hundred houses. You had counted about twenty from a ladder in August. He asked a supper and left the day open. The two pounds stayed out. At the reckoning Heminges set the next week. He did not read a sum. You did not answer Anne's letter. The coffer was not counted.
