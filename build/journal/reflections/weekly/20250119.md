# Week of Jan 19, 2025

You began the week in bed, and you ended it with a book that had been signed and a week that had been set. On Sunday Mistress Ansell brought the sermon home with the posset, and Gilbert brought Anne's letter from the Bell. The children want shoes in one month. Your father's cough is mild. She would know whether you mean to come at Lent. You did not answer. You struck twenty lines and wrote a rougher pair for a tall boy. By night the fever was gone.

On Monday you were well and you stayed in. The Shrew and Ranger's Comedy went on, and you stayed in. You tried a spring note and stopped. On Tuesday, before dinner, you finished the two notes and shut the comedy on one prose line, and you kept the plain refusal. After Richard the Third, Garnish found Katharine and Rosaline with the same lover, and you chose. Rosaline keeps Berowne. Katharine keeps Dumaine. Heminges wrote one dull line: Navarre ended this day. On Wednesday you read at three stalls and heard that the Venus moves and the Lucrece is respected. The Walk was a wedding at Greenwich on Sunday. You left it as news, and you played Titus at two.

On Thursday you stood in Westminster Hall while a shortened grievance was said again at greater length. In the afternoon you were not cast. Margery had thirty-three shillings on the stair. On Friday Tilney struck two oaths and signed, and Heminges counted seven shillings of the company's onto his table. On Saturday you played Two Gents, and the reckoning set next week. You gave out no part, and you entered no share. You did not answer the letter. Nothing went to the Bell. Edith's eight shillings is still owing till Lady Day. The coffer was not counted.
