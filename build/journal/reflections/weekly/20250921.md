# Week of Sep 21, 2025

You put the clove in and went to church. Master Thwaite reached fourteenthly, and you lost the thread of what he was saying. Sir John sat in his pew. He did not speak to you. You brought no sentence home. At night you wrote one blow and struck the line that explained the boy in the middle. You left the foot of the leaf empty. The name had Sunday off for church. You are not at ease in the name.

On Monday Gannet had Raleigh home in a ship so deep in gold she would hardly steer. He had told you the first half in February, and he had the rest from a lighter. Paul's had it smaller. The phrase was with little riches. Nobody there had spoken with a man off the ship. Drayton had looked for his own name on the Cambridge leaf first. Eight months, he said, and a story. You said it was a living. You paid fivepence for a blank quire. You were at the Theatre by ten with the left side of your face in a scarf. Kemp spent the name. You played the King of Navarre at two. The loaf was a penny and a full thumb short. Board was threepence because you sat to it. You did not open the coffer.

On Tuesday you wrote one word under the blow. You did not start the speech around it. The moon was drawn new on the almanac. You copied nothing off it. My lord of Southampton called from Holborn, where he is for two nights. He asked for fourteen lines to send with a pair of gloves, for a lady. You told him the players pay by the week, and you told him of the friar. You gave no verse, and you promised none. You played Egeon. George Bryan brought a Carey groom's talk: Mistress Vernon, too much familiarity, and the court says so. You did not better it. At night you said he was happy. Not a leaf in the drawer would fit. You wrote nothing.

On Wednesday the word was still the one word. You played the afternoon. The part was not named. You were dead by the third act and out of the gown by four. On Thursday morning you rehearsed. John called from Aldermanbury. The crown is new gilt at four shillings. Nick has grown two fingers since Candlemas, and the gown is let down for eighteenpence. You said the tooth is a tooth, and after Thursday. Asked for Love's Labour's Won, you said when this king is buried. The jaw was hot to the hand at night.

On Friday he laid the pearwood glass on the throne, face up. Nobody asked. You spoke this England round a clove, and you stood the rest at the door. The king goes off at Flint a king and comes on in a London street a prisoner. You called the curtain the law. He called it a hole. On Nick's highest line there was a grain that was not there at Easter, and you called it a grain. Phillips looked up, and down. Kemp said it wants a dog. Over the water it was Faustus, and you stayed. On Saturday you struck eleven lines and left her one turn. The margin says trust the boy. At five the share was thirty-four shillings, the best since Whitsun. You entered it beside twenty-nine. You did not open the coffer. It stands at twenty pound fifteen.
