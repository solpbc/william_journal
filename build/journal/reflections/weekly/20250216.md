# Week of Feb 16, 2025

You began the week at St Helen's, where Master Thwaite named Lent and turned the glass twice. Sir John Spencer did not speak. At the lodging the penny loaf was a finger short, the sixpence for firing and washing was entered, and you wrote Anne that you will come before Mid-Lent Sunday. You named no day. The comedy's ladies kept the better of it. You struck a London story out of the letter, marked a pause on the last page, and left the sheet for the carrier of the 27th.

At the Cross Keys the watchet gown went onto Nick. Fifty shillings of it were still owed. Sam kept the notes of the song and lost his place. You told the messenger to come slower and then say nothing. You played on Tuesday and on Saturday. You were not in The Shrew on Monday, Wednesday, or Friday. On Thursday the new comedy was played at two. You went on first in a paper crown. Kemp added eleven lines and Garnish ticked them. The boxes held seven pound eleven shillings, and the yard went out quiet. At supper a young man of the Inner Temple said Southwell had been tried at Westminster that day and condemned. You asked when it was to be. Tomorrow. You were not in that court.

On Friday you did not leave the house, and you did not look at a bill. Three accounts came, and they did not match. At night you refused a likeness between the poet and the play, found no ink in the pen, and stopped. On Saturday the snow thinned the yard. The share was thirty shillings, the best since Christmas. Garnish read the eleven lines off the margin. Heminges asked, half in jest, for a Love's Labour's Won, and you said you have not written it. You counted the coffer by one candle and wrote the share. You set down no sum for the coffer. Edith's eight shillings and the mercer's fifty were still unpaid at the end of the week.
