# Week of Jul 20, 2025

You kept Sunday at St Helen's. The penny loaf was a thumb short, and the price stayed. After dark you fair-copied a keyboard sonnet onto one leaf, and changed one word in the couplet. You said you had gone by the lane, and that the shutter was up.

Monday the fire arrived twice. Gilbert had half the High Street, Henley Street and forty houses, and then twenty and the north end. He stopped before the commendation. At the Bell, Anne's letter put the fire on Tuesday the fifteenth, beyond the Rother Market, about twenty houses, and the house safe. You read it again by candle. On Tuesday you tried the street, two lines of Mab and the feast on the men who will speak them. Nick stopped at her entrance and said she was careful. In the afternoon five men were condemned for levying war. John asked on what you would go, and you said on these.

Wednesday the street cried tomorrow on Tower Hill. You heard it from the stair. A dozen words took Thursday off the board, and the almanac got No play. At supper Mistress Ansell heard the letter, and blamed the thatch. You wrote Anne five lines, Saturday and Monday and the bridge. Thursday you heard the bells. You scratched out a start. You said you had heard the bells, and you said enough. Friday the shoes, the fables and the pins were already in your hand when you climbed Field's stair. The horse was dear. His Holinshed stayed under the cloth. Jacqueline's bread and cheese are for the first day, and a chipped H is for the boy. Saturday you bought a quire for fivepence. The morning had a Spaniard ashore in Cornwall. Before the jig the box had four galleys and four places, and the yard had forty and Plymouth. The house went dark in the book. You walk on Monday. The sheets go where you go.
