# Week of Jun 15, 2025

The week opened at the board, on a loaf still a thumb short and still a penny. At St Helen's, Master Thwaite preached that the powers that be are ordained of God, and he set the glass down after one turning. Halberds stood at the Mayor's door. You did not count them. Sir John did not speak. Verbeke sat with the house, because not to come would have been noticed. That night a neighbour stood on the step and said the Counter in the Poultry had been opened and the prisoners taken out. He had heard it. He had not seen it. Mistress Ansell barred the door. On Monday Alse came home from the Cheapside conduit with the buckets half full. She had seen Paul's churchyard at noon, and she had heard the armourers' shops and Ludgate on the way. You kept the two tellings apart. The house was shut, lodger included, to anything west of the Stocks for the rest of the month. You did not go to Paul's. Daniel's third book stayed the copy you already own. On an older leaf you wrote York naming the rights, and you stopped where the breath stops. The prison and the end were still unwritten.

On Tuesday Cuthbert called from Holywell. Two hired men would sleep in the tiring-house at sixpence each, the gates would be chained after the play, and the flag would stay up, three to one. You crossed out the sentence an alderman could have said, and you kept a list: Pell's two hundred, Verbeke a silkweaver and English, Alse's Ludgate. You said that writing them would make them a crowd, and they were not. In the afternoon you played in Fair Em. On Wednesday the Errors was thin. Sly had a cousin's letter three weeks old: at a place in Monaghan, Tyrone's men had mauled Bagenal's column two days running. You wrote down no number of the dead. George kept the letter apart from what strangers had added. Raye brought a Rose title, a second part of Caesar. He brought no receipt. Richard said Rome sells. You did not name the unbound Plutarch at home.

On Thursday you rehearsed at nine and were a duke in a thin Richard the Third at two. The almanac still printed Corpus Christi, and nobody kept it. The Rose's title was Long Meg. At the window you said the play had been thin and that you were a duke in it. You did not name the play. You told your father's waggons and Herod as a fair he was at, and you named no town. On Friday term opened. Fair Em was thin under the watchmen's eyes, and the Rose, by Raye's account, was thinner. By one candle you wrote the gardeners, thirty-odd lines, and you left the queen's curse on the plants. The deposing was already behind that sheet. On Saturday, a little before ten, you ruled a line under the king. He dies fighting, which you had not known he would. Gib stayed on the chronicle. The account book says the king ended this day and is not yet shown. There is no fool in it. You did not tell him. Mistress Ansell asked if it was the lovers, and you said no. At two you played in Errors. The reckoning named no sum.
