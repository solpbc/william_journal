# Week of Dec 07, 2025

The week began at her table, before the loaf was measured, and the verdict was already prepared. She had gone once. She would not go again. Alse asked the question she had held since four o'clock on Saturday, and you told her the one who dies is fifteen, lodges in Aldermanbury, and ate two pies. In the afternoon Kettleby called from Somerset House. Sir Edward wanted the king whole, in a gentleman's parlour rather than at court. He did not name the guest. You sent four lines to Heminges and wrote the work on your tables. You did not write a reason.

On Monday you changed the orchard cue by two words and left them as a mark, because Saturday's house had not made the entrance safe. Larkham marked the book and both rolls. The three leaves stayed in the chest. The Shrew was an ordinary afternoon. Kemp did not ask for a line. On Tuesday you were Egeon at two. After a clean band, and the last of the flood, you were Gaunt on a floor six paces by four. Burbage gave the crown with his own hands and dashed the glass. Four shillings. The purse was five pounds, in Heminges's hand before the last speech, and the four shillings stayed outside the five. Twelve and six the share. Lady Hoby thanked Nicholas and Arthur by name. Sir Robert said one sentence. On the Strand, at a shut gate, you gave the words back and withheld a verdict. Burbage was only cold.

Wednesday you kept the parlour off the dinner table, and you spoke the prologue again to a yard nearly as full. Thursday, in the dark hour, you said the sentence over, and it stayed between praise and warning. By daylight you refused his face and kept two facts: the Queen was late in the garden, and the groom held. Friday a hall in the Blackfriars still had no price. The public king was still the cut book. After dark you wrote a weaver waking, failed in prose, and gave the speech back to Kemp's mouth. Saturday the thirty-seven lay beside the twelve and six. You left them side by side. You tied the private leaves with tape from the property basket and put them at the bottom of the coffer. You did not name anyone. The lid came down.
