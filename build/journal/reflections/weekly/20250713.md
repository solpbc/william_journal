# Week of Jul 13, 2025

You began with two turns of the glass and a letter still on the nail. At the Bull you stood a shilling supper. Nashe made Harvey immortal, made an alderman out of thumb-rings, and made the night small. At nine the pain was behind the eyes. The tables, once you were in, kept the wagon small.

Monday you were Egeon, and then you waited. At her table the Prince was the only sensible man in the quarrel, and she told you to speak up. The loaf wanted a full thumb. The penny stayed a penny. A knot took four tries, and Alse's took one. Tuesday the mutton was smaller and the sixpence stayed sixpence. Phillips will speak the fairy. Burbage has one word, and it is enough. Wednesday you were in the old play. The loaf stayed where Monday had left it.

Thursday Heminges called with the book open. Yesterday's boxes were the best since Whitsun. You told him to pay the five pound, and you promised a king at Michaelmas rather than the play he asked for. At the Bell the hay was in, the barley short, a feather unexplained, a third ballad, and a whom. You sent a line of thanks. Friday was the feast, in a shirt, with the book open. Two lines of a sonnet, servants above them, a second opening the Nurse breaks, and her short line tried in Nick's voice. You stayed at the lodging. Saturday's share was seventeen shillings. From the boat the Rose flagstaff was bare, and the new timber was wider than the Theatre. Green oak, a goldsmith, a letter from last November, and two numbers of galleys. You stayed in the boat.
