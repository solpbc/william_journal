# Week of May 25, 2025

You began the week with a loaf a finger and a half short of the notch, at a penny, and you ended it with the same measure at the table and two fingers under Prices. Between those two writings the parish walked three marks, Alse showed them to Gillis, and a neighbour said the Mayor might better look to the meal-tubs than the laundry-tubs. The precept against blue starch came into the house on Wednesday, to be read on Sunday next. It was not read this week. Alse's one band, starched blue at Whitsun last, was washed white.

The company was worse than the parish and then a little less worse. On Tuesday nobody had the February book. Cowley dried four times in the same place and apologised to four men. Pope, padded to a barrel, said Sincler needs none. You got eight lines right and were thanked sarcastically. They broke at eleven. On Friday Cowley came through the dry place once and apologised to nobody. The eight lines held. You were not proud of them. The book was not mended.

The king stayed at two-thirds. He came down off the wall on Sunday, and the glass was struck. The queen went as far as a hedge, and then a garden, and the gardener binds apricocks and is not a man with a name. You did not finish the act. You did not write the parliament. You did not show the book to Kemp. Susanna is twelve. The letter that should have gone with the thimble is written, the answer under the question is a blot, and the sheet waits for the fifth of June. At Paul's a play of yours had Pembroke's name and not yours. You did not buy it. Drayton asked. You said it was, and that they paid somebody. On Saturday John would not name a share. The bag was not shut.
