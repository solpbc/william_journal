# Week of Aug 03, 2025

On Sunday nine of you went down Old Town to Holy Trinity. Your father stayed home. The vicar preached the fire as judgment on Sabbath malt, twice in fourteen months. In the porch Quiney took your hand in both of his and asked you as far as his shop, for the town. You heard him. You refused the shop. At dinner Judith gave the sermon in the vicar's voice, and your mother said John's name once. He said the aim was poor. A wheelwright, two weavers and a widow lost their roofs, and the great malt-lofts in the High Street were still dry. The children kept the burned row for Thursday. At dusk you kept the sentence you wrote in March. It falls on them. On Monday the shoe stayed off, and you wrote the winter list fair. Salt, light, malt, and the quarrel with Joan about soap went on two leaves by ten. Your mother put a letter back into an old field-name. Anne struck a quantity. In the afternoon the old shoes came off again until morning. On Tuesday the shoe went on. You bound at Hewlands from six till four, with an hour when Bartholomew called it. The ears were short. At the table the price of barley stayed Quiney's to name. Quiney said two-and-twenty the quarter, and thirty by Christmas. Anne gave the town's side. You said you would give what you can give, and that your lord's ear is kept for plays.

On Wednesday your father stopped your hand at the wicket and let Anne settle the door from her own bag. The amount stayed in her bag. Susanna cast examples on a slate, and the sums stayed on the slate. In the yard you laughed until your ribs ached and spoke four lines. On Thursday the new wheat was six-and-thirty. The children took you north past the Rother Market. The row was some twenty plots. Your father directed from the fourth rung. You gave twenty shillings toward a neighbour's timber, and your name for a dozen if the frame is pegged by Tuesday. At the bakehouse you said yes to two pounds for Quiney's own malt-house before Anne looked. Judith Sadler made the word be loan, a pound by Martinmas or soon after, the rest by Lady Day, and no use. She promised the three children mutton and a pie on Sunday. In the street Anne said that is two pounds you know where it is. You wrote the timber and the loan as two lines.

On Friday you looked at hose. At four you put thirty shillings in Anne's lap, over the quarter. She counted them as from anyone and tied them in her apron. You told her the box in London, to the shilling: twenty pound and eight. A share, if the Mayor lets the house alone, sits between Bartholomew and Christmas. She asked what it was for. You said a house, here. She told you the promise waits on the purchase. You said the looking was still ahead. Your mother set a pan down. You entered the thirty shillings on their own line. On Saturday the carrier was still the eighteenth, for letters. The play had waited nine days. Under the moon you wrote the orchard scene on the bench. She speaks first. About one, Anne asked who you were talking to. You said a girl at a window. You came in at half past two. You left the parting line in. The sheets stayed under the stone, with the inkhorn in the knot-hole and two quills beside it.
