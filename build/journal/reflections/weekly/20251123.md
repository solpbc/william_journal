# Week of Nov 23, 2025

You left a bank of wild thyme standing because it came too sweetly, and you did not trust it in the yellow fog. Two candles were the light. The sixpence is the firing. The loaf stayed two fingers short, and a penny. Gib stayed on the Chaucer.

Monday you played Egeon, and the boxes waited. At the Bell the brief was granted, and the gathering is still ahead. One pound of the August two came back before the capon. One pound is still owed. After it you gave two pounds to the town rather than to the man, and wrote Anne six lines that wait for the carrier of the fourth. The book took the two items, and the old count stood. Tuesday you gave John joy of his day by the item. You did not write his Thursday into the book. You played Gaunt in the cut book. Three leaves stayed under it. Nobody startled Nick. Arthur has the lines in case. Nick was not told.

Wednesday the house played Titus, and you were at home. At five the nights were named. St Stephen's is the Navarre comedy. St John's is the lovers, ten days old by then, and the second night is for a house that has supped. Innocents' is Errors, and the dog stays where she was heard. New hose, the whole company, of the stock. Nobody named a price. The court money is not in the house. Thursday you walked the cut, and then you tried the next pages. Kemp stopped a joke that made a workman a fool. You struck it out. Six, and a lion. The head is not written. Friday the term ended. Fair Em was the house's afternoon. At the Rose the yard shouted for a king who wins, and you brought home one line. Saturday you were in Titus, in the gown you had. The share was two-and-thirty shillings, said once. Five mornings at nine begin on Monday. You did not count the coffer. You did not write another page.
