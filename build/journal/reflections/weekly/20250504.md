# Week of May 04, 2025

You took the comedy to Blackfriars and left it there. The grace was long and the carving short. Jacqueline mended two words and said the French was an English dream of French. Field told you to leave the book, and he let the chronicle stay. He was not telling you news about Greenwich. By the cheese the home vowels were back. At your own board the uncles kept the breaths they already had. You did not write England.

Monday you said there to a hedge, and you stopped a king who might have stooped. The post had the Rose's Knack to Know an Honest Man. The afternoon was Egeon, and you added nothing. Tuesday the book was home. Nick took the mended vowel. He did not invent a French. The loaf was a finger and a half short, at a penny. It was not on a pan. The jig waited.

Wednesday the yard was thin. Pell named a giant over the water. Raye had seen a lion's skin and a club like a ship's mast, and he would not swear a height. James asked the timber and was given no bill. You wrote that they have Hercules, and you have a man who talks. Thursday you read Venice and went to none of it. Kemp had the stage for a quarter of an hour. You stood in the door. Friday you walked one speech, the opening and the turn on the sheet. The middle was not copied, because October will want the breath. She said you had followed a hearse and then stood. You told her you are the one, in the second act. Saturday morning you began the third act, half a sheet. The share was eight-and-twenty, and the news in that room was that the house stands. At ten past six Cuthbert, from Holywell, gave you Allen's figure. You agreed nothing. You did not write home.
