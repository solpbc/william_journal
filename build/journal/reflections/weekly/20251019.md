# Week of Oct 19, 2025

On Sunday the jaw was quiet for the first time since August, and you ate the soft side of the loaf. You left the almanac unmarked. You tied what you have of the lovers with a red thread from the house, an arm's length, and you did not write finished on the first leaf. The dawn in that bundle was already written. You wrote Anne three lines before you corrected another word. Gib sat on the Holinshed. The latch stayed unmended. On Monday Kettleby called from among the hampers at Somerset House. The court removes to Richmond, and my lord with it. Sir Thomas's place stays empty until his lady has rendered the accounts, and the Chamber pays nobody until then. He said you will be sent for, and that pay is another office. You stayed home from the Shrew. After five the tiring-house door shut. The company goes if it is sent for, and it does not ask for money down. The stock holds six pounds, enough for a boat, a lodging, and the boys' meat for five days. That sum is not a receipt. Kemp stopped before he could speak first, and gave the place to the grocer. You wrote expected, struck the rest, and entered no sum.

Tuesday was the last tender day of the jaw, and the hole where the tooth was has closed. Pope played old Capulet flat, down to the spices. Kemp found a pause, Nick laughed, and you wrote that one word. You promised nothing further. You stayed out of the afternoon's king play. On Wednesday the jaw was healed. You made three rolls of the king play agree. Your cue is the one that stands, and the copying cost eightpence out of the stock. You played in the afternoon. George Bryan told you the Earl of Arundel had died in the Tower on Sunday, and he would not add to the story. At night you wrote the girl alone with the vial, and you stopped before she drank. You spoke the death once, as he had told it.

On Thursday you played Navarre, and you stayed for the comedy instead of leaving early for the carrier. At half past five, six horses were still steaming. The money had come. Anne's letter said Susanna corrects the curate under her breath, Judith will not name a price for her song, and the brook at Shottery was dammed and then opened. Quiney is for London at Martinmas, and he still owes two pounds. At the Bell you added one line: the tooth is out, the king draws, and you are well. Hobbins read it back. At supper you declined the crust. Daniel had heard the boy in the garden, and his own stanzas seemed cold to him. You parted at Paul's Chain. On Friday Nick spoke the two lines truly, and the call for the Nurse cracked. Nobody laughed. You did not cut her part, and you did not say that in the room. Phillips will have him in the mornings. Those mornings have not begun. On Saturday you named the parents and the Nurse's noise, and you did not write the tomb. The week's share was thirty-eight shillings. The king play stands for Thursday week. Christmas stayed expected, with no sum written under it.
