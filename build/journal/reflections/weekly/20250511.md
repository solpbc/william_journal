# Week of May 11, 2025

Sunday was ordinary. The lesson was plain, and nobody numbered the heads. At her table the loaf was a penny and a finger and a half short. It was not on a pan. You said the king died on Friday in the second act, and that the third was begun yesterday on half a sheet. It was not told at the table. You paid threepence. At four you wrote home on a clean sheet, answered in her order, and sent the seamstress's part of the sleeve. You left the barn line blank. You did not defend the trade.

Monday you looked at Saturday's half-sheet and added nothing. James told the figure himself. Four-and-twenty pound a year, where the company pays fourteen, two years less a month, and a clause he would not explain. You said you have a king coming, and that it is long. You cursed nobody. Nothing was agreed. You played Navarre. At home the thimble went into the book at two shillings and sixpence. You wrote no name on the line.

Tuesday was a writing day at the lodging. The tables took sit, ground, stories. You continued Saturday's half-sheet of the king come home, in two runs with a long halt. Holinshed and Daniel lay open at the return from Ireland. You copied no sentence. You struck one greeting and wrote it shorter, his hand on the earth. The book took candles at fourpence. At noon you rehearsed Egeon's roll. On the Bishopsgate post the Theatre's bill for Two Gentlemen stood beside the Rose's for Long Meg. From the tiring-house door, Kemp's Launce had a rope with nothing on the end of it. At seven Gilbert, from St Bride's, gave you the wells, the beans, the twopence, and the twenty-sixth, which you had forgot. A year ago the middle of Stratford burned. You heard it nine days after, from a carter. You asked whether the frame in Chapel Street is finished. Then thirty more lines under the short greeting. The three words stayed three words.

Wednesday you wrote one page more. The men heard a false death and went home. You set the pen down, short of the ground speech. She hoped the ground was not the point. You sat, and you paid threepence. The three words stayed three words.

Thursday the rehearsal was a door. At Field's the sheets hung like washing. You paid fourteen shillings, unbound. You promised nothing about Whitsun. He said an error acquires company. Theseus was the next sheet, and you carried it flat. At the Bell the thimble was marked for Susanna, not before the Monday after Rogation Sunday. Two letters and the parcel were tenpence. Anne's letter gave you a water-wheel, a dispute, a second ballad, and a fault Susanna will tell you herself. You left the Bell, walked north, and were at the Theatre in time. At two you spoke Egeon in Errors, and then stood idle until the end.

Friday you asked Burbage to let Buckingham finish. He waited so largely that the pause was another way of commanding. You tried it again. You did not announce it. The empty yard changed more than the louder reading. You wrote, before you left the house, that a king who listens frights more than one who shouts. The Plutarch was unbound on the shelf. Daniel's third book was Saturday's. In the afternoon you were in the bill of Richard III. The roll had the play and the hour.

Saturday you bought the thin book for sixpence. You said nothing. At home you copied one sentence and ruled a line under it. You wrote no answer. The share was forty shillings, the spring's best, against eight-and-twenty last Saturday. The chest was not counted. After supper you sat. The landing and the greeting were already done. You wrote the two lines, went on to the hollow crown and the little pin, then Flint and a king coming down. You did not walk it. At one you refused the boast.
