# Week of Mar 02, 2025

You began at St Helen's with three days still to run before the ashes. Master Thwaite turned the sand-glass twice and said he would be brief. You paid sixpence for firing and washing till Lady Day. Sir John Spencer sat three rows ahead. He did not turn. Mistress Ansell had the heel of the loaf, a penny and a finger short, and would not salt the house ahead of the church. In the afternoon you took Friday's leaf from the folder, read the one line, and put it back. You did not give it to anyone. At the Bull the supper was tenpence a head. Nashe came uninvited, sang one verse, and paid a penny for one cup. You told him he was the page, not the pedant. Heminges touched the table and said the winter had held without a quarrel over the purse. You left close on nine.

On Monday you played the Duke at the Cross Keys at two. The part had his name at the head. No speech was copied under it. After the play, the masque at Whitehall was heard of only: Proteus, a rock of adamant, and no invitation. Kemp set the scholars' nothing against a penny. Bryan had waited at Somerset House. The warrant for December's twenty pounds is drawn and moving. You did not fetch it. My lord's word was to stop cleanly on Tuesday. At home Mistress Ansell asked which queen, and you gave her no ruff and no plot. On Tuesday the morning tally stayed twenty-four, and Garnish would not tick a parlour tune. You went on as Ferdinand, in the paper crown. After the third and last playing he said thirty-five lines in three days, and one whole song. You drank my lord's health. You named no age. The trestles stacked were the inn's. You heard the apprentices harder than the holiday. You did not answer them. The new lines were not copied into the book.

On Wednesday Master Thwaite read the commination and enjoyed it. The account book still said 1594, and the Queen's twenty pounds were not yet had. At the cold Theatre Ralph Raye had a bill for Seleo and Olympo and had seen the flag, and would not give a figure from a book he did not see. Kemp said three pounds. You said a fortnight. You stayed on this side of the river. Evening prayer was the office, not a second reading. Supper was fish at threepence. On Thursday you paid sixpence at Paul's for a worn Brooke, lacking the last leaf but one. John Harrison was in the yard. He did not speak. At night the page was headed Verona: two couplets, two names struck, two houses, the days left blank, a torch in a vault, and a stop. You wrote that the King comes first. The only payment on that page was the book. Gib lay across the right-hand page. You wrote no speech.

On Friday breakfast was threepence. Mistress Ansell said alike is no plot, and you would not give her a name or a quarrel. Anne's sheet stayed in the chest. You looked at the friar's name and wrote nothing under it. On Saturday you cleared the board. She counted four candle-ends and called the tidiness repentance. You paid her fivepence for a quire she had by her and twopence for a new horn of ink, and you counted the coffer. You set down no sum. The Lent list was written whole. You walked to Finsbury twice. The second time you did not try a name. Before eight the shoes were off. Monday's baskets are in the book, and they are not on the list.
