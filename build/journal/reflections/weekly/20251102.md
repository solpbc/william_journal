# Week of Nov 02, 2025

You stood through morning prayer on Sunday and read the day's leaf. Sir John sat his pew. At dinner the board was threepence. In the afternoon the other play was still one page. At the window you named the dead who live in books, and left yourself the Friar's letter. On Monday the horn was twopence. You played in the Navarre comedy. After it you wrote Kemp three minutes beside the bed, on a drum. He called it a crust and took it. Cowley's ten shillings stayed his wage.

On Tuesday Phillips put the girl a third under the note. She is fourteen. You kept her words. He counted two-and-thirty mornings to the sixth of December. The afternoon was Richard III. At home you struck a vault. On Wednesday Paul's was louder than the stalls. The book was printed over sea. You heard the talk at Paul's. On the tables the king is Shoreditch only. At home you said that June was a chronicle and November is a pamphlet. At the Hazel, Bryan kept the groom's report apart from the talk, and Alice asked where he heard it.

On Thursday you rehearsed the book as it is played. In the afternoon you were Gaunt for the sixth time, the jaw whole, done in the second act. A man in a frieze cloak paid for three and struck the rail. Heminges guessed a country justice. You struck another false start at home. On Friday the account book lay on the tables. Still in a duke's gown, you heard three nights at Richmond, and you heard Antwerp named for a book. You told Heminges at once who the cloak had been. On Saturday the fog took the porch while you were still at home. At the Theatre the share was thirty-four shillings, counted by candle. Last week had been thirty-five. You took one candle upstairs, sat, and wrote as far as the dagger. At midnight you said he is wrong by a minute.
