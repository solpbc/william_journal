# Week of Jun 22, 2025

The king stayed one book. On Sunday you looked at the forty leaves, with Gib on the Holinshed. At St Helen's the glass was turned twice. Sir John sat in his pew. Halberds stood at that pew. Verbeke came with the house. At dinner you ate with Mistress Ansell. The loaf was still a thumb short, and still a penny. You said Midsummer is Tuesday.

On Monday you looked at the same forty leaves. The Rose's title was Seven Days. You played Navarre. Kemp added nothing. The tally stayed at nineteen lines and a noise like a goose. At the parish fire the watch was doubled and went by in harness. Nobody leapt. Phillips tapped the bells on the doorpost. A boy supervised the grown men. Mistress Ansell stayed twenty minutes and took Alse in. Verbeke stayed at the door of the close. You called it a feast with the guard set on it.

On Tuesday the close was dressed, and you stood a quarter of an hour under your own lintel. You were in by six. You paid twenty shillings, counted twice, and took a receipt in her late husband's law-hand. The candles had come to more than the bread. After the rent the coffer was twenty-five pounds exactly. Sixty shillings sat apart in canvas, inside that count. Eight shillings and twelve were entered. At dinner Verbeke said memory makes bread bigger, and Alse, cutting the loaf four ways, said cutting does not make it larger. You played. You went to evening prayer.

On Wednesday you stayed in. The forty leaves stayed one book. At evening you told the house the king, since it is finished. She heard it with the knife down. The cousin reigns uneasily. She called that indigestion, and a bad example. A groom, and the man who had him killed. The glass told the truth, and Alse said he should have kept it. The fellows have not heard it. Kemp was not in the room.

On Thursday you wrote home that you are well and that you send threescore shillings, and you named the three children. John Heminges's man took the canvas to the Bell. The morning title was the second Caesar. At the day's end it was their last. The Admiral's men were packing hampers. Kemp said they have run. John said Bankside has nowhere to go of an afternoon but here. Margery would as soon they had somewhere. Nothing came back. You wrote the last day apart from the reasons.

On Friday you were in the house for Richard the Third. By report, eighteen hundred, two pillories, the Counter again, and timber toward Bishopsgate. You were not in the crowd. At the Mayor's gate you looked at a raw frame. She said that is not how you ask for bread, and she barred the shutter. Twenty are taken, you heard later. The memo was four words: at his door, ours. On Saturday the reckoning was two minutes and eight shillings, received. No order has shut the house. You meet Monday and see what Monday is. The July roads were asked. Kemp has still not been told.
