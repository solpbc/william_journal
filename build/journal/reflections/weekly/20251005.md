# Week of Oct 05, 2025

You kept the parish hour. At dinner the king was three steps and a bad example, because the cousin is not punished. Eleven pound six stayed Thursday's box. Forty-five shillings stayed your share. She held you to last Sunday. You did not go down Bishopsgate. Brooke lay under the weight, Gib on the chronicle, and the sheet for morning was clean.

Monday you opened the almanac on the sixth. At the window you said two-and-twenty, and turned the matter to the girl. By seven she had eleven lines of night. They ask the day to be done. You paid the first sixpence. At two you were the old Roman, off in the fourth. The Rose bill was Seven Days and Wise Man. You did not go.

Tuesday Nick gave the farewell plain. You struck the direction out. It was less to look at, and more to believe. Gaunt was the second time the king has been played, and you were dead by the middle. The next king is the sixteenth. Three candles went to the socket for the tooth.

Wednesday the moon was full, and the sheet was still Monday's. Two pence of cloves. She saw the three sockets and saved the speech. Cowley offered the old Roman. You kept the gown. The tooth had been at you since the ninth of September. The Rose bill was Crack Me. You did not go.

Thursday the term opened. The galleries were black with gowns, and they had come for the king. The bill was the comedy. They shouted Remuneration. You sent the book to Abel. Every third man had been told Thursday next. The next king is the sixteenth. At night you copied one line, and you wrote the morning's look at the tooth into the almanac.

Friday he found the villain, lower left. Tuesday at ten. Twelve pence, to be thanked after. You asked for Lent, and you took Tuesday. The shrew was the afternoon. The Rose had The Disguises, which you had already read. The first bran went on. A saucer of clove-wool sat by the inkhorn.

Saturday you had not slept since two. She said it was the nightingale. He stayed because she asked. There were rooks. At dinner three candles were the tooth and one was the lovers, and they had more sense than you. Titus again, off in the fourth. The share was thirty-eight shillings. The glass stayed locked. At night, in the rain, with aquavitae on a clout, you copied three leaves and laid them under the account book. The jaw is not quiet. The tooth is still in.
