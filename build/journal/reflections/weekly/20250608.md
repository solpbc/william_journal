# Week of Jun 08, 2025

The week began at St Helen's and at a shut shop in Blackfriars. You heard one glass, and then a portion read by order. You lost the psalm. You said nothing. At dinner you kept the comedy, because the copy is Garnish's and the company's, and you told the Fields the streets were quiet enough. You told them nothing of Friday. After dark you made one mark. You made no account of it.

On Tuesday you stayed in the chair from before four until eleven. The chronicle stayed shut. Three sheets came out of that stillness: the hall, the unmaking, the glass. You did not walk once. You did not start the prison, and you did not put the sheets away. Wednesday was an ordinary Two Gentlemen, and a full moon marked and nothing else. Thursday morning was cloth. A cloak and two sleeves became the sleeves only, at six shillings, to be paid when they are delivered. By dinner the cry had left the fish and become strangers. The house swore, you first. If anyone asks, the back room is let to a man from Norwich. Nobody came.

Friday you heard Southwark at the window. You did not cross. They had paid, by the telling, and you asked who had sold the sellers their butter. Saturday you agreed to play rather than be written down as afraid. You walked two hundred yards to Leadenhall, heard fourpence, and came home to a loaf a thumb short of the notch. The afternoon was the Shrew, by the fields, and the yard was thin. No sum was named.
