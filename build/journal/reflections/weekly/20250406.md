# Week of Apr 06, 2025

You spent Sunday and Monday in Henley Street, and the house was the work. Your father stayed out of the pew. He blamed a fairy. Joan told you to walk to Anne. She then found the leather. The wheel turned unevenly, and it turned every time.

On Monday each child had one ask. Hamnet was satisfied about the sword. Edmund was told that fifteen is for school. Your father sang half a catch and lost the rest. Anne counted four shillings that should come home and gave you a shirt with the collar turned. The tables held two words.

Tuesday she stood in the door, and the children slept. You wrote nothing. At Shipston you retied her knot as she had tied it. Long Compton was dry. At the Bear the spring notes were smaller than you remembered, and you slept.

Wednesday you passed Oxford. You said the question over. The horse had governed the day. At the Catherine Wheel you wrote a counting rhythm.

Thursday you came the long way. The bay went back at three. Sixteen days at twelvepence stood. The coffer was £19 6s. The four shillings were inside that count. You were asleep by eight.

Friday the yard was three times the Cross Keys. Cowley missed the Latin three times. You found your own correction on the wrong copy. Burbage had the size before the morning ended. That night the line ended "and that is enough," and you pencilled two jokes. Saturday Garnish called the mend against nature, and you called it cheaper than a new player. There was no bill. At dinner Anne's sentence stopped the table for a herring. The loaf was a finger and a half short. The children, she said, were well. You paid threepence.
