# Objects and books

What is in the room, and what has changed hands. One glance, not two files. The coffer, the loaf, and the sonnet tape have their own threads. Kemp's dog is a story of Christmas 1594. No dog appears in any 1595 scene.

Golding's Ovid, *Venus*, and *Lucrece* are there on 1 January. Field's Holinshed, volume two, comes on 19 March and is not back by 31 December. The Chaucer comes on the morning of 27 Oct (W407). § 9.2.2 names October and not the day. The entry names the morning. Follow the entry. Do not stage a shop.

John's 1568 sketch is shown once, on 2 Apr, and is never a filing. That fact is [stratford-kin.md](stratford-kin.md).
