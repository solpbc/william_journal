# *Love's Labour's Lost*

The comedy is finished in January, played from 20 February, and stays in the stock. *Love's Labour's Won* is a title Heminges asks for. It is never a play.

From world § 5.1, § 5.2 February and June, and the entries below. § 5 February says he asks for a *Won*, and names no day. The dated asks a writer may stage are 2 Jun (S051), 17 Jul (W058) and 25 Sep (W338). William does not write it.
