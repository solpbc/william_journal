# The earl

Southampton is "my lord", never *thou*. He is patron and dedicatee. Nothing becomes an affair. No verse is delivered to him or to a named woman. No gift of money passes either way.

From world § 5.2, § 9.2.5 and § 9.3 rule 11. He gets no further than the porter's lodge at Southampton House. Florio is the voice in between, until the one call on 23 September.
