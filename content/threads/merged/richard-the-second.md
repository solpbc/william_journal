# *Richard II*

No king play before the Holinshed. Begun 17 April, finished 21 June, read to the fellows on the morning of 30 June, licensed on 10 July with the deposition cut, first played 2 October, and played whole once, in a parlour, on 9 December.

From world § 5.1, § 9.2.5 and § 9.3 rule 13. The book has no clown in it from the day it is begun. He hears the play from the fourth act on the morning of 30 Jun (W053). He is not handed an empty place in the pile of rolls until 27 Aug (W076). That discovery belongs to [clown-war.md](clown-war.md). The 30 Jun afternoon is no play. A struck *Errors* bill sits beside it and is not played. The reading still happens. See [the-season.md](the-season.md).
