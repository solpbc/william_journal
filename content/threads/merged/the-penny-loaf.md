# The penny loaf

The price stays a penny. The portion shrinks. Never a penny loaf at 2d. Never "the wet summer": that was 1594. The harvest is thin, and no cause is given. There is never a flood.

From world § 1, § 4.1, § 5.2, and the entries below. The notch was cut by Mistress Ansell's late husband, in a good year. It is on the board all year. Butter is not this thread: 5d in June, 3d by the apprentices on the 13th, 4d where the constable stands on the 14th. Those prices do not move the loaf.

A measurement is only the one the entry gives. Do not smooth these into a steady shrinking.
