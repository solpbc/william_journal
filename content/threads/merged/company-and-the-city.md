# The company against the City

Eight sharers, a wooden playhouse on a lease with two years left, a ground landlord who wants more, and a Lord Mayor a hundred yards from the bed. The year presses this from gossip, to a named price, to riot and martial law, to a letter that is not granted, to a new mayor, and at the very end to talk of another house. Nothing is settled. No one pulls the Theatre down, and no one buys Blackfriars.
