# The coffer

Nine counted totals, and silence between them. Do not invent a balance. A share, a gift, a loan or a rent payment is not a new total unless this table says the coffer was counted.

From world § 5.1 and § 9.2.4. The nine totals: 1 Jan £24 10s · 25 Mar £27 2s · 10 Apr £19 6s · 24 Jun £25 0s · 27 Jul £26 8s · 22 Aug £20 15s · 29 Sep £23 10s · 24 Dec £33 0s · 31 Dec £33 10s. Superseded figures (£22 15s, £25 10s, £34 0s, £34 10s, £84 6s 8d, £28) are not to be used.

Belt composition only where an entry states it. On 25 Mar, £19 2s stays under the locks and £8 goes in the belt (60s in canvas and £5). On 27 Jul, £20 8s stays under the locks and £6 goes in the belt. On 22 Aug the locked £20 8s is as he left it, and 7s out of the belt makes £20 15s (W075). On 24 Dec he keeps 9s out for the journey and counts the rest at £33 0s (W107).

§ 9.2.4's own sum for the Lent drop, not a fresh derivation: £27 2s − £19 6s = £7 16s, and £7 16s = 38s horse, fodder and inns + 60s carried home + 58s of John's debts. On 10 Apr "the 4s brought back makes £19 6s".

He counts the coffer on 22 Feb and writes no figure (S117). That is a silence.

The year-end page (S116, and § 5 December) gives the three headlines: opened £24 10s, received £55 14s, laid out £46 14s, closed £33 10s. Check, in shillings: 490 + 1114 − 934 = 670, which is £33 10s. § 9.2.4 itemises the received: shares £51 14s + Hunsdon's 10s at Richmond + warrant 37s 6d + Hoby 12s 6d + Quiney £1 = £55 14s. Court Christmas is not in the received. What is owed him, and what he owes, is [debts-and-reckonings.md](debts-and-reckonings.md).
