# The thread register (Grok merge)

*Grok merger, 2026-11-16. One file per through-line, for a week-writer who will not read the world. Reasons, failed claims, and the world's own contradictions: reasoning.md.*

A thread is a fact that changes across 1595 and that a writer can get wrong by not knowing where it stands. Each file is one table in date order. `to` is inclusive. The next row's `from` is the next day. Every table runs from 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 with no gap and no overlap.

To brief a week, take every row whose span covers the week's first day and every row whose span covers its last day. If they differ, the week crosses a turn, and the writer needs both. **true now** and **not yet** are the instructions. Obey them. The world's prose is the source. This register does not add a fact. A sum, a date, or a sentence that is not in [`../../../world/world.md`](../../../world/world.md) is not in these tables. A coffer figure between the nine counted dates is not filled in.

The afternoon bill is a confirmed `company_play` line in [`../../../calendar/merged/calendar.json`](../../../calendar/merged/calendar.json), as it stands after the correction at `b8c71c159c7`. `repertory.json` is not a bill. Where an entry and that calendar disagree about what was played, the table follows the calendar and reasoning.md quotes both.

### Why this grain

Kept apart, because a week writer gets them wrong independently:

- **Four play files.** The world calls them four plays and four seasons. One row that holds all four hides which of them exists this week.
- **The coffer apart from debts.** A share, a rent, a loan, or a gift is not a new balance. Folding them invites a figure on a day he did not count.
- **Where he is, apart from Anne, apart from the kin.** Sleeping at Henley Street is not the state of the marriage, and a birthday is not a place.
- **The earl apart from the court.** Southampton is not Hunsdon, not Hoby, not Cecil. Patronage of the company is the court thread.
- **What he knows, apart from the season and the company.** The day a fire, a death, or a pamphlet arrives is its own trap.
- **The penny loaf on its own.** The price stays. The portion is a dated measurement. It is not "the news".

Changed from Grok's twenty-four:

- **No address file.** The pronouns that never change are standing bounds, below. The dated turns are few: Norwich from 12 Jun lives in the household; "Sweet Master Shakespeare" and Kemp's *thou* live in the clown war. A fifth file for the same three dates is noise.
- **One objects-and-books file**, not a shelf file and a separate objects file. A writer looks once at the room. The rows still turn on different things: the Holinshed that never goes back, the glass, the three leaves, the latch, the Chaucer on the morning it arrives.
- **A household file, added.** Grok had no Mistress Ansell thread. A week inside her house will invent her. Her state changes: the January posset, the Norwich oath, the garret on the riot nights, the weeks he is away, the quince jaw, the one afternoon she pays her own two pennies, the mended latch. Dinner verdicts that do not change the house are not rows.

Gemini's thirteen follow the world's chapter list. They catch real slips, and their cells are essays. A writer cannot obey an essay. Where a Gemini date survived a check against the world, it is a row in the narrow file, not a reason to adopt the broad one.

## Where one turn forces another

- **6 Jan, the bean** ([nick-sorrell.md](nick-sorrell.md)). The bean falls to Nick. He fines Kemp a silence. Sam does not find it.
- **15 Mar, the warrant** ([debts-and-reckonings.md](debts-and-reckonings.md)). The £20 becomes coin. His 37s 6d is his. It is not a new coffer total. The account page that night rules the *expected* and *received* columns. No balance is entered.
- **19 Mar, the Holinshed** ([objects-and-books.md](objects-and-books.md)). *Richard II* may begin. Before this day there is no king play.
- **26 Mar–10 Apr, and 28 Jul–21 Aug.** He is out of London ([where-he-is.md](where-he-is.md)). No call. The coffer is not recounted until he is home. Anne's question is asked on 5 Apr.
- **10 Jun, the deposition, and 10 Jul, the cut.** The scene exists from the first date and is out of the public book from the second. Kemp does not know he has no part until 27 Aug. Canon Row on 9 Dec is the only time the cut scene is played. *Errors* plays at two that afternoon first (S112a, cal-0744).
- **12 Jun–18 Jul.** He does not cross the river. On 19 Jul he does, the first time since May (W059). From 13 Jun to 5 Jul he stays within 600 m of home except the road to the Theatre. Verona's first brawl (5 Jul) is written by a man who watched a riot from behind a shutter. Do not send him to Southwark to research it.
- **12 Jun, Norwich** ([the-household.md](the-household.md)). If anybody asks, Verbeke is from Norwich.
- **21 Jul, the second fire.** He learns it today, not on the 15th. Gilbert's version is wrong and the letter corrects it. The walk home follows. The back is hurt on 12 Aug. On 16 Aug Anne names the house, with no price.
- **7 Aug, Quiney's £2, and 24 Nov, £1 back.** The loan, the brief, and the barn gift are three different sums. The coffer thread does not absorb them.
- **13 Sep, the Mayor's letter, and 16 Sep, "play on".** The company is threatened and then not stopped. No order arrives.
- **18 Sep, the margin, and 22 Nov, *thou*.** "Sweet Master Shakespeare" sticks from 20 Sep. The clown war ends because the *Dream* gives Kemp a weaver. The tally closes the same hour.
- **27 Oct, the Chaucer** ([objects-and-books.md](objects-and-books.md)). It is in the room that morning (W407). The earl does not become more intimate because his household lent a book.
- **24 Oct, the crack, and 28 Dec, the break.** Juliet is played on a managed voice. After the jig he does not sing. Arthur takes the verse.
- **9 Dec, the play whole, and 26–28 Dec, the Queen.** The deposition is heard once, in a parlour. The glass is broken there. The court is not paid. The sonnets are already at the bottom of the coffer (13 Dec). The *Dream* is still open on 31 Dec.
- **30 Dec, the letter** ([anne-and-the-house.md](anne-and-the-house.md)). He answers Shottery in writing. He still does not buy the house.

## Standing bounds

These do not change, so they are not threads. They bind every week.

- Hamnet is nine until 2 Feb and ten after. Judith the same. Nothing plays on 1596. No illness, no last, no grave near the children. Nobody says "Agnes". Hamnet Sadler is a different man.
- Arthur Meeks is fourteen. Sam Latchet is twelve. Nick is bound to Heminges and lodged at Aldermanbury. Sam is bound to Phillips. Phillips trains Nick. Phillips is not Nick's master.
- No plague, except as something past. No tour. No New Place price, inquiry, or purchase. No arms filing. No *Love's Labour's Won* beyond a title Heminges asks for. No Hand D, no *King John*, no finished *Dream*.
- The lady is never named. The earl is never an affair. No sonnet is addressed to a real person. The 13 Dec tape is the private-verse thread, not a gift to her.
- He is never at Tyburn or at Tower Hill on the day men die. He is never inside a riot. A Rose bill is not a play he watched. He never knows a Rose receipt, nor Henslowe's shillings.
- Nobody remarks on a device.
- Calls: at most two a week, London range only, none while he is away (26 Mar–10 Apr, 28 Jul–21 Aug, 24–29 Dec). The ban includes the days he arrives back.
- The legal year is headed "1594" until 24 Mar and "1595" from Lady Day.
- Kemp's dog is the Christmas before this year, and it does not appear. The only dogs in 1595 are S071's dog on the stair and W073a's inn dog, both § 6 (the editor, 2026-11-17, from the continuity audit).
- **Pronouns, all year unless a thread names the day they change.** Burbage is *thou* both ways. Heminges is *you*, and "John". Phillips, Pope, and Bryan are *you* and their first names. The boys are *thou*: "Nick", "lad". Field is *thou* in private and *you* in the shop. Anne and the children are *thou*. His father is *you*, and "sir" when it counts. Hunsdon and Southampton are "my lord", never *thou*. Mistress Ansell is *you*, "Mistress". The lady is *you*, and almost nothing aloud. "On the thirty-first of April" means never.
- Saddle-soreness and blistered feet are not illness. Court nights cut sleep and are not illness. No child is ill. There is no fourth illness.
- Heart rates, step counts, and hours of sleep are colour in the entries. They are not a state a week writer must hit.

## The bill, after the calendar correction

Recounted 2026-11-16 from confirmed `company_play` lines in the current calendar. Detail and the diff against the pre-correction file are in reasoning.md. The season file carries the counts a writer needs. Two facts belong here because they bind every week:

- **17 and 18 Jan are both *Errors*.** That is the one confirmed same-title pair on consecutive dates. He plays Egeon on the 17th (S007). Cowley plays him on the 18th (G006). The stock list's sentence "never the same play on consecutive days" is the world's, and the calendar does not obey it for this pair. Follow the calendar.
- **30 Jun is no play** (cal-0406). An *Errors* bill for that afternoon is cancelled (cal-0407). The morning is the reading (W053).
