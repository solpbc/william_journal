# The clown war

Kemp is the star and the first name on the warrant. William writes him less and less room, and *Richard II* has none. Kemp fights for the laugh the yard paid for. The quarrel runs from the first tally on 12 February to the truce on 22 November, when a weaver with an ass's head earns the first *thou*.

From world § 1, § 3.3 (Kemp, Garnish), § 9.2.4 (the early totals), § 9.2.9 (the pronoun), and the entries below. § 9.2.4 stops at March: 11 lines on 20 Feb, 24 after two days, 35 and one whole song after three performances, nine written into the book on 12 Mar. § 6 continues, and those later totals are included here.
