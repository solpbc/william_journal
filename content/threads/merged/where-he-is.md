# Where he is

London, the road, Stratford, Richmond. Calls are forbidden while he is away: 26 Mar–10 Apr, 28 Jul–21 Aug, and 24–29 Dec (§ 9.3 rule 19). The ban includes the days he arrives back.

From world § 5.1 and § 9.3 rules 10 and 19. He does not cross the river from 12 Jun through 18 Jul. The crossing on 19 Jul is the first since May, and the May crossing is the Rose on 20 May (W043). § 5's "since May" and the rule agree if read that way. From 13 Jun through 5 Jul his tracks stay within 600 m of home, except the road to the Theatre.
