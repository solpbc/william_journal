# Stratford kin

Ages that change, John's porch, and two fires of which only one is this year's news. Hamnet is nine until 2 February and ten after, and ordinary in every appearance. Nobody says "Agnes". Hamnet Sadler is a different man. No illness, no last, no grave near the children.

From world § 5.2, § 9.2.5 and § 9.3 rule 5. Dated ages, and no others: the twins are ten from Candlemas (S012); Edmund is fifteen on 3 May (W038); Richard is twenty-one on 11 Mar (W020's day); Susanna is twelve on 26 May (S049); Gilbert is twenty-nine on 13 Oct (W403). Model ages in § 9.2.1 are not for factual copy. Do not put a number on Anne, John or Mary.

The first fire is a year behind him on 13 May (S045). The second is 15 July, about twenty houses and malt-barns in a row north of the centre. None of the family's is touched. He learns it on 21 July, not on the 15th ([what-he-knows.md](what-he-knows.md)). Henley Street is not burned.

John stays from church on Mothering Sunday 30 Mar (W027) and on 6 Apr (W030), the motive left on the 1592 return, "for fear of process for debt". He stays again on 3 Aug, shop door shut, and the entry leaves the reason as it lies (W065). The world does not show him restored after that. Do not invent a return, and do not invent a fourth absence. On 2 Apr he shows the yellowed 1568 arms sketch once (S031). No filing and no promise.
