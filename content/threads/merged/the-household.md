# The household

Mistress Ansell's house, and what a week inside it may say about her. Dinner verdicts that do not change the house are not rows. The loaf is [the-penny-loaf.md](the-penny-loaf.md). The coffer is [the-coffer.md](the-coffer.md). His illnesses are [the-body.md](the-body.md). Where he sleeps is [where-he-is.md](where-he-is.md).

She is *you*, and "Mistress". Callers stop at the foot of the stair. Ink is 2d the horn, from her. The chamber is £4 a year, 20s on each quarter day by noon. Firing and washing are 6d a week from Michaelmas to Lady Day, and not between. Board is 3d a meal. Candles are his. Gib sits on the book he should not be on. None of that is a turn. The rows are the turns.
