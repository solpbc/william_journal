# The court

Hunsdon, Tilney, three nights at Richmond, and money that is not paid. The Christmas 1595 warrant is dated 21 December 1596, payees Heminges and Bryan. It is not received in this year. Expected and received stay apart.

From world § 5.1, § 5.2 December, § 9.2.4 and the entries below. The public bill is [the-season.md](the-season.md). The Queen's £20 for the Greenwich plays of Christmas 1594 is company money. It is fetched on 15 Mar. The split is [debts-and-reckonings.md](debts-and-reckonings.md).

Tilney licenses *LLL* on 24 Jan for 7s, cuts the deposition on 10 Jul for 7s, and on 13 Nov reads *Romeo* and changes nothing. Those three acts are in the play files. This table is the patron and the unpaid nights.
