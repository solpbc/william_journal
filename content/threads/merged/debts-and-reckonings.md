# Debts and reckonings

What is owed, what has come in, and what must not be spent twice. None of these sums is a coffer balance. The balances are [the-coffer.md](the-coffer.md).

From world § 5.1, § 5.2, § 9.2.2 and § 9.2.4. The £20 of 15 Mar is company money, fetched by Kemp, Shakespeare and Burbage. Costs £5: boat and baskets 16s, rewards 34s, the Cheapside mercer 50s. The remaining £15 is eight parts of 37s 6d: seven sharers plus a stock share held by Heminges. His 37s 6d is his share of that warrant, not the coffer.

From March the account book keeps *expected* and *received* apart (§ 5 March). Before that, on 3 Jan, expected money is already a danger at the table, and it is not yet two columns.

Weekly share bands, not balances: Jan–Feb 18–30s · Lent 0 · Apr–May 25–40s · Jun 8–15s · Jul 10–20s · Aug 0, except the 26s share on 30 Aug (W077), the first since July · Sep 25–35s · Oct 30–45s · Nov 25–40s · Dec 20–40s, court unpaid. Named weeks: 30s (22 Feb), 38s (26 Apr), 34s (3 May), 40s (17 May), 36s (24 May), 8s (28 Jun), 45s on the week reckoned 4 Oct (W086, the best week), 38s on the week to 25 Oct (S100). First-day company boxes, not his share: *LLL* £7 11s, *Richard II* £11 6s, *Romeo* £12 13s. Canon Row is £5 to the company from Hoby, 12s 6d a share.

Rent is £4 a year, 20s on 25 Mar, 24 Jun, 29 Sep, and Christmas paid early on 22 Dec. Firing and washing 6d a week from Michaelmas to Lady Day only: it ends 25 Mar and resumes 29 Sep. Sent home, 60s a quarter: by hand in Lent, by carrier 26 Jun, by carrier 2 Oct, with the Christmas pack 18 Dec. Over that, 30s into Anne's lap on 8 Aug (W331). No Southampton gift. No New Place price. No price for the barn beyond the 20s below.
