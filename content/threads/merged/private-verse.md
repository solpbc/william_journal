# Private verse

Sonnets through the year, no addressee, no number, no title. Tied with a tape on 13 December and put at the bottom of the coffer. No leaf is addressed or delivered to a real person. The *you* of them is not the earl, the lady, or anyone else.

From world § 5.1, § 9.2.5 and § 9.3 rule 11. Where a page quotes a finished sonnet, the text is the 1609 printing in old spelling, flagged as public-domain text in an invented setting. No composition date is presented as a record.
