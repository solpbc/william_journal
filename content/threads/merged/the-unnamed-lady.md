# The unnamed lady

Seven crosses, and one speech of six words. She is never named. She is never kin to court musicians. She is never an affair. Advent Sunday, 30 November, is not a cross.

From world § 9.2.5 and § 9.3 rule 11. The crosses: 5 Jan (W002), 28 Feb (W018), 22 Mar (M009), 22 May (W044), 8 Jun (W048), 14 Sep (W336), 29 Oct (S101). Her only speech is on 22 May: "You are the one that writes." "I had thought you taller" is unused. Do not spend it.

The week table calls the 5 Jan window Morley's. He does not go to a music evening in that house until 22 Mar.
