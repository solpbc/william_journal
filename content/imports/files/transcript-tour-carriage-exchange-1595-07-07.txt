[00:00:06] John Heminges: Three dates at the foot of the sheet and not one figure written under them. Saturday the twenty-sixth, Monday the twenty-fifth of August, and Thursday the seventeenth to look again.
[00:00:24] Augustine Phillips: Will Kemp took his coat out the door still muttering about Kent. He meant every word of his against.
[00:00:40] William Shakespeare: He has a right to it. Five shillings in a country town against ten in Shoreditch is an easy sum for a sharer and a hard one for a hired man.
[00:00:58] John Heminges: Oats are dearer every mile and country mayors want no crowd at their gate. A wagon eats coin on wet days and dry days alike. An empty yard costs us nothing till we open it.
[00:01:18] Augustine Phillips: I sat on the chest and kept my mouth shut because the argument was already finished. I saved my throat for tonight's singing.
[00:01:34] William Shakespeare: Thomas made me own my share of the vote aloud. August suits the pages better than it suits the road, and I am not pretending otherwise.
[00:01:52] John Heminges: The road stays a guess till the seventeenth. Lock the sheet in the desk, Augustine — we have Richard's twenty-seven years waiting at the Jackdaw.
[00:02:10] William Shakespeare: I have his pearwood glass wrapped in cloth under my arm. Let us walk before the capon cools.
