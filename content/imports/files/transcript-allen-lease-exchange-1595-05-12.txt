Tiring-house exchange on Giles Allen's lease terms

[00:00:06] Richard Burbage: My father is already halfway up Holywell Lane. He went out before the yard was dry.
[00:00:20] John Heminges: Let him walk it off. Nothing was signed in this room and nothing is going into my book until a figure is agreed.
[00:00:34] William Shakespeare: Four-and-twenty pound a year where we pay fourteen, and two years less a month left on the ground. That much I heard plain.
[00:00:50] Richard Burbage: It is the five-year clause that sticks in his throat. Cuthbert brought the heads on Saturday, and Allen still would not let anyone read what he means by it.
[00:01:08] John Heminges: An unread clause is no bargain. A man who will not show his ink is asking us to sign blind.
[00:01:24] William Shakespeare: Cuthbert came to Great St Helen's Saturday wanting me to write Allen a sharp answer. I have never seen Allen. I will not curse a man I have not seen.
[00:01:42] Richard Burbage: You kept your head, Will. My brother wanted the timber priced before my father talks of pulling beams down, and he is right on that.
[00:01:58] John Heminges: Price the timber when there is a carpenter in the yard, not after an afternoon of Navarre. What of this long king you promised James?
[00:02:14] William Shakespeare: Half a leaf of the third act, and three words on my tables. Long enough to keep him patient, and not a page more tonight.
