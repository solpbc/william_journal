# Week of Sun 28 Sep 2025

## Reflection

You kept St Michael with a hand on your jaw, and the parish was welcome to call it devotion. At the board the jaw was a quince. Mistress Ansell priced the drawing at a shilling and you said after Thursday, meaning the king. You would have given half of Saturday's thirty-four shillings to chew. You did not turn the half into a sum. Verbeke offered a door. Alse brought sops. Anne got six lines: trust the boy, the fact of a tooth, and the king on Thursday. The Mayor's letter is not in them.

Monday you broke once on sceptred and paid the quarter, twenty shillings told twice. Drayton brought the specimen to the hall. You did not go to Paul's. You would have entered the pen of gold only if they judged by speed. The loaf was short of the notch. The sixpence for firing was named. It was not taken. You ruled the coffer at twenty-three pound ten, with sixty shillings sewn apart inside it. Tuesday a carter named the play before he had seen it. Burbage said the prison speech twice to an empty house. The gown did not show. You gave the tooth two days. Wednesday Field showed you what a turned letter costs, and Jacqueline made you move the pile. Gilbert carried the canvas to the Bell. You did not walk it. Alse asked whether the man is sorry to leave England or sorry for it, and you said both.

Thursday the Rose promised a disguise and you promised an ending. You did not learn what the disguise earned. Gaunt landed in a hush. The glass was locked at half past one. The deposition stayed a report. The boxes held eleven pound six, in one sum, and the yard was quiet. Pell said so. Richard looked at you. He said nothing. Friday the girl kept fewer words, and the Jackdaw charged sixpence for eggs and a knee. You had rather a full house were like a wedding. The jaw was no better for fame. You did not go to the barber. Saturday the share was forty-five shillings, the best week. You entered it. You did not make a jest. The king is to be played once a week, on a changing day, Tuesday next and then Thursday the sixteenth. The coffer was not counted again.
