## Pulse

- title: Took forty-five shillings
- one sentence: You played Egeon, and after it the share was forty-five shillings, the best week.
- details: The afternoon is Errors. You play Egeon. The journal does not keep the performance. At the reckoning the boxes come open on Heminges's green baize: pennies, half-groats, threepences, a few sixpences, and one clipped shilling Margery swears she never took. She is in the room and does not have to say it again. The galleries this week come to more than the whole house took in a June week, and nobody names the June week as a figure. Heminges has his items: a crown new gilt, three shillings; rosemary and a black pall, fourteen pence; a looking-glass at four shillings, bought in May and not yet looked in. The hired men have their ten shillings, which is a wage and not a slice of Thursday. The share is forty-five shillings. James calls a Thursday that takes eleven pound a thing worth a landlord's envy, and Cuthbert steers him off the lease. Kemp wants you to learn to empty a house. Garnish's tally gives Kemp no interest in the king and no principal in the jig. They fix the king once a week, on a changing day: Tuesday next, then Thursday the sixteenth. Richard says it will be. You take the forty-five and enter them without a jest. The coffer is not counted again.
