[01:04] william_shakespeare: Show me the cost, then. I have been living as if a fault were a mood. I should like to see a fault that has weight.
[01:20] richard_field: The forme comes unlocked. The line of type comes up in the fingers. The whole page is justified again, because one letter forgiven is a line that will not lock. Then a sheet is pulled, and the sheet is the only honest witness.

[02:34] william_shakespeare: I heard thee. I was about to say it myself, in worse words, and I am glad thou wert faster. The king has had too many of my mornings already.
[02:54] richard_field: Then stop giving him mine. A turned letter is not a tragedy, Will. It is a morning. Tragedy is what thou wilt charge for on Thursday, and I hope the town pays thee better than a reader pays a corrected page.

[03:36] richard_field: Confess it to him, not to my type. My type is already tired of authors. Hold this sheet while I see whether the line sits. And do not improve it. Improvement is how a page learns to wobble.
[03:56] william_shakespeare: I will hold it. I will not improve it. Thou hast no idea how rarely I make that promise, and how poorly I have kept it since the spring.
[04:14] richard_field: I have some idea. I have printed men like thee. They all think a second thought is a kindness. The kindness is leaving the sort alone once it locks.

[06:42] william_shakespeare: The paper, and the scolding, and nothing that has a price beyond fourpence halfpenny. I am not buying a lesson. Thou gavest it free, which is like thee and unlike a stall.
[07:02] jacqueline_field: He gave it because you were standing in the way of the press. Do not promote it to friendship until the sheet is dry. Friendship can wait on a damp proof.

[07:40] richard_field: Another man's. Be grateful. Thine would have taken the morning and the moral with it. This one takes the morning only, and the moral thou mayst carry back to Shoreditch at no charge.
[08:00] william_shakespeare: I will carry it. A word changed here is a journeyman's morning. A word changed in a roll is three pages for a man who is not me. I have been pretending those were the same cheapness.
[08:20] richard_field: They are not. Go and play thy king, or thy shrew, or whatever the afternoon has the cruelty to ask. Chew on the right. And take the quire before Jacqueline finds a second pile.
[08:42] jacqueline_field: There is no second pile, unless you make one by standing still. The door is behind you, Master Shakespeare. The paper is in your hand, if Richard has let go of it.
[09:00] richard_field: I have let go. Fourpence halfpenny is on the nail, not in a ledger of mine. God keep thee. Try not to alter a locked line before dinner.
[09:18] william_shakespeare: God keep thee. And thee, Mistress Field. I am moved. The pile is moved. The rest of the grandeur can stay on the stone, where it costs someone a real morning.
