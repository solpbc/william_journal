# Week of Sun 13 Apr 2025

## Reflection

The house stayed dark, and the date the company kept saying was Easter Monday. On Sunday Thwaite preached the crowds who cry Hosanna, looked at nobody, and named thirty years without palms. You wrote nothing in the aisle. The apprentices had sallow in their caps. You took no sprig. The almanac said the day was cold. The tables still said one answer. You did not write the answer.

Monday Sam sang the cuckoo clear. The hooks stayed split. You did not call for a hem. At dinner the loaf was a finger and a half short, still a penny, and you paid threepence. You did not open the coffer. Mistress Ansell told you not to improve your wife's sentence. It had silenced the table once. The full moon was in the almanac. You did not start a king under it. Tuesday you left the Worthies at ten minutes to eleven, read Daniel's second book standing at Paul's, and paid two shillings, the dearest book since Golding. Drayton said there was no laugh in four books. You would not tell him how many sonnets you have. The ledger took one line. You wrote no total. Joan's day passed with nothing sent. That night a finger stayed in Daniel and a finger in the chronicle.

Wednesday the messenger took three paces, Nick held two beats, and Burbage spoke the twelvemonth and a day in a plain voice. Sam's note was a little thick and still came. You ruled no sheet. Thursday at first light the two lines went down, Gaunt's four words, and you said them several ways. At the yard the sleeve was empty. Kemp was not shown the page. By dinner the quarrel was sixty lines. There were no blots, and the tables said he likes to hear himself. Friday you wrote one question beside the King's last days, stood through the long gospel in black. You took no bread and no cup. In the afternoon Verona got two notes. You wrote no line. You sat still. Saturday Sam croaked like a rook and was stopped. He was not told there was another boy. At four you said the owl may be a judgment and the cuckoo cannot, and that the king is talking. You laid the sheets face down and shut Daniel. You copied nothing.
