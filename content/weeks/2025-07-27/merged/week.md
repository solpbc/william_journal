# Week of Sun 27 Jul 2025

## Reflection

You counted the coffer on Sunday and shut it. Six pounds went into a belt. Twenty pound and eight shillings stayed under two locks. You wiped the pack-list. Brooke stayed on the shelf, and Holinshed stayed under its cloth, with Gib on the cloth. On Monday you left the close at ten past four. The lane after Kensington cost a mile. You paid a penny for ale at Uxbridge and two pence for a loft at Chalfont. The parcels in the pack were the ones you had packed.

On Tuesday, between Aylesbury and Waddesdon, you said you would tell her the horse was dear. You wanted to arrive tired, and to have paid something. You spoke a boy under a wall who waits to answer. At Grendon a constable gave you one bench. You wrote six of his words, and the four you meant to keep. On Wednesday a man binding told you two for one where it should be four. Thunder wet you to the shirt on the last mile into Banbury. Supper and the bed were ten pence. The sheets in the oilcloth were dry. You said she speaks first.

On Thursday Judith showed you your father's walk, and Hamnet kept a hundred miles. Susanna looked at your feet. At the town end of the bridge Richard took the pack, and the flat parcel rides on top. Anne had written stay away, and the letter had also said come for harvest. You told her the horse was dear. She put your feet in a tub and dressed two burst blisters. On Friday the rye was in, and light, and the barley stopped short of a figure. On Saturday the three of them told you the fire, and you gave the fables, the ballad, and the chipped H. Anne asked three weeks. You said till the eighteenth.
