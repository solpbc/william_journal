## Pulse

- title: Told Anne till the eighteenth
- one sentence: The three of them told you the fire, and you gave out the book, the ballad, and the H.
- details: On the bench under the orchard wall Susanna gave you the fire in order. The cry about two in the afternoon, the wind in the south-west, the bell, buckets from the Guild Pits, twenty houses and barns by dark, and nobody hurt. Judith gave it in voices, the widow who would not leave her hens, and the bailiff's man shouting at the wind. Hamnet gave it as the run to the top of the street, Richard turning him back, and a bent nail. Then the bundle. The fables with woodcuts went to Susanna. The ballad went to Judith. You sang it once, badly, and she had the tune and the words before dinner. The chipped H went to Hamnet. You told him it had been in Plutarch. On whom, you yielded to Master Aspinall. Susanna asked if the queen hid, and you said she steps into the shadow of the trees. Anne passed with a basket and asked three weeks. You said till the eighteenth. She said hm.
