## Pulse

- title: The horse was dear, sir
- one sentence: Your father asked if they took the horse for debt, and you said the horse was dear.
- details: You woke at six into a house that had been up an hour. Joan called your feet a disgrace. At noon it was a fish day, and Hamnet Sadler had sent a Lammas loaf of the new wheat, the size the assize allows. Ten were at the table. Your father said grace and then the line about the Queen's highway. You answered only that the horse was dear. Your mother looked at your ankles before your face and put the heel of the loaf on your trencher without a word. The rye is in, and light. The wheat at Shottery stands thin. Nobody put a figure on the barley. Richard said the weather will hold, and nothing else. Afterwards your feet went into a basin of salt water. At four you were at Holy Trinity for the ordinary evening prayers, and you came home after.
