- one sentence: Judith showed you your father's walk, Hamnet kept a hundred miles, and Anne dressed two burst blisters.
- details: The air was washed after the storm. At eight you were on Sun Rising Hill, and Stratford's spire was a pin in the haze of the Vale. Edge Hill was behind you by noon. At the brook below Ettington you sat with both feet in the water and shaved by feel, the heel bound in a strip of shirt. The bridge at five had fourteen arches and low reed-green water. Judith said you walk like your father, and she showed you. Hamnet asked the miles and was content with a hundred. Susanna looked at your feet and said nothing. At the town end of the bridge Richard took the pack, and the only argument was where the flat parcel should ride. At the door Anne said she had written don't come. You said her letter said come for harvest, and that the horse was dear. She sat you down. Joan took the cloak.
