[02:34] henry_carey_hunsdon: Ha. Your grocer would know who pays. A pox on it, so would I. The office is not filled. You'll be paid. You'll be paid slow. You were paid slow when he was alive. Tell your grocer the Queen's debt is better than another man's coin.
[02:48] william_shakespeare: I'll tell him so, my lord. He'll write it in his book and look at it.
[02:54] henry_carey_hunsdon: Ha! Let him. Kettleby will send you the days and the hours. And Shakespeare. Clean hose. All of you. Last year one of your fellows had a hole in his heel a man could have put a fist through, and I saw her see it.
[03:08] william_shakespeare: It shall be mended, my lord.
[03:12] henry_carey_hunsdon: Mend it. God keep you.
[03:16] william_shakespeare: And your good lordship.
