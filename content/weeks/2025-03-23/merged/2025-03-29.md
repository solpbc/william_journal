## Pulse

- title: Sat tenth at a Lenten table
- one sentence: You sat as the tenth at a Lenten table, heard the gloves counted short, and showed a death with a spoon.
- details: The house stayed the house. At noon a stool came in from the shop and you were the tenth. Lent food: pottage, salt fish, new bread. Everyone talked across everyone. Joan asked whether London ladies paint. Richard passed things and said nothing. Edmund asked to hold the sword and to hear how Master Burbage dies. You showed it with a spoon, and you told him to finish school. Judith did your father crossing a room, and nobody rebuked her. He turned the London gloves inside out and counted the stitches short, and said they smell like a duchess. Anne put them on. Susanna kept yesterday's book by her bowl and was not asked to perform it. Nobody counted a coffer. Nobody paid a shop. The night was the easiest you have had in a long while.
