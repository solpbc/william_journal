# Week of Sun 23 Mar 2025

## Reflection

You began at St Helen's with the prodigal, and you packed in the afternoon as a man who means to be present when he arrives. The gifts went into four knots. Brooke stayed. Holinshed went under a cloth, against the cat. You promised to carry Mistress Ansell's opinion of the forsworn gentlemen, and the rest of what you had been saying you said only to the plaster. Half the Stratford list came out, the speech and the walk that would have made a show of being home. On Monday you read the chronicle, wrote that it was not begun, and put the cloth back before the candle.

On Tuesday the law's year changed, and the room stayed as it was. Twenty shillings were counted twice. She kept the chamber, ended the firing sixpence until Michaelmas, and wrote the receipt in her husband's law-hand. The page records a horn of ink at twopence and a quire at fivepence, and the told sum stays twenty-seven pounds and two shillings. You locked nineteen pounds and two shillings, and you put the canvas and five pounds into the belt. She gave you small beer and a blessing. On Wednesday you took the bay at first light and said eight words. The climb and the lantern-yard are what the day kept of the miles. You were sore from one o'clock. On Thursday you rode seven hours, and in the afternoon you said Gaunt twice. Two masters argued an ending, and you kept to the cup. On the dark miles after Oxford you forgot what you had meant to say to her, and said from the saddle that you were glad of it. At the Bear you tied a parcel and wrote questions.

On Friday the rain held, and you kept the road. At the bridge Richard began with the hay. At the door you stopped rehearsing. The sword, the ribbons, and the book went to three different hands, and Susanna opened hers at once. On Saturday you were the tenth at a Lenten table. The gloves were a short stitch and went on anyway. You showed a death with a spoon, and then it was a spoon again. Judith's walk was recognised. You did not count the coffer, and you did not pay a shop.
