- one sentence: You rode seven hours, said Gaunt twice in the afternoon, and wrote questions at the Bear.
- details: You left Wycombe in the dark and led the bay the last of the pull to the ridge. The morning was bright and cold, and the vale was blue below. You talked to nobody for seven hours. In the afternoon you said the name twice, to hear it, and you gave him no words after it. Mud to the hocks through the bottoms. Bread and a salt eel where the road levels toward Oxford. Lambs, and plough-teams on the heavy land. The towers were there with the sun going. In the Cornmarket you baited at a tavern with no name worth keeping, and you heard two masters dispute a comedy that ends without a marriage. You did not say you had written one. After that, nine miles in the dark, a carter's lantern for company part of the way. From the saddle you said that sixty-odd miles were behind you and that you had forgot what you meant to say to her, and you were glad of it. The Bear at Woodstock had a parcel-tie loose. You tied it again and wrote a few questions for the chronicle while supper was slow.
