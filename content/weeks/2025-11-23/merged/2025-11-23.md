Sunday, the xxiij of November 1595. Three of the clock. Two candles. The porch is gone from the window.

The fairy king, answering her ruined year with a place where nothing is ruined. For the sound first. The sense after.

I know a banke where the wilde time blowes,
Where Oxlips and the nodding Violet growes,

The thyme is off the bank above the Shottery brook. The oxlips are from the lane to Wilmcote. The violets are anybody's.

It came almost too sweetly. Left unstruck. I do not trust it.

Nothing after these two lines. The three sheets of the tradesmen stay as they were on Saturday. The head is still not written. This is not those sheets.
