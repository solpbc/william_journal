# Week of Sun 10 Aug 2025

## Reflection

You came home to a week of work with your hands. On Sunday you slept through the second lesson at Holy Trinity, ate dinner at the Sadlers', made three bark boats under the bridge, and walked the field path to Hewlands with Anne. On that path you started a tale about her stile and stopped. Bartholomew said the wheat was thin, and he told you to leave it. On Monday you stayed in the house. You read the orchard sheet and left the lines as they were. The shop had six pairs of sheep skins where the order was kid. Richard said he would walk them back after the frame went up.

On Tuesday the first bay went up cleanly. On the second lift your back went, and you stayed on your feet. Anne was sharp at the plot, where the carpenters could hear, and kind when you were on the bed. From Wednesday you lay on the floor. Your mother rubbed in bay and said a grace. The children's machine failed until Judith took off the piece Hamnet called essential, and then it worked a little better. You promised you would not turn their joke into a story for London. Your father was pleased with a fairy. Quiney talked about the town, and he did not mention the two pounds. Edmund asked how a king is put down. You told him that scene is cut, and you sent him back to school.

On Thursday the children played the orchard scene at the bedside. Judith's moon was a candle. Susanna said the girl is too quick to say yes. You left the lines as they were. Anne asked if this was the work, and she thought better of it than she had. On Friday you promised only the carrier on the eighteenth, which is already set. Anne told you that you have not looked at a real house. She named neither the house nor a price. Richard carried the six pairs of skins. On Saturday Richard cut a stick to your hip, and you walked to that length. Anne took you to the great house by the chapel. You asked her for three years. She said she will wait until she believes you. You did not ask the price, and you did not ask whose house it was.
