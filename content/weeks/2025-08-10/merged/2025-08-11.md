### Page

the xi of August 1595.

Wheat, new, at the Cross on Thursday: six-and-thirty shillings the quarter. Sadler's penny loaf is the size of that price. I weighed nothing. I am not the villain of it either, and neither is he.

Six pairs of sheep, written for kid. I walk them back myself, after the frame. The bundle is on the bench. Wednesday. No speech at the door.

Twenty shillings already in the oak. Not in the belt. Not to be told again.
