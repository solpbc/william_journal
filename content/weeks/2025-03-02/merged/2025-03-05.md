[01:40] john_heminges: The Mayor counts which house played in Lent and which did not. Let him count. We are the house that did not. I would rather be counted idle than counted clever. Clever is how a company explains a Wednesday to a clerk and then meets the clerk again.

[03:08] john_heminges: A season may have a name without every tradesman stopping at once. Lent is a name. Their afternoon is a trade. I can dislike the trade and still refuse to invent a fine for it. The Mayor's count is enough of a fine if we mean to be the house that stopped.

[07:10] hired_men: I have said the title twice. I will not say a sum to make a third. The flag was up. That is the end of my errand.

[07:58] john_heminges: Empty, and shut. Easter. If anyone asks what we did, we looked and we left and we did not play. I will not dress it richer than that.
