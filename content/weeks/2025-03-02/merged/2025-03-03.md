[01:42] will_kemp: The scholars play before the Queen for nothing, and we play before the scholars for a penny. Who's the fool?
[01:56] william_shakespeare: The fool is the man who goes where he is not asked. We played here. The Duke is back on the peg. I am not dressing him for Whitehall, and I am not sending a boy to look at a door.
[02:16] george_bryan: I was at Somerset House, waiting on my lord's clerk. The warrant for December's twenty pounds is drawn and moving. That is the news I waited for. It is not the coin. Nobody is to fetch it tonight, and nobody is to spend it in talk.
[02:40] sam_latchet: If it is moving, where does it arrive? A thing that moves ought to come to a hand. I have waited for worse things than money, and they arrived.
[02:58] william_shakespeare: It is going through clerks, Sam. That is a country where a boy can wait a long time. Thou art not to spend it. I am not to spend it. John is not in this room to write it down, and I will not write it for him.
[03:20] george_bryan: My lord's word, with the warrant, is to stop cleanly on Shrove Tuesday and hand the Mayor no stick. We play tomorrow. We do not play on Wednesday. The word is the stopping. It is not a quarrel about who else keeps open.
[03:44] richard_burbage: Then tomorrow is the last of this house, and the rock can have the Queen without our help. Thou wilt not go, Will. I heard thee. I am agreeing, which I dislike. I am agreeing because a stick in the Mayor's hand is a duller thing than a rock.
[04:08] will_kemp: A penny. I said a penny, and I meant the scholars' penny. I did not mean our supper, and I did not mean the Queen's twenty pounds. Let the twenty pounds move. Movement is not payment. I have been paid in movement, and it bought no ale.
[04:30] sam_latchet: Do they wear ruffs in a masque? I can picture a rock. I cannot picture the gentlemen, and a ruff is the part I understand.
[04:50] william_shakespeare: No ruffs of ours. We are not going. Thou mayst picture a rock. Thou mayst not say we were invited. Heard of is the whole of it.
[05:12] george_bryan: You have it right, Master Shakespeare. I will not improve a hearing into an invitation by telling it twice. The tilt was Saturday. The tourney was yesterday. The masque is tonight. We were at none of them. I was at Somerset House, which is a clerk's stair, and not a revel.
[05:36] richard_burbage: The afternoon was ours. Two Gentlemen, and the gown, and a yard that knew when to be quiet. I am not giving the afternoon to a rock I have not seen. Thou playedst the Duke as a man who means to be obeyed. Leave it there. Do not improve him at supper.
[06:00] william_shakespeare: I will leave him there. The name is enough, and I am not bringing a speech out to be admired. Tomorrow is the comedy again, the last time before the ashes. Thou wilt be in the yard at nine, Dick. Not at Whitehall, and not late.
[06:22] will_kemp: Nine in the yard, then. I am not singing tonight for anyone's feast, and I am not bringing a new tally to the morning. The old count can sit where it sits. The Duke did not need my help, and the Queen has scholars enough.
[06:44] george_bryan: Good. Leave the old count where it is. My lord asked for a clean stop, not a new song, and not a walk to the Mayor. Twenty pounds, drawn and moving. I will say it again, because a boy can spend a sentence if you leave it shining.
[07:06] sam_latchet: I will not spend it. I asked where a moving thing arrives. If the answer is nowhere tonight, I can carry nowhere. It is lighter than a rock, and it does not need a ruff.
[07:24] william_shakespeare: Nowhere tonight. Thou hast it, Sam. Bryan waited on the clerk so the rest of us need not wait on a door. I am for home. The house will ask which queen. I mean to answer before the question grows a sleeve.

[03:34] william_shakespeare: Last night's word. Tonight I am tired. The Duke can stay at the Keys, and I am not giving him to the house. You may both keep the eggs.
[03:54] dorcas_ansell: Then take the tiredness upstairs. I have the eggs. I have no ruff, and no queen in the kitchen, and no verdict on a play you will not tell. That is a sufficient Monday. Do not improve it on the stair.
