### Page

VERONA — out of Brooke, bought this day in
Paules ch:yard, vi d., lacking the last leaf
but one. Gib hath sat upon it already.

¶ He begins:
    There is beyonde the Alps, a towne of auncient fame,
    Whose bright renoune yet shineth cleare, Verona men it name
— a jog-trot. xiiij syllables & then xij. A man could plough to it.

¶ The matter:
    There were two auncient stockes, which Fortune high dyd place
    Above the rest, indewd with welth, and nobler of their race
— two houses. Alike. That is the thing. Not one good one bad.
  Alike in
                                    alike in

¶ Names he gives:
    Romeus, stricken. Romeo is better in the mouth.
    Iuliet
    Capilet, stricken. Capelet.
    Montagew
    Tybalt
    Paris, a Countie
    Mercutio — hath but one cold hand in Brooke, & no more.
        A man with a cold hand. Hm.
    a Nurce — he lets her talk. GOOD. let her talk more.
    Fryer Lawrence

¶ His time: moneths. They are wedded & meet in secret a whole
  winter in Brooke. too long. ?? how short can it be made
         a moneth       a weeke        — dayes —

¶ He preaches. To the Reader he sayth they are punished for
  lust & for hearkening to friars. I do not think so.
  I think nobody is punished. I think it falls on them.

¶ First meeting. hands. pilgrims?

¶ How word goes between the houses. Brooke hath a letter,
  and it goes astray.

¶ Two houses — a plan:

      [ CAP. ]                         [ MOUNT. ]
         |          the streete           |
         |   servants — swords — heat     |
         +----------- PRINCE ------------+

¶ One thing seen, walking home, a link-boy passing a doorway:
      a light in a vault. Not day. A torch brought into
      a tomb & the tomb made a feasting chamber by it.
  keep.

¶ NOT YET. The King first (ask Ric: Field for his Holinshed).
  This after. Summer. It wants heat.

Item pd. for the booke vi d.
