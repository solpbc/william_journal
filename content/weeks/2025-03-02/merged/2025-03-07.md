[02:44] dorcas_ansell: Good. I will not have a telling at my board. I have had the forsworn gentlemen, and I have said what I think of them, and I am not opening a new ruling for houses I have not been given. Threepence covers the fish. It does not cover a performance.
[03:06] william_shakespeare: You will not be given them. Anne's sheet is in the chest. I am not opening that either. The letter that went has gone. There is not a new one this morning. The chest stays shut, and the fish does not require it.

[05:40] dorcas_ansell: I should hope not. Chronicles are heavy, and Alse has the fish to clear, which is learning enough for a maid on a Friday. Wipe when you are done. A great clearing can wait until there is a Saturday worth of mess. I have looked at your desk. It is not there yet.
[06:00] william_shakespeare: Saturday can have the mess, if Saturday wants it. Today has the fish and the blank. I am not filling the blank to please a breakfast, and I am not striking anything, because there is nothing yet written in a list to strike.
