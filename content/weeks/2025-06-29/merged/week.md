# Week of Sun 29 Jun 2025

## Reflection

You began at a barred door and ended with a brawl on a clean sheet. Sunday was St Peter's, three heads, and a man at the porch who was told Norwich. About nine a sound began to the south-east. You took the other end of the bar, kept the one candle on the boards behind the bed, and told yourself to remember that dark for a street among serving-men. Brooke stayed on the shelf.

Monday the streets to Holywell were swept, and the gate looked at every face. The afternoon line was struck. You read the king at James's table. At the hollow crown Dick's hand stayed flat. At the deposing nobody moved. John asked where this week was written down as allowed. James named Friday's gallows. George said send it whole and let the Master cut. Kemp came at the fourth act, heard the end, and was late. Dick took the forty leaves under his arm. At home the horn was twopence and the board was threepence, and the loaf was still a thumb short. Tuesday you rehearsed the comedy you played on Wednesday, then played Egeon in Errors after two dark days. The flag had taken under three hundred, and the yard heard every word. Margery had nine shillings, and the house had been quiet. Dick gave Garnish the leaves in a shirt. Six days, and seven shillings set by. You wrote Anne. Wednesday you played, and Brooke stayed on the shelf. The Rose was dark. Thursday Edith finished a cuff. Arthur's shins were out. You played the old man in Errors again, to a yard that heard every word.

Friday you came home from Shrew, and then you went to the gate. The heading names unlawful assemblies, Greenwich, and this day. You copied the heading, and alone you said that a playhouse yard is two thousand people gathered by a flag. Saturday Brooke came down for the first time since March. Brooke was open at alike in. Ninety lines, the jest before anybody is out, the room already hot. Ten shillings at the reckoning, written as received. The roads are Monday's question. You came home by Bishopsgate Street.
