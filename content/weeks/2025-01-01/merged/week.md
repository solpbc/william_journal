# Week of Wed 1 Jan 2025

## Reflection

The year opened on a leaf still headed 1594. You wrote the Queen down for twenty pounds, not yet had, and the coffer at twenty-four pound ten. You did not count the coffer again. Under the sum you set three winter marks, and when the pan dropped you wrote that the year begins with demands from below. You paid them that morning: sixpence for firing and washing, threepence for Wednesday's board. The fifth act is begun. It is not finished. The oath is on the page. The Worthies break up in laughter. Nothing follows the laugh. Berowne has no answer yet that will hold. You did not read him to anyone.

Thursday you were Egeon. You spoke, and then you sat two hours in a cold parlour. Kemp got up on a hamper, with a stool for a chariot, and the line quieted the room even when he was mocking. Heminges said they will be paid as slowly as we are. Pope said she is keeping two dogs and feeding neither. A stuffed glove ended the second roar. What they cannot out-bellow they will have to out-write, and that stayed at home with the sheets.

Friday the same twenty pounds were still expected. At the Split Hazel they bought nothing. Alice said so. Kemp laughed loudest and paid for his own, and you paid fourpence. You played in the Shrew. At her board the penny loaf was short of the notch by the width of a finger. She would not give a pin for a play that begins in a broken oath. You told her four gentlemen and four ladies, and you wrote washing. Saturday the Prince of Purpoole rode to Crosby Place. You stood among the backs and laughed with Burbage over the Night of Errors. You did not go in. She named the City. Those gentlemen had your Errors on Innocents' night, and they keep a better court than Navarre's. The gallery was not all in. No share was struck, because Monday still plays. You lined four payments. You did not write a total.
