- title: Stood at Crosby Place and lined four payments
- one sentence: You stood among the backs at Crosby Place and came home with four payments lined.
- details: In the morning the ladies were in the park on the page, and you still had no sentence for them. At eleven you stood at the mouth of the close, a hundred yards from the porch, among citizens' backs. The Prince of Purpoole's mock-court rode up to Crosby Place under torch-smoke. Sir John Spencer, a furred gown on the steps, received a law-student king with a straight face, and did not speak to you. Mistress Ansell said he will dine a play-prince at his table and shut the door on a player, and that that is the City. You and Burbage laughed over the Night of Errors. At home you lined four payments on the right-hand side: Wednesday's board, the Split Hazel, Friday's board, firing and washing. You did not open a new count.
