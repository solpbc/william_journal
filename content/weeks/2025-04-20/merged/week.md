# Week of Sun 20 Apr 2025

## Reflection

Easter week put you back in the open house and kept you, for one day, at the desk. On Sunday you took the bread and the cup. You said nothing, and at dinner the loaf stayed off the notch because it was Easter. Master Verbeke said what the table was thinking. On Monday the yard was timber before the swan went up, Sam sang clear, and the hush at the messenger was a house of two thousand. Margery Pell's fifty-two shillings stayed hers. You did not write a Rose receipt on any morning you staged. On Saturday you set the six titles down. You wrote no sums.

Wednesday you said thirty-one to the grass and to nobody else. You heard of the Garter trumpets. You did not go near them. The Shrew was on at the Theatre. You were not in it. You did not report it as a thing you had seen. Thursday you were an old Roman and dead by the fourth act, the hired men were ten shillings apiece. There was no total, and a boy brought Gilbert's aye. You wrote four lines after the play. You did not answer the question from the fifth. Friday Burbage took the long speech at the Jackdaw. You named nobody. Saturday the share was thirty-eight shillings. Easter week brings the best share since February. The gingerbread was once. The book stayed shut.
