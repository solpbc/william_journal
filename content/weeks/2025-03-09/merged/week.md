# Week of Sun 9 Mar 2025

## Reflection

You began the week by finding the postscript you had missed, and you ended it with thirty-seven shillings and sixpence that is yours. Anne put fourteen shillings and sixpence in a darker ink under the children's news. The first answer went into the candle. The one you kept lets her judge what the house must pay, and it is still in the room. Richard turned twenty-one. Nothing was sent. Gilbert read three shops twice and called five pound near, and the whittawer's line on his ticket is the same fourteen shillings and sixpence. Nothing of it was paid.

The company left the Cross Keys and paid thirteen shillings and fourpence. You would not tell Nick how wide the stage is, because you have not paced it. Nine of Kemp's lines went into the book on Wednesday, two came out, and his exit cue is his cap off on the ninth line. At Bishopsgate you heard three hundred and twenty-five boards and twenty-four shillings. On Saturday the warrant was twenty pound on a cloth, and your share was thirty-seven shillings and sixpence, entered as received. The stock kept its own share. Edith's eight shillings waits until Thursday, out of that share. Seven yards of peach velvet wait until autumn. You have named a hired horse as the way to carry the share home.

The Verona page still has a query, a struck month, and a torch in a vault. No one in it spoke. Daniel's queen at the window is a woman, and the book stayed at two shillings on Waterson's board. The Monday ink was the old receipt. One meal was threepence. You struck Costard off the list, and then the Queen's money, and the letter is still on it.
