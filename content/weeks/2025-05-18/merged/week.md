# Week of Sun 18 May 2025

## Reflection

You began the week with a king still coming down a wall, and you ended it with three sentences on the ground and an order for a glass. Sunday you left the page, stood through church, and scarcely spoke at supper. The bill was two shillings and eightpence, and the jug. You did not divide it. Monday you were an old Roman in Titus, dead by the fourth act. The Monday copper was on. You meant to stand in the Rose yard. Tuesday you sorted four uses of the word king, and the margin says that some are only a cough. You were not in the Shrew. You stood by a post on the right while a man in a lion's skin made the yard count. Henslowe, at the bridge foot, pays by the play, six pound, and he never argues the jig. You are paid by the share. You came home to a looking-glass. You did not say it to him. Wednesday you wrote the three sentences. The king is come down. The lords look at his hands. He looks at the ground. There is no fool in the book. Kemp has not been told. Thursday you were the Ghost, a handful of flour in the hair. At the porch she knew your hand. You said Mistress. You marked a cross, and no name. Friday Susanna's letter stayed unwritten. She is still eleven. You were an old man of the house in the Shrew, and off before the end. Saturday the share was six-and-thirty, four shillings under last Saturday's forty. You told Burbage he shall have a looking-glass when you have written how the king gets there. Garnish gave the week as nineteen lines, and a noise like a goose. You shut the chronicle and left Gib on it. The margin is empty. The glass is not bought.
