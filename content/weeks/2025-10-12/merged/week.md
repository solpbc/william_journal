# Week of Sun 12 Oct 2025

## Reflection

The jaw took the week. The parts that were yours went to other men. Sunday you sat the parish hour at St Helen's. At four the supper was peas boiled down, a sop of bread in warm ale, and a baked apple. You complained of all three. She said the food had already yielded more than you had. Alse began a riddle. Master Verbeke finished the sound of it in his own tongue. You laughed, and held the jaw. Evening prayer had already been said at the table. You were to be in bed by eight. The jaw woke you in the night.

Monday you struck eleven lines in the Friar's cell. Richard Cowley came as far as the foot of the stair, with today's part already learned. The second cut stands. You stayed in, and you called her broth water. At half past six Gilbert was twenty-nine. The money had gone down safe by the carrier of the second. The cheese stayed with him. The frame raised in August is thatched. The tooth comes out tomorrow at ten, at twelve pence. He would have done it for six with the shop pliers. You asked him to keep you the rind.

Tuesday, before dawn, you wrote one word, weaver. At ten Yaxley set you on a joined stool, told you to hold it and think on your sins, and you got as far as the second sin. Three roots, like a small black claw. Twelve pence. You walked home with a clout. Callers stopped at the foot of the stair. Alse went up once with salt water. At dusk the tyrant was gone, the size of a pea. You entered the twelve pence.

Wednesday Heminges stopped at the foot of the stair with a custard from his wife. Pope will play Gaunt. He waits till the garden in the third act. He will die at Ely House and rise a gardener. You said he will not get the laugh. Garnish has cut the long cue. The Rose has something of a woman and a wonder. You let them. You wrote four words. Pope has Thursday.

Thursday you stayed in. Pope had the king. The jaw is tender.

Friday you looked at a dawn that was already written, and you shut it. At seven in the evening Florio called from a house in black. Sir Thomas Heneage died this day at the Savoy. My lord is with her. You sent your duty, and then you asked who signs. A widow must first cast up the accounts. The Chaucer waits. She asked whether he made a good end. You wrote one line. Sir Thomas is dead. Who pays.

Saturday you walked up for the reckoning only. The share was thirty-six shillings. Three galleries. Heminges and Bryan will wait within the week. The sun was down before five. The Theatre will be played cold. She had lit the hall candle at five. You ate broth. Shoreditch mud is on the hem. You marked St Luke. You were in bed before the Bow bell, with the cat on your feet. The jaw is still not quiet.
