No tis not so deepe as a well, nor so wide as a Church doore, but tis inough, twill serue.

Aske for me to morrow, and you shall finde me a graue man.

[eight lines, one stroke through them, given and taken back]
I had a iest to last me to the bridge,
And one more for the gate, and one for thee.
Keepe them. I shall not want them where I goe.
A man may owe a laugh and pay it deare.
Tell them I went out talking, as I came.
The rest is yours. I haue no more of it.
Bury the point, and let the wit goe free.
I would haue said the rest. Giue it him back.

A poxe of your houses

No other blot. The talker stops here. The rest of the play goes on without him.
