# Week of Sun 31 Aug 2025

## Reflection

You stood at St Helen's, and you came home. At three you struck three beginnings that were only London, and you kept the short letter. It says you are come safe, that the back mends, and that you have counted twenty pound fifteen shillings, and a share of six-and-twenty since. You did not add those figures. You sealed it for Anne, by Hobbins, on the eleventh. At supper Mistress Ansell said she had heard no walking at all, and that a short letter to a wife has less room for lies in it. At night you said you would not improve it. On Monday the seal was still shut. You had a mind to take out the word live. The word stayed in. At two you played an old man's side in The Shrew. You did not open the Verona papers.

On Tuesday Kettleby called from Somerset House. My lord is with the court in Surrey, and he will come unlooked-for on the second of October to see the new king. The Lord Treasurer cannot stir hand nor foot. You told Bryan and Heminges after Two Gentlemen. Bryan named the weak king and the men who would hold the pen. Heminges said you play it as licensed. Nobody added anything. On Wednesday you tried the rose on Alse. It was not from the sheet. She said it would not sell the same, cried in Cheap for stinkweed. At dinner Mistress Ansell approved the friar until she heard the marriage was that afternoon, without the fathers' leave. She said it will end in a churchyard. The loaf was a full thumb short of the notch, at a penny. Gib slept on Brooke, and the chronicle stayed under its cloth. At eleven you wrote two leaves. No name is on them. You turned the second face down.

On Thursday Phillips stopped you at Sam's entrance. Sam has read his roll. He has not read the play. From the door, he has seen his lady not sleep, and that was enough. The afternoon was Richard the Third, and you were out of the gown by four. At Old Swan you heard that Drake and Hawkins had sailed. Gannet said six-and-thirty sail and four thousand men. The merchants' letters say seven-and-twenty ships and some five-and-twenty hundred. You did not buy the ballad. You said it would be spring before we know. On Friday the post had Crack Me This Nut beside your house's Two Gentlemen. You read them. You did not cross. You began the street and left the talker talking. You did not yet know the how. On Saturday morning you knew. The wound is under the boy's own arm, and it is hardly a wound. You struck eight lines you had given him. You did not tell Phillips. Two Gentlemen was at two. The share was one-and-thirty shillings, and the coffer stayed at twenty pound fifteen.
