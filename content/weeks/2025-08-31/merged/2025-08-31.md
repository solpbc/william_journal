[the second beginning, one stroke through it]
On Friday the Rose had a king who never doubts, and fills the house. I went to learn the size of him. That is the news, and it is nobody's at home.

[the third beginning, one stroke through it]
Hobbins is due on the eleventh. The loaf is dear here. The yard has been mended with paint. I am well, and the back is nothing.

[the fourth, kept]
Anne. I am come safe and my back mends. I have counted: twenty pound fifteen shillings, and a share of six-and-twenty since. I do not promise. I tell thee what I think on, walking: that I work here and do not live here, and that the garden wants digging before the house wants glass. Three years, if the playhouse stand and the City suffer us. Say if that is too long. I was told not to come and came, and am glad of it. Tell Susanna she is right and I shall not mend it; tell Judith the moon was well played; tell Hamnet the sword is no sharper than his. Thy Will. From London this last of August.
