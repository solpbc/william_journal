## Pulse

- title: The price of oats
- one sentence: You walked the long day, asked the price of oats, and let Hobbins call you a carrier with one horse too many.
- details: There is no morning scene. You walked every mile, up out of Oxford and on. At Wycombe he told the day backwards: the spare offered at the foot of the hill, refused, and the back better going than sitting by the top. In March you had ridden the other way like a man who had been paid. He knows the houses by what they owe. A widow at Wheatley will not take forty pound. That is her house. You opened your mouth for the house by the chapel and asked the price of oats instead. The answer took the rest of the talking. At Beaconsfield, by the last light, the road pack and the boots were on the inn floor. Bread and bacon were eleven pence. You checked them against what was left, and you wrote the eleven and not the rest. Over the ale he said they say at the Bell you make kings. You said you make them and your fellows wear them. There is a share in it. He said you are a carrier, with the one horse too many.
