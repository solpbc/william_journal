# Week of Sun 17 Aug 2025

## Reflection

You stood through the sermon because sitting was worse, and in the orchard you gave Edmund two cues and took the page back. Supper was ten at a cold pie. Judith asked for a ballad with a murder in it. Susanna asked for the answer on too quick, written. Hamnet was told the sword matches the lath one for edge, and is a deal heavier. Edmund was told to finish school. His place is the work at home, and the living is that work. Joan said you might take the back and leave the rest. Your father put twenty shillings and a spine in one toast, and the table let it pass. Your mother put the bay grease in the pack at that supper. After, on the bench, the talk was a hand, a pair of shoes, and Joan. You told yourself you live here.

Monday Anne said mend the back, and when you write, say something. Richard handed the parcel up at the bridge. You rode the spare as far as Shipston, were glad of it, and said otherwise. Oats there were a penny the peck over last month, and the hay was gone. You left the old stones on the right. Supper and a shared bed at Chipping Norton were fourteen pence. In the yard you guessed a house at forty pound and at a hundred, and you took the guess back. The left strap wants shortening. Tuesday you walked. A hired rider's horse went lame, and Hobbins kept on past it. You wrote the friar on the tables, wisely and slow, and at Oxford you read the orchard sheets, found the moon and the ladder waiting off the page. Supper was nine pence. You left the scholar his wager.

Wednesday was the long walk, and the back was better going than sitting. A widow at Wheatley holds her house against forty pound. That is her house. You asked the price of oats, and the house by the chapel is still to price. At Beaconsfield the road pack and the boots were on the inn floor. Bread and bacon were eleven pence, entered as the supper alone. Hobbins said you have only the one horse too many. Thursday the corn was in at Uxbridge, and the gleaners stayed at their work. From a rise you saw the smoke lying along the river, and at St Giles, walking, you said it was one scene and a house beyond the purse. At the Bell Gilbert said you look like Father, and that your father owes Sadler. The debt stayed his. You paid Hobbins his two shillings, and the road was nine shillings, all told. Friday the welcome was a basket of washing. The coffer, ruled, is twenty pound fifteen shillings. Gib is still on the Holinshed. You told Mistress Ansell the house is the fourth act. At the Theatre the posts were new marble and the heavens blue, and Garnish put Gaunt's roll in your hand. Burbage may have the orchard on Sunday, and he is to con the king's roll by the hole. Saturday you opened Brooke. You wrote the list fair, gave Susanna's verdict to the friar the scene talks past, and stopped before the letter. The fair is Sunday. The doors open Monday.
