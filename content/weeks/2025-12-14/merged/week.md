# Week of Sun 14 Dec 2025

## Reflection

You wrote the Christmas letter in the dark hour, and the sheet was still open when you stopped. You told Anne you will be at Richmond, and in the porch you told the house the same, and she refused a pie on a rumour. You did not name one thing for each of them. On Thursday Hobbins read the pack back and corded it, and he brought her letter the other way. You heard two faults, a street, a sword, and a porch, and you read the cheaper pane twice, laughed with your shoulders, and sent Sunday's letter on as it stood.

You drew an ass's head, and you wrote that the jaw must move or the voice will be lost in it, and you wrote his father down for canvas, paste, and light. Field wanted a third poem for an idle Friday. You told him the work in hand is a duke, four lovers, and a weaver, and you said you would come on Sunday for the goose. You kept the second volume. The lovers went a third time on Monday and a fourth on Saturday. Nick came in under the note and held. Saturday stayed the fourth.

On Tuesday the beard came off close for the chair. Nobody named a price on the stair. The Master left the Nurse, took forty lines, and signed three books for Richmond. He did not name the king. Two cloaks went out for the Prince and for Paris. On Wednesday Edith reached the fastening at the back of Sam's coat, let out Nick's seam, and two stale rolls went into the brazier. You answered at once and found it harder than a page. On Friday morning you copied Cowley's part and corrected the cue. At Field's door James named six hundred pounds for the stone hall, come the spring, and you kept the six hundred between yourself, James, and Dick. At the Theatre at two the news was that the Queen had come up to Whitehall, and nobody could say why. On Saturday the court was at Whitehall. You left the tilt-boat standing for Monday, and you refused a share under the week.
