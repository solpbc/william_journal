## Pulse

- title: Heard six hundred pounds for a stone hall
- one sentence: At Field's door James named six hundred pounds for a stone hall, and at two the news came that the Queen is at Whitehall, and nobody can say why.
- details: Before you went out you copied a part for Cowley, Benvolio, and you corrected the cue without writing a reason in the margin. You came out of the shop with a quire of Rochelle crown under your arm, at the old trade, fourpence halfpenny. In a thin snow in the lane you met James and Dick, James with a carpenter's rule badly hidden under his cloak. There was no pretending. At the printing-house door, over a hot spiced gill, he swore you both to the cup. The Upper Frater can be had outright, six hundred pounds, come the spring, when the money is found. He would roof the company against the winter, the Lord Mayor, and Giles Allen together. You said it was a great deal of money. He said it was a great deal of roof. Dick said nothing. You looked at the stone lancets and did not say what you think of those neighbours. You did not tell Field. You did not write the sum in the book. The afternoon's bill was the Shrew at two, with no mark that you play. You went to the Theatre all the same. At the tiring-house door the gatherer brought the day's other news: the Queen has come up suddenly to Whitehall, and nobody can say why. Heminges said a court does not move in December for nothing. Dick caught your eye, and nobody spoke of a hall. You heard the first act from the door and were home before the candle.
