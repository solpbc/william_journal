# Week of Sun 16 Mar 2025

## Reflection

You heard seven heads and one turning of the glass. The first meal was threepence. The loaf was a finger short. Anne's letter stayed on the board. Brooke stayed open. Saturday's part stayed had.

On Monday Richard Burbage gave up the old lath sword at Holywell. It had killed four Richmonds. The boy does not have it. The book took a riddle-book at twopence, ribbons at fivepence, and gloves at two shillings and fourpence. The sum laid out is two shillings and elevenpence. The bay is bespoken from Wednesday the twenty-sixth at twelvepence a day and his fodder. On Tuesday the chair took one pale peg. Master Kemp is not to sit on it. You were home by eleven. Florio said the paper was received, and that my lord is glad you prosper. You did not ask for more. You did not copy the proverb out.

On Wednesday Field said plays are a waste, and a waste with a crowd in it, and he lent you the second volume. You read four columns standing and carried the book home. That night you closed Verona and opened a heading, Of Kings, and set five lines under it. On Thursday you sent four lines and twopence. You leave on Wednesday the twenty-sixth, by Oxford, and you look to be at the bridge on Friday by dusk. The sixty shillings stayed in the canvas. Edith's eight shillings came out of the stock, and you struck the owing line. On Friday you said the names and wrote that it was not yet. On Saturday you paid the second threepence, heard a strain played twice, wrote four lines, and put a cross by the day.
