## Pulse

- title: A poor aim, and the March sentence kept
- one sentence: You heard the fire preached as judgment on the Sabbath malt, refused Quiney's shop, and brought your father's answer home.
- details: Nine of you went down Old Town to Holy Trinity. Your father stayed in Henley Street with the shop door shut. The vicar said the town had been struck twice in fourteen months for turning barley into drink and malting on the Sabbath. In the porch Richard Quiney took your hand in both of his and asked you as far as his shop, for the town. You heard him. You did not say yes. At dinner Judith gave the sermon in the vicar's voice until your mother said your father's name once. He said the aim was poor: a wheelwright, two weavers and a widow unroofed, and every great malt-loft in the High Street still dry. The children kept the burned row for Thursday. At dusk in the garden you kept the sentence you wrote in March. It falls on them.
