# Week of Sun 16 Nov 2025

## Reflection

You let a decision stand that had already been made a hundred miles off. The wash is fourpence a week, paid, and the letter that says so is still in the house. The Christmas money is three uses on the sheet: house, shop, and what she may spend. On Sunday night the coffer was opened, both locks, and the foot of the leaf got no total. What is spoken for is plainer than a total: twenty shillings for the Christmas quarter, sixty shillings for home, the hose, the river, the brief, and two pounds out since August.

Monday was a long walk on the Queen's day. You paid twelve pence to stand a hundred yards off, and the man you were bid to see unhorsed kept his seat. Peele asked a groat and got a nod. The fire at the corner marked the day. You did not tell the house why you had gone. One line went on the tables. The rest of the week was the work that line pointed at, begun late. You did not finish it. You read the lovers on Wednesday and stayed home from the Shrew. You were Gaunt on Tuesday and Navarre on Thursday. On Friday the leaf turned the last two days round, and you played both as the leaf had them. Saturday's reckoning was eight-and-twenty shillings, said once.

Field sold you a quire and a piece of news, and you brought only the quire home. At supper the loaf was two fingers short. You learned what a bottom is, and you wrote that one word. On Saturday morning the three sheets existed, and the ass's head was still only in the talk. At the Jackdaw Kemp read them, roared, a jug was dropped, and he said thou. You did not promise the rest of the play. Garnish shut the book. Monday at six is set. You have paid nothing toward it.
