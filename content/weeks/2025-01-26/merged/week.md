# Week of Sun 26 Jan 2025

## Reflection

You began at St Helen's, where Master Thwaite meant to be brief and reached fourteenthly, and Sir John Spencer sat three rows ahead. He did not turn. The loaf was a finger short. The board was threepence. Firing was named at sixpence and stayed on the plate. The sentence to Anne stayed on the desk. Nothing went to the Bell. At Holywell, over mutton, Derby's wedding at Greenwich was the whole of the talk. You said a wedding wants a play as a feast wants a fool. James said a wedding wants paying for. Nothing was written down. On Monday you stayed in. The horn was twopence. You began a leaf and dated it in the old year. No name stood at the head. Titus and the first Tamburlaine went on, and you stayed in. At night John called from Aldermanbury. Four ladies and a dairymaid, three boys, and one of them the page. You offered to cut the dairymaid. Kemp had already seen her. Two boys out of Pope's parish at three shillings the week. They were not paid. Sincler in black for the messenger. John takes the schoolmaster. He said the Princess wants a gown. You did not buy it.

On Tuesday you read the book at Dick's, every voice, and you read Costard badly so Kemp would mend him. Abel had copied seventeen parts in six days. Pope lost the third word of a letter, and the table could not save him. Kemp unrolled his part against Dick's. Neither of you called a winner. Augustine asked for the songs by Friday. Nick asked whether the Princess weeps. She governs. You ate little and played in the Shrew. You did not name the roll. You did not go to Set at Maw. On Wednesday you pointed the two notes. You did not copy them. Under the stage, in a sheet and flour, you cried the old line in the last of the light. The horses coughed. The quiet gallery was Richard's, from the stage. Kemp said you were more fearful a fortnight ago. Sincler asked how a man stands so as to look dead, and you told him to think of the reckoning. On Thursday you put the pointing in Augustine's hand at nine, which was soon enough. You opened Errors and waited. After the play, Lent on the fifth of March, five weeks, twenty-eight afternoons, and John wants four of them for the comedy. Richard wants three full weeks for Berowne, for he never stops talking. You fixed Thursday the twentieth of February, and a stop on Shrove Tuesday, the fourth of March. A comedy needs no jig. That was the first small cloud. The Rose will play till they're stopped. The Rose has no mayor sleeping next door to its poet.

On Friday you wrote a second leaf, and the fish was threepence. The leaf still had no name. The loaf wants a finger. The Shrew and Grecian Comedy went on, and you stayed in. You did not write the packet, and you did not set anything beside the horn. On Saturday you went down from the Keys to the hill. Fifteen barges, music, trumpets and guns, and a student playing a prince. A man in front described what everyone could see. Kemp was better on the people trying to see round him. Augustine took the trumpet. Richard studied the step out of the boat. You kept an oar lifted almost upright, and you said the rest of it on the slope: you do it for a penny and are rogues by statute. You were back for Errors. At the reckoning John set next week. He put no share on the leaf. You did not open the coffer. Edith's eight shillings is still owing. The Queen's twenty pound is still not had. The gown is still not bought. Tomorrow is Candlemas. You did not send the letter.
