## Pulse

- title: Stood for fifteen barges and played Errors
- one sentence: You stood on Tower Hill for a student in a prince's cloak and played Errors at two.
- details: You went down from the Keys. The street between was not a place you stopped. On the hill, fifteen barges came up from Greenwich with music, trumpets and guns, and the Gray's Inn prince landed at the Tower to ride through the City. A man in front described what everyone could see. Kemp was more use on the people trying to see round that man. Augustine hummed the trumpet to steal for an entrance. Richard studied how a man in borrowed majesty gets out of a boat. You kept one other picture: an oar lifted almost upright. You said it afterward. A student playing a prince, and the Tower's guns answer him as if it were so. We do it for a penny and are rogues by statute. The picture is Richard's russet back, at the left, and his face is not in it. You were home, then in Errors at two. The Rose had the first Caesar. You did not go. At the reckoning John set next week and put no share on the leaf. The coffer stayed shut. Edith's eight shillings is still owing. The Queen's twenty pound is still not had. The boys' three shillings were not paid out. The gown was not bought. The packet is still not written, and nothing was set beside the horn.
