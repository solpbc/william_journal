## Pulse

- title: Tilney has cut the deposing
- one sentence: Tilney cut the deposing and sealed the book, and at the stage door you told Richard, who kept the glass.
- details: You went out by Moorgate and Smithfield with John, further from home than since Midsummer, and the walk is not a stop of its own. At St John's he had the king open at the fourth act. He wrote the margin, left the bishop's protest, and sent the scene from the quarrel to the Tower. You said it was all in the chronicle. He said two thousand would hear it standing. John counted down the seven shillings set by on the first of July. The foul papers stay in the coffer, the deposing and all. On the walk home you said only that he had cut the glass. You said it again at the Theatre's stage door, to Richard, who answered with the cut speech and would not give the pearwood glass back. The Knave this afternoon does not have you in it, and you went home.
