## Reflection

You began with one turn of a sermon and the pew in the north aisle. The same afternoon you said the people who had gone into the country were sensible, and that you are writing a boy sick for a woman who never comes on the stage. You let that serve. The letter is still on the board.

On Monday, after a half yard of Love's Labour's Lost, the sharers chose the open days over the wagon. Last day is Saturday the twenty-sixth. The house opens again on Monday the twenty-fifth of August. Kemp was against. You said the silent month suits you better than it suits the hired men. At the Jackdaw you gave Richard a pearwood glass. He looked into it. At nine the pain was a city.

The middle of the week was the brawl's own street, a minute with Field, and Titus at the door. Thursday Tilney cut the deposing, sealed the book, and counted the seven shillings that were already set by. The foul papers stayed in the coffer. At the stage door you told Richard that the deposing was cut, and Richard kept the pearwood glass. Friday Florio gave you Verona. Saturday's share was fourteen shillings. A cup stopped short of a drink. At the stairs you stayed for the moon.
