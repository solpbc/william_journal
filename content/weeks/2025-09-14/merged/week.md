# Week of Sun 14 Sep 2025

## Reflection

You stood three pews from the Mayor at St Helen's. He answered the litany short and cold. Neither of you looked. The north aisle was full again. A dark head under a plain hood did not turn. You learned nothing from the sermon. The jaw had a pulse, and the night's writing was a cross. At dinner the loaf was a penny and a full thumb short. The back was not eased. At evening a pavan came through an open shutter, a hard place in the left hand, and then the lid. In the close you said you know one.

On Monday the back was eased on the stair. You did not make a feast of it. The tooth was the same tooth. You played the Knave at two. The part was not named. On Tuesday you kept the king out of the garden. Kemp was not sent for. Sixteen days stand before the yard. Phillips was in the house. Verona was not in anyone's mouth. The paint of Titus was still red on three of the hired men when Kettleby called. The sense was play on. Nothing my lord says is in writing. Heminges wrote the two words and looked at them.

On Wednesday your jaw was the size of an egg. Field would not tell you the word. You said you would write tonight. You did not answer the Holinshed. On the Bishopsgate post you read The New World's Tragedy twice. A new world, a fortnight after Drake sailed. In the afternoon you cried revenge through a clove. On Thursday at Paul's the margin said Sweet Shakspeare, and the rest was too small to take. Harrison had told two customers. The Lucrece is respected. Field said the praise was for the poems, and that you are wasted on players. You shut the book gently. You did not buy it. You have it by heart. Three years ago a dying man put you in print as a crow in borrowed feathers, and you could not decide which was harder. At the Jackdaw Kemp stopped eating and tried the name once.

On Friday you played Titus. The part was not named. The hired men had the other name by then. After the play the clove did less. You did not call that a cure. You changed my for thy on a leaf, sat, and changed it back. The leaf has no name. Then you wrote Quiney about the certificate. No sum is on the paper. A sonnet can lie by leaving a man out. A letter cannot. On Saturday the name stuck. Sam said it as a title. You had been got by heart in two days. Kemp was first on the Queen's paper. You mean to be comfortable there. Pope was equally wronged. Garnish wrote the name and scraped it off. The share was twenty-nine shillings. The shilling was a hurried change. It was not a thief. The correction stands on the line. The coffer was not opened. It stands at twenty pound fifteen.
