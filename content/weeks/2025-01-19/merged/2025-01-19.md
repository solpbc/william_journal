### Page

Propped on two bolsters. The foul papers across my knees. I struck the twenty. They were conceit, and they were stale, Berowne to Rosaline, and every syllable too neat for a boy that tall.

In their place, quicker, and rougher:

Rosaline. You are too tall for so small a vow.
Berowne. Then the vow must grow, or I must stoop and shame it.

The stillness lets me hear the false ones. These two I can hear. The songs are not on this leaf. The last line is not on this leaf. I am not writing either from a fever, and the fever is not gone yet. It will be gone by night or it will not. I am not the judge of it from a bolster.
