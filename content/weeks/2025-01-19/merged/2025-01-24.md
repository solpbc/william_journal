## Pulse

- title: Carried the book to St John's, and Tilney signed
- one sentence: You carried the book to St John's, and Tilney struck two oaths, signed the last leaf, and took seven shillings of the company's.
- details: Heminges came to the lodging and you took the book as far as the door. Richard the Third at two was not yours. At the old priory Tilney read with a pen in his hand while his men stitched masquing stuff for Sunday's wedding. He struck two oaths and nothing else. The Queen's ladies may hear God's name in church. Elsewhere, by Jove serves. You told him Navarre was only the name, and that it was in the air. He smiled once, at Armado's melancholy, and he asked whether any French lord glanced at a living ambassador. You said no. He signed. Heminges counted out seven shillings on that table. They were the company's. On the way home he entered it, and you said you had never been cut so little and felt it so much. By afternoon you were home. The Rose bill was Faustus. You did not go. The letter was still on the table.
