### Page

Sunday night.

The king is still talking, and every man around him sounds as fine as he does. I have caught it, and I have not mended it. If I mend it tonight I shall only make the uncles prettier. Tomorrow the house plays, and I am not in it. The differences can wait until I have a morning.

Kemp has not seen this page. There is no fool in the king, and I am not going to show him that to prove it.

Anne's letter is read twice. I have no better answer than the one I have already given her. I am not writing one.

Gib is on the second volume. I wrote to the left of his paw.
