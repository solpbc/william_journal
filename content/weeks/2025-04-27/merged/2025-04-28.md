### Page

Home before the second bell. The house is at the Shrew. I am not in it. I came away, and the king was where I left him, on the left of the paw.

York sounds as fine as the nephew. Gaunt sounds as fine as both of them. I am improving every voice into the same eloquence. That is my convenience, and I can hear it. If I correct them tonight I shall only pretty the correction.

Tomorrow. The differences are tomorrow's work.

Sunday is set. The chronicle does not go. The comedy goes if the book is in my hand on Saturday. Gib has not moved.
