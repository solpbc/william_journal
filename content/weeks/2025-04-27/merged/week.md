# Week of Sun 27 Apr 2025

## Reflection

The week came in on a letter four days late. Gilbert had carried it since Thursday in the other doublet, and you answered the porch with the sentence your father had already chosen. The frame in Chapel Street is roofed. The money came safe. There was no sum to repeat. Susanna was correct, Hamnet had won a race, and Judith's man with his arms out was named by her sister. You read the sheet twice. You did not write Anne a better answer than the one you have already given her.

The king took the mornings. You caught the uncles as fine as the nephew, and on Tuesday's second pass you gave the breaths back: York short, Gaunt long, the King never out. Not all of them may sing. You did not write under that line. You did not show Kemp the page. Wednesday's dinner made a proverb out of a wrong date. Twelve pence, right to the farthing, and nobody loses a shilling by mistake. Thursday was one bough. There was no ribbon. Long Meg had no sum. A garland went over the mud, and a hush still came for Navarre. You wrote nothing at evening prayer.

Saturday paid thirty-four shillings and called it received. The court went to Greenwich, and with it my lord and Kettleby, and any summons before Michaelmas. Edith's two hands priced a sleeve, and the third gesture was the smaller one. Edmund is fifteen. The letter tells him to finish school. It encloses nothing, and it waits for the carrier of the fifteenth. The one bough is still over the door. You have not told the house about Sunday. The comedy is in your hand for the cloak, and the chronicle is still under the cat.
