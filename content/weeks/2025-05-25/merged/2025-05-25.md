## Pulse

- title: Four willow wands, and three parish marks
- one sentence: You ate a short loaf, struck a glass off the king's page, and walked three parish marks before bread and ale.

Gib was on the shut chronicle, and you left him. You said the glass was not bought. After morning prayer you were home, and you struck the order for a glass off the king's page. He comes down. Alse showed Gillis the post in Bishopsgate Street, the corner by Crosby Place, and the stone in the garden wall. It took forty minutes. There was bread and ale after.
