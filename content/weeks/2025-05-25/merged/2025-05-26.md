### Page

26 May. At the board, after Navarre.

Susanna. You are twelve. I have set it in the almanac, where the day already knew.

You have found a fault in my last, and I look to be told it plainly. The Master of the Revels has corrected me, and it did me good.

There is a queen in the play I am making. She learns bad news by listening to gardeners. Is that likely? You read. Tell me.

My love to Judith and to Hamnet, in this same line.

Anne. The king is two-thirds made. I played Navarre this afternoon with the day in my mind, and I came in a line late.

If the house could buy only one thing this summer, which should wait?

I began an answer under the question and struck it out. Nothing of it can be read. The strike is a blot.

The thimble was 2s 6d. It went on the fifteenth of May. It should be in the house already, if you kept it back. This letter should have gone with it. It goes by Hobbins on the fifth of June.

Susanna, I will hear you read when I come in August.
