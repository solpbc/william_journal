[04:00] alse_pinder: Leave, and one band. I will not buy another.
[04:10] william_shakespeare: No one is buying. The thimble is already gone to Stratford, and this paper is not a purchase.
[04:22] dorcas_ansell: Then leave Stratford out of my supper. Pass the salt. The clerk did his office and went. We will do ours.
[04:36] alse_pinder: I will take the band down after the dishes.
[04:46] william_shakespeare: Take it down. Sunday can have the reading. Tonight has the leave, and the loaf as it was this morning.
[04:54] alse_pinder: I will remember the loaf. I am only washing a band.
[05:00] dorcas_ansell: This morning and tonight are the same loaf. Remember that when you go upstairs to your book.
