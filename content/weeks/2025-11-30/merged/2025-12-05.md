### Page

Friday, before Egeon. The page is the question. It is not the line.

Five.
Six?
Ten, because Richard had a prison, a groom and four murderers, and this man has a cup and a kiss.

I have not answered. The rehearsal does not get the answer. If he asks when the house is quiet, the answer will be in my mouth and not on this paper.

I am not writing the five words down to see whether they look like enough. Enough is a decision. A decision written here would be a yes I have not given.

Egeon at two. The old father explains nothing. Then the evening. Then, if he cannot sleep, the no, or the cowardice. I do not yet know which I will call it.
