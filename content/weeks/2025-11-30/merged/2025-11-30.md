Advent Sunday, the xxx of November 1595. St Andrew in the same morning.

What must be done before Richmond.

The lovers, Saturday, at the Theatre. From here to the court the houses are citizens and their wives. The gowns went with the term.
Thursday, the court rolls. After that copy, nothing, unless a fault in the playing demands it. The undertaking of the xxvj. The thumb-biting is already out. Two hours and a half for her Majesty. That is Garnish's cut, not a new one.
Three nights, already named. Stephen, the Navarre comedy. John, the lovers. Innocents, the old Errors. Not the wood. The wood is not ready by being wanted.
The Master, the xvj, at Clerkenwell. Heminges and Bryan are named for the money. The money is not in the house.
Hobbins, Thursday. The letter of the xvj, which lays the Christmas money in three parts and does not name the parts, and which asks after the children's engine. The six lines of the xxiiij: the brief granted, Master Quiney a good man, the purse in his other hose. Those six are not written again. They wait.
Thursday the xviij, the pack, and lx s. with it. Not this Thursday. No visit. Arrangements, not a surprise at the door.
Monday the xxij, Mistress Ansell's Christmas quarter, xx s., by noon. Not this week.
Quiney still owes j li. Not asked for. The wash is already hers to order, at iiij d. the week, out of the Michaelmas money. Not opened again.

What I would only like to better. Struck this evening, and not copied fair.

Another page of the hard-handed men, before anyone asks to see it.
Ten lines where five will do, in the vault. He will ask. Asking is not the same as having them.
The wood, finished because December is long. December is not that long.
