Saturday night. She was ahead of me. At the door she said, "Good night." Nothing else. I am not writing the nothing else into a meaning.

Alse beside her in the gallery. The church hat. First time in a playhouse. I did not ask the price. I do not know the price. I will not invent one in the morning to be tidy.

Forty shillings in the pouch, the week's share. The boxes were twelve pound thirteen. John said both and would not divide them. Eight times forty shillings is sixteen pound, and the house was not sixteen pound. I have not made them agree, and I am not going to count the coffer to comfort myself with a total.

Tuesday's dinner was threepence, already on her slate. The firing and washing is sixpence the week, the rate, not a sum I laid out tonight. I am not adding meals I did not eat in order to have a handsome reckoning.

The moon was over the church when I was still at the gate. Then the street. Then this. Good night is the whole of the door.
