### Page

Monday night. Not the list. The list is shut till Thursday's copy. This is the evening, and the evening is not Richmond.

Hard-handed men. A tailor who cannot read the bill, and will not be a fool for that. He has the play wrong in his mouth. He wants it right with his whole heart. That is the joke, if it is a joke. Kemp, the xxvij: men first, then asses. I crossed the other kind out. I will not write it back to please a tired yard.

The wall is a man. He knows it is a man. He is sorry the moon will not come when it is called, and he stands anyway.

Six of them, and a lion that is also a man. No duke yet to be afraid of them. No finish. The copy of the lovers is another book.

Margin: do not count the leaves. The year may count them. I will not.
