[01:14] thomas_hobbins: Twopence the letter, sixpence the parcel, and nothing for a speech in the doorway. Three papers and one tying is a letter. You have not made it a parcel. Twelve hours to Wycombe if the way's dry. That figure is how I measure a road. It is not a promise about this one.
[01:36] william_shakespeare: Twopence. Here. There is no visit hiding in the sheet, and no pack. The pack is the eighteenth. This Thursday is the play, and the play is the line you just read.

[02:50] thomas_hobbins: I have your twopence. I have your line. Go home. The Bell is full of men who think a carrier is a confessor, and I am not one.
