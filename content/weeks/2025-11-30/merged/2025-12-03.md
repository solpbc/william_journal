### Page

Wednesday, after the apparel. Home. Two Gentlemen is other men's afternoon.

One cue in the vault. Two men heard it.

The book heard the cressets already up, and the speech finding the light there.
The player heard the dark held, and the fire coming in when he says light.

I said: light them when he says light, and not before. I did not say which of those mornings the book is to keep. Thursday's copy keeps one. A fault in the playing may still be mended. An attractive change may not. These two are the fault, because a book cannot keep both.

Not a printed word. Not a line I am going to fetch out of a stall to decide it. The word is light.

Two hours and fifty, by Garnish's glass. The court paper is still two hours and a half. I am not mixing them tonight.

The hall was said. It was not priced. I am not writing a figure under a sentence that refused one.
