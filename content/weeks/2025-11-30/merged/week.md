# Week of Sun 30 Nov 2025

## Reflection

You came out of St Helen's on Advent Sunday still on the last chord, and Mistress Ansell had to say your name twice. In the afternoon you set four voices down and said the scene was still ahead of you. You struck the half of December that was only what you would like to better. The almanac got one line. Richmond is twenty-six days off.

The mornings belonged to Romeo. Nick stayed under the note. On Wednesday, in apparel, you told them to light the torches when Romeo says light, and not before, and you carried two hearings of that cue until Thursday. Thursday's copy kept the fire on the word. You let a better word for the Friar go, and a turn in the Nurse's lament. The boy carried the packet. At the Bell, Hobbins read back that the lovers are played on Saturday, and you paid twopence. On Friday night Richard called from Holywell and asked for ten lines, then six. You said no. The death stays five words, and the words stayed in your mouth.

Saturday she went in her church hat, with Alse beside her. It was her first time inside a playhouse. You spoke the first line alone. The sun was gone before four, and the light was brought when he called for it. You shut the play as the Prince. The boxes held twelve pound thirteen, above October's eleven pound six. The share was forty shillings. John said both figures and refused to divide them, because eight times forty shillings is sixteen pound and the house was twelve pound thirteen. At the door she said good night, and stopped.
