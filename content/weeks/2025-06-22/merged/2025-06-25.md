- title: Told the house the king, a bad example
- one sentence: You stayed in, looked at the forty leaves, and at evening told the house the king, and she called it a bad example.
- details: You did not go out. The forty leaves stayed shut under the same rule, and you did not copy them onto a clean sheet. Gib stayed on the Holinshed. You did not open the coffer. At evening Mistress Ansell said she had heard no walking for a fortnight, and asked what you had done up there. You told her the whole, since it is finished: a king who thinks the name is the thing, a cousin wronged who comes back and takes everything, a crown handed across, a prison, a coffin. She heard it through with her knife down. The cousin reigns, uneasily. She called that indigestion, and a bad example, and she would not show it this month of all months. Verbeke asked who speaks for the king at the end. A groom, and the man who had him killed. Alse asked why he broke the glass. It told him the truth, and she said it was a good glass and he should have kept it. The fellows have not heard it. Kemp was not in the room, and he has not been told.
