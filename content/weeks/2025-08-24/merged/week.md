# Week of Sun 24 Aug 2025

## Reflection

You kept morning prayer at St Helen's, and then you went to Smithfield, because tomorrow there was a house to open and tonight the lines were left for the opening. Pope came away with a bolster and the feathers thrown in. Sam spent his sixpence on a whistle after a long look at a knife, and Phillips took the bone. Burbage stood through the wooden king three times. A cut-purse was taken a yard from Arthur, who gave the arrest back in four voices. Kemp bowed from a ring of two hundred. Gilbert, behind the points and the hat-bands, affected to be a stranger to the players. You bought three gingerbread men before you thought, one with gilt, and Mistress Ansell said fairings rot the teeth. The back complained from the fourth hour.

On Monday both houses opened. You walked on as Navarre in the smell of fresh paint, and the oath of three years had a new sound in your mouth. Margery Pell had fifty-two shillings, prentices let off for the fair and their masters after them, and the lawyers missed her count. Garnish ticked fourteen lines and broke a pen. All five hired men answered, and Raye may bank on both sides of the water. Walking home, the company took its six-day gear like a mill-wheel taking the water. The letter waited. Tuesday the strap was as it was on Saturday, and you played Egeon. Wednesday was the first rehearsal of the king, and Kemp's name was off the rolls. Kemp took the jig as a man takes a groat in settlement of a pound, and he said that he and the Master are served alike. A plain reading brought Burbage's joins back the second time through. You stooped for the armour-basket, the back answered, and you played Gaunt from a stool, which he called the best thing in it. You came home by way of every wall.

Thursday you sat, because the back kept you in the chair, and you cut four lines of the boy's that admired the window. A neighbour scolded a prentice at the casement. You shut it and missed the air. In the afternoon you were Egeon again, and at night you marked Friday for the yard. Friday you paid a penny at the Rose and stood by a post. Alleyn, in gilded mail, played a king already sure of himself, who fills the house like water in a jug. On the tables, their king is all is and yours is all seems, and five weeks. He asked if you had come to count his inches. You had come to learn them. You came home by the Bridge and saved the penny. Saturday's reckoning was six-and-twenty shillings, the first share since July. The coffer stays at twenty pound fifteen. Heminges named Monday at two as The Shrew. You went to bed at nine.
