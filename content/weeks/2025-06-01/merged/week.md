# Week of Sun 1 Jun 2025

## Reflection

You began at St Helen's. Master Thwaite read the Lord Mayor's precept against blue starch out to the fourteenth head, with the Mayor ten yards off in a white ruff and a draper's wife in the north aisle blue at the band. Alse's band was white. At the door she asked who decides where a permitted shade ends, and nobody told her. At home you called it a war with a colour and stopped. The garden stayed open. The parliament sheet stayed blank. Susanna's letter stayed on the chest until Thursday.

The company set a month and then spent the week inside it. On Monday, after Errors, Heminges took the comedy for the ninth and asked for a Labour's Won at Michaelmas, with the same four and the weddings. You did not give him one. You said you have a king, and that he is about to be unkinged. Burbage set that for Michaelmas, when the Inns are back. Bryan asked whether a king put down will pass the Master. You said it is in the chronicle. Kemp asked what part he has. You said the parts are not cast. They agreed that some of the summer's takings may be kept back if playing thins, and they named no sum. Trinity term opens on the twentieth, eighteen days off. On Tuesday you bought nothing at Leadenhall, left the penny loaf unmeasured, and wrote three proverbs under Prices. On Wednesday you read the king from the first gage to the castle wall, drew a line down a margin, and named the deposing, the prison and the end. You did not write them. At the Jackdaw Ralph Raye brought seven pounds and two shillings for a throne in the heavens, and three pounds ten for The Seven Days of the Week, played on the Bankside the day before. You set a glass at four shillings against the chair. James said five pound and a lease. He did not have the five pound. Kemp asked for a barrel. You said a boy and two men can build a castle out of air, and it costs nothing to strike. Nothing was built. On Thursday Field called from the shop, asked you to be angry at his table on Sunday, and asked for the Holinshed. You said Sunday, and the Holinshed stays. After Errors you gave Hobbins Susanna's letter at twopence. He had nothing for you.

Friday was the Bedlam gate. You saw the Bedlam gate standing open, the hasp wrenched, a cap in the road, and backs running north. You did not see a man brought out. A water-bearer's story of the Lord Mayor stayed talk. At the Theatre Margery's figure was two hundred. Cowley had an account and no count. They agreed only that people ran toward the noise, and you would not go down to choose. You rehearsed Richard the Third with the street still audible, and you played it at two. At supper Verbeke said the man was a silkweaver, like himself, and English, so it is not yet us, and he cut his bread smaller. On Saturday the house played The Two Gentlemen at two. You were not in it. Heminges shut the bag at twelve shillings. Last Saturday's bag had not been shut. You did not count a coffer, and you sent nothing home.
