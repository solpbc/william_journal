# Week of Sun 28 Dec 2025

## Reflection

You began the week on the palace side of the green, and you walked to the inn. Morning prayer was at the inn's table. At night you played the old comedy once, found a roll in a chest, and heard Nick's voice come back a fifth lower in the jig. He stopped, bowed, and Arthur took the next verse. You told him it had come. Nobody sang on the way back. Kemp saved the button story for a different inn.

On Monday my lord's man brought four pounds for hose, and John split it into eight parts of ten shillings. You put your ten behind both locks, and you did not count the rest. The Queen's three nights stayed on the other leaf. You were at Old Swan by two. At the Bull the seven of you sat down with no summons. Kemp told the button story at last, with spoons for the tapers. The button stayed lost. You were home by four.

On Tuesday you wrote Anne the sentence from the brook, and the coffer as nine pounds more than at New Year. You did not give the total, and you did not ask anyone the price of a house. You wrote one page that joins the workmen to the queen who wakes, and you did not number the leaves under it. At Field's fire you called the pages a dozen, and there is no last act. Jacqueline told you not to punish the queen. You told them you have not been paid. On Wednesday the loaf was a penny and two fingers short of the notch. After supper the book came to thirty-three pounds ten, counted twice. The three nights stay expected, and they are not in that figure. You marked twenty pounds in your head and wrote no reason beside it. Then you lit a fresh candle and took up the twelfth leaf.
