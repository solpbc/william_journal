[02:52] william_shakespeare: Two quires. Fourpence halfpenny the quire. Lay them by. I am not paying for a kindness in the same breath as hearing it. And how is old Master Shakespeare, if the Blackfriars has heard what Henley Street has not yet sent me?

[03:54] richard_field: God keep thee, Richard. No. God keep thee, Will. I have had a morning of type, and I am saying my own name back to myself.
[04:08] william_shakespeare: God keep thee, Richard.
[04:14] richard_field: And thee.
