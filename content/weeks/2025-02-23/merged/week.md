# Week of Sun 23 Feb 2025

## Reflection

You began at St Helen's. Master Thwaite turned the sand-glass twice and counted ten days to the fast, Ash Wednesday the fifth of March. Sir John Spencer sat three rows ahead. He did not speak. At dinner the loaf was a penny and short by a finger. You paid threepence, and sixpence for the week's firing and washing, once. After dinner the house was one spoon short. Mistress Ansell accused nobody. Alse found the spoon in the ink, black to the neck, and Mistress Ansell said it had been put there to be found. The Sunday letter stayed folded.

On Monday you bought a horn at twopence. You still had no newer word of your father than mending. At the Cross Keys the house was two-thirds of Thursday's, and louder, and Margery Pell named no sum. Kemp added thirteen lines. Garnish put the two days at twenty-four. There is no song in the tally. Kemp said they had laughed at his lines, and that he had counted. You told Burbage what the yard did with the ending. A lawyer asked who made the play, and Burbage pointed with his thumb. On Tuesday James Burbage put a knife two inches into a post laid in 1576. The ground falls back to Giles Allen on the thirteenth of April, 1597. You gave two mornings in March. No price was named. You opened The Comedy of Errors, and you were idle until the end.

On Wednesday Richard Field called from the Blackfriars. Venus will be printed again, small, and not a penny in it for you. You were glad to be thought portable. He will lay by two quires at fourpence halfpenny. You did not pay. On Thursday the tally stayed at twenty-four lines. Hobbins took the Sunday letter and brought Anne's. You paid him twopence. Gilbert looked at the horses and left before you opened the sheet. The cough is gone. She will believe the horse when she hears it. You said it was just news. A joiner's man took three cut leaves of the comedy out from under the stool. Mistress Ansell paid him threepence. You kept the wedge. On Friday, from the west window, you saw a woman come down the church step with an older woman. You did not go down. At night you fair-copied one line, said four words, and put a small cross in the almanac. On Saturday the moon was new. At the reckoning nobody named a share. Last Saturday's thirty shillings stayed last Saturday's. Edith's eight and the mercer's fifty stayed unpaid. The Queen's twenty pounds was not had. At home you set down no sum for the coffer.
