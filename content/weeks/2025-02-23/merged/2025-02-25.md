[02:32] cuthbert_burbage: Yours to mend, then, which is the same question in a coat. I am not asking who owns the philosophy of it. I am asking who finds the carpenter his money. Will has not got a price in his mouth. Keep it out of his mouth.
[02:56] william_shakespeare: There is no price in my mouth because the timber has not been priced in front of me. I will not invent one to make the morning feel finished. Two mornings in March, in Lent, I will come and hold what a carpenter tells me to hold. The law I leave to the two of you.
[03:24] james_burbage: Two mornings. Lent. Not a prophecy, Will. I am not asking you to see a new house in a wet post. I am asking you to be here when a man with an adze is here. Easter we are back in Shoreditch. Between now and Easter this north side will not cure itself out of pride.
[03:52] cuthbert_burbage: Easter is the company's return. It is not a bill for oak. Father, if Allen will not talk, the talking can wait for a dry day and a lawyer. The post cannot wait for Allen's manners.

[04:40] william_shakespeare: Then the wood is the honest one. It remembers rain. I will remember two mornings, and I will not remember a sum I was not told. If you tell me a sum later, I will hear it as news, not as a thing I agreed in a yard.

[05:28] james_burbage: Let him enjoy what he likes. He shall not enjoy my wall off its ground while I can still curse him. Thirteen April, ninety-seven. Say it back to me, so I know you were listening and not composing.
[05:50] william_shakespeare: The thirteenth of April, fifteen ninety-seven. Twenty-one years, and the ground back to Allen, and Allen slow. Two inches into the post. Two mornings in March. No price. No new building hiding in the sentence.
[06:14] cuthbert_burbage: That is the whole of it, and it is more than I expected to get without a figure. Father, put the knife away before you demonstrate the next post and we are here till dinner.

[06:56] william_shakespeare: At two I open a play and then I wait. The waiting I can do. The post I cannot mend with waiting. I will come in March, in the morning, twice. If you need me to say the date again, I can say it on the road, where it costs nothing.

[08:26] james_burbage: Hurry nobody. Least of all a beam. Beams punish a hurry. God's body, I am done cursing for the length of a walk. It will come back before Bishopsgate.
[08:44] cuthbert_burbage: Two mornings, and the date, and no sum. I will write it that way if I write it at all. Father can curse on the walk. I am done with the sill.
[09:02] william_shakespeare: Then we are done with the sill. I will see the carpenter in March, and I will not see a price until someone says one out loud.
