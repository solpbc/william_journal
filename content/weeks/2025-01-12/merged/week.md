# Week of Sun 12 Jan 2025

## Reflection

You began the week well, and you ended it in bed. On Sunday you heard morning prayer out to fourteenthly, wrapped two of the three things Anne asked for, and wrote to ask what the third was. Mistress Ansell knows the name. She did not say it. Gilbert carried the parcel to the Bell. On Thursday you paid sixpence for it and twopence for the letter, eightpence in all. Nothing from Stratford was put in your hand.

The cold began on Monday, in a sneeze over the fifth act. You cut the blot away, wrote fourteen lines, and stayed in while Titus played. On Tuesday and Thursday you played the Knave with the throat getting worse, and you denied it once before you went on. On Friday you changed three cues, kept Egeon, and got through the tale on breath and consonants. Kemp said, kindly, that you never sounded so near death. On Saturday Cowley came as far as the chair, took the current word, and refused the reason. Errors played at two, and you stayed in bed. Nobody in the room made that a kindness you were doing the company.

The comedy is still unfinished. The messenger's half-line is empty. The songs are not written, and the last line is not written. On Saturday night you struck a line and wrote it back, because you could not tell whether it was bad. Edith's eight shillings is still owing till Lady Day. Nothing was paid on it. The Queen's twenty pound was not fetched, and it is not yours. The coffer was not counted.
