# Week of Sun 2 Feb 2025

## Reflection

You kept Candlemas at St Helen's and at the lodging. The sermon was a candle that is spent, and you lost a minute of it to the owl. The Mayor did not turn. At the board the penny loaf was a finger short, the meat was threepence, and the firing stays sixpence until Lady Day. You wrote the twins, who are ten, two groats to be spent foolishly, a twist of comfits, and a riddle for Susanna to keep. You told Anne you are mended of the cold, the comedy is done, and that Lent waits until you know the day. The packet sat in the room until Thursday.

Monday was Titus, and your voice held. You did not hear a price. Tuesday, on Field's step, a forme was on the press. He asked for the day a chronicle would come home. You did not name one. You will ask again in Lent. At noon the gown became fifty shillings, watchet taffeta and copper lace, half down asked and left on credit, the stock at eleven shillings till Saturday, the whole of it set against the Queen's twenty pounds whenever those come. You played Fair Em. You wrote the fifty under Edith's eight. You paid neither.

Wednesday you read Mahomet on the post by the Bull. Ralph gave it in a breath, and the head says nothing worth the powder. You did not learn a receipt. Titus went on without you. You struck a scrap so the folder stayed at two leaves. Thursday Nick walked the Princess's speech. The speech is not copied out. You moved two stops. A boy has a pint of breath. You sewed the groats at home. You went to the Bell after the play. After the Sins, Hobbins took the letter. Twelve hours to Wycombe if the way were dry. The twins' day will be nine days old. The outside of the packet says the groats are within, one to each twin. Anne's letter was a pan, a ladle, a turn at the well, and your father mending. The ledger has twopence, and that the letter was late.

Friday you stayed in. By one candle you fair-copied a sonnet onto a leaf, and you laid it with the two older leaves. The leaf has no title, no number, and no name. You left the new leaf as you had copied it. You did not say it aloud. You were in bed by ten and awake about two. Saturday at nine the old book was walked, and Kemp stayed civil. The tally was not opened. The reckoning wrote the fifty and the eight as unpaid, named no share, and left your twopence in your own book. You did not count the coffer.
