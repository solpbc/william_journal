# Week of Sun 30 Mar 2025

## Reflection

You gave your mother your arm, and she spent the morning on shirts and on one sentence. The place beside her stayed empty. Under the responses she said a grace you knew, and you let it pass. On Monday the sword stayed on the nail, Hamnet's leave was tomorrow morning, and you gave Anne the opinion you had promised to carry. She said the ladies were right to make them wait, and a twelvemonth was short.

Tuesday the carpenter said fire makes work, and Anne said it makes debt. You answered, Susanna told you so, and you carried the bucket. Wednesday your father named his debts smaller than they are, showed you the old pattern once, and put it back. You did not correct him. Your mother remembered the ink. Thursday butter was fourpence. Quiney asked nothing. At the bakehouse you finished Anne's purchase after you had listened, and Susanna's penny came home in a paper.

Friday you paid the five pounds. Forty-two shillings left the canvas. Eighteen stayed in it. Hamnet Sadler called the morning eight loaves. Saturday she weighed the eighteen before she untied it, named malt and corn and one pair of shoes fewer, and said it is the one leather. At the brook you gave her three answers. She left them. In the yard the leaf held buds, a grey gown, and the brook. In the garden you said it was not well done.
