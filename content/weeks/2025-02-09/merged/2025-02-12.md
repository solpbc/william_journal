[04:52] abel_garnish: Hold. I have a margin. I am writing a K at the head of it. I am not writing a sum. A sum is a Saturday habit, and this is Wednesday, and Wednesday is not a total.
[05:14] william_shakespeare: Write the K, if you must have a head to the column. Do not write a figure under it for my sake. I have not asked for a figure. I have asked which lines are the better ones.
[05:36] will_kemp: Better is the one that gets the laugh before your next cue buries it. Two lines longer is two lines the yard has time to love. You may call that theft when you are less courteous.
[05:58] thomas_pope: The gowns will thin. What they loved in term they will not all stay to love. I should like the play to survive a yard of ordinary men, not only of students.
[06:18] sam_latchet: I still have the farthings in front of me. I have not taken them. May I go and stand where the cue is, and not where the sale is?
[06:36] abel_garnish: Go and stand. The K is a letter. It is not your price. Nobody is buying you out of the book.
[06:52] william_shakespeare: Again, from the offer. I will hear the two lines, and I will not keep them if they are only noise. Tell me which, Will. I asked so that I might write them, not so that I might lose them.
[07:14] will_kemp: Hear them, then. I will not label them. A clown who labels his laughs is already half a book-keeper, and Abel has that stool.
[07:32] abel_garnish: The pass again. Two lines, if they come, I will know. I will not turn them into a number while Dick is still at the wall.
[07:50] thomas_pope: Let him have the wall. A love speech in a stable is still a love speech. The horses have no votes, which is more than I can say for a term-time yard.
[08:10] william_shakespeare: Hilary term ends today. I am not going to Westminster to see it end. The thinning will show itself without my help.
[08:28] will_kemp: Then we are agreed. You will be courteous, Abel will be alphabetical, and I will be two lines longer until the yard tells me to stop. The yard is the only master I came here to please.
