# Week of Sun 9 Feb 2025

## Reflection

The week began at St Helen's. Master Thwaite turned the sand-glass twice and stopped. Sir John Spencer gave the responses from his pew. He did not speak to you. At the lodging the loaf was a finger short, and the board was threepence. You wrote Anne a letter and left it on the desk. You told the new comedy at too great a length, and you told her of fifteen barges on the first of the month. You did not set her question first, and you did not name a day. In the fields you said Navarre's first line until it sat. The tables took three phrases. You told yourself that a king who opens on Fame had better not have a hole in his hose. At night you began two lines, and you set Monday's hour for the lodge.

On Monday you left a sealed paper at Southampton House and sat four minutes on the bench. My lord was at court. Master Florio was within, teaching. You did not see him. You played Egeon, and Kemp stayed civil. On Tuesday Davy Gannet took a penny and gave you forty ships, the Queen's blessing, and rain that did not come. You were not in the Knave. You wrote the forty sail onto Sunday's letter. It stayed on the desk. At the Split Hazel supper was sixpence. A gunner's chart of beaten gold was put out when he called a doubt disloyalty. You wrote that gold is surer the further it is. On Wednesday Kemp bit three farthings and would not say which of his lines were the better ones. You asked, very courteously. Garnish headed a margin with a K. He wrote no figure under it. Burbage took the love speech to the stable wall. Hilary term ended. The gowns will thin. You were in the gentlemen that afternoon. You did not name the part.

Thursday the column was still headed K. You played Egeon again, and you wrote Friday as the Rose. On Friday you were not in the Knave. You paid a penny at the Rose and stood by a post on the left. The laugh started at a stride. Henslowe kept the count at the door. Alleyn was cordial for a minute. At home you left the unkind sentence on the page because it was the one you had written. On Saturday you were in the gentlemen again. John Heminges set your share at twenty-two shillings. The fifty and the eight stayed owed. The coffer stayed upstairs. No figure was put under the K. The letter is still on the desk.
