The iv of Maye 1595. At the board, one candle. The comedy is in Blackfriars. This page is the king, and it is not a new discovery.

The line is already on the tables from Tuesday last: not all of them may sing. York's breath runs short. Gaunt's runs long. The King's does not run out. That was the work of removing a convenience. Tonight it still stands. Nothing under it is the speech of England.

In the margin: a cheese that tasted of home. Thursday's duke, forty lines, and a part.

The foot of the page is empty. Not a king on the ground. Not a line of the island.
