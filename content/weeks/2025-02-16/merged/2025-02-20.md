## Pulse

- title: They went out quiet
- one sentence: You played the new comedy at two, the boxes held seven pound eleven, the yard went out quiet, and at supper you asked when it was to be.
- details: You rehearsed in the morning and still had no total in the margin. At noon, by the inn, a bill on a post in Gracechurch Street gave the title only, in black-letter, spotted with rain. You went on first, in a paper crown and old velvet. The Inns were in the yard, term or no term. Burbage's Berowne had them. Pope's Armado stopped the play twice. Kemp added eleven lines, and Garnish ticked every one. Nick and Arthur sent the Muscovite blunders back. Sincler came in in black, and the laugh went out. By the end the yard was blue dusk and two torches. The boxes held seven pound eleven shillings. Margery Pell said they went out quiet, and that she had never known a comedy do it.
