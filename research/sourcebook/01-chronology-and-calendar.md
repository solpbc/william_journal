# Source book 01: the decade chronology and the 1595 calendar

**Section of the william journal source book** (request deliverables 1 and 5). Reconciles the three research reports in this folder (chatgpt.md, gemini.md, claude.md) and checks their load-bearing claims against primary or authoritative sources. Compiled 2026-11-14. Project: the william journal · request: research-request.md.

> **Superseded column (design decision, 2026-11-14):** the journal holds **2025**, not 1995. 2025's calendar is identical to 1595's (Julian): it starts on a Wednesday, has no leap day, and has Easter on 20 April. So the 1595 weekday in every row *is* the 2025 weekday, and the "1995 weekday" column and § on the 1995 mapping no longer apply. [Synthesis § decisions](../00-synthesis.md#decisions-for-the-editor).

## How to read this

**Tiers** are the request's: **T1** documented · **T2** scholarly consensus · **T3** contested · **T4** tradition · **T5** imagined. Two extra labels:

- **T1·calc**: the rule is documented (the Prayer Book calendar, the Easter computus) and the 1595 date is arithmetic done in this file.
- **T2·calc**: computed from a rule that is itself scholarly reconstruction (the law terms, from Harrison's description; Sunday adjustments not checked).
- **calc**: astronomical computation (moon phases). As certain as the arithmetic, but it is not testimony: no one in 1595 is recorded seeing it.

**Dates** are Julian (Old Style). New Style ran 10 days ahead, so foreign and Spanish dates are 10 days later (N.S.). Dates from 1 January to 24 March carry the legal year both ways: **15 Mar 1594/5** is 15 March 1595 in our terms and in the journal.

**Weekdays** were all computed in this file with a Julian day-number formula. The calculator was first checked against known days: Thu 4 Oct 1582 (Julian), Fri 15 Oct 1582 (Gregorian), Tue 23 Apr 1616 (Julian, Shakespeare's death), Sat 14 Nov 2026. It was also checked against a deliberately wrong claim, which it rejected. Moon phases use Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms* ch. 49, ΔT ≈ 2 minutes, in UT (London local mean time is within a minute of UT). Controls: the new moon of 6 Jan 2000, 18:15 UT, and the full moon of 23 Apr 2024, 23:50 UT, both reproduced.

**The 1995 mapping (the open question).** Both years have 365 days, so every 1595 Julian date maps to the same month and day in 1995 with a constant weekday shift: **the 1995 weekday is the 1595 weekday minus three** (Wed → Sun). Both options:

| Option | How | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| Match dates | 1595 Julian *d* → 1995 *d* | Weekdays wrong all year. A 1595 Sunday shows as a 1995 **Thursday**: church, the theatre's dark Sundays and "upon Sunday next" all land mid-week. Easter Day 20 Apr is a Thursday. |
| Match weekdays | 1595 Julian *d* → 1995 *d* **+ 3 days** (or −4) | Dates off by three. Easter Day becomes Sun 23 Apr 1995 (St George's Day, his traditional birthday). With +3, 29–31 Dec 1595 fall in 1996; with −4, 1–4 Jan 1595 fall in 1994. |

Real 1995 (Gregorian) Easter was 16 April, so neither option lines up the two Easters. First of each month, 1595 Julian weekday / 1995 weekday: Jan Wed/Sun · Feb Sat/Wed · Mar Sat/Wed · Apr Tue/Sat · May Thu/Mon · Jun Sun/Thu · Jul Tue/Sat · Aug Fri/Tue · Sep Mon/Fri · Oct Wed/Sun · Nov Sat/Wed · Dec Mon/Fri.

For the weekday-matching option, add three days to the date. Henslowe rows are limited to season boundaries and new plays ("ne"): the full Rose repertory belongs with deliverable 6. ## 3. Weekday and moon audit of the reports

- **Weekdays: no report is wrong.** I checked 100 dated weekday claims across the three reports' chronologies and calendars (CL 55, CG 13 beyond those shared, GM 32, including 1592–94 dates such as Fri 3 Mar 1591/2 and Sun 1 Dec 1594), plus the 40 legibly dated weekdays in GM's scene-seed table. Every one matches the computation. CG's month grids are also right.
- **Moon: GM is right, CL is a day late at several full moons.** GM's phase list (Meeus, with times) agrees with this file's computation to within a minute. CL's "mean lunation, ±1 day" table puts full moon a day late in March, May, June, July, August, September and December (e.g. "~12–13" June against 11 Jun 22:05 UT; "~7" December against 6 Dec), and new moon a day late in October and December. CL's two derived claims still hold: new moon 27 June puts the Tower Hill riot (29 June) on a dark night, and new moon 18 Feb 1595/6 fits Wiles's "silver bow" for the Berkeley–Carey wedding. CG gives no phases.

## 6. Report claims found to be wrong

- **CG**: second Stratford fire "21 September [1595], T1" (unsupported; see #4) · Clontibret "25–27 March" (it was May) · *Galfrido and Bernardo* on 18 May as a T1 Rose performance (a Collier forgery) · *1 Hercules* at £4 4s (Henslowe has £3 13s).
- **GM**: cold, wet 1595 summer as T1, with Stow and Forman's 1594 weather moved into August 1595 · a 1596 St Helen's tax record (the earliest is Nov 1597) · Shrove Tuesday 1595 "apprentice riot", T1 (no record) · the 1595 fire's street list (that was the 1594 fire) · the cause of the summer closure, stated as fact.
- **CL**: Raleigh's return in "Aug" (by 21 Sept is what's documented) · full moons a day late in seven months · Raleigh's departure under-tiered at T2.
- **All three**: Greenwich given as T1 for the 1594 plays when the record names no venue.

## 7. Gaps: what none of the reports could establish

1. **Shakespeare on any day of 1595.** The 15 March warrant is paperwork at Whitehall and does not place him anywhere. No other 1595 document names him.
2. **His own company's 1595.** No Henslowe-equivalent survives for the Chamberlain's Men: not their playing days, their repertory, or when they used the Theatre, the Curtain or the Cross Keys. Only court dates survive.
3. **Where he lodged in 1595.** St Helen's begins in the record in Nov 1597.
4. **Any Stratford visit in 1595**, and the route.
5. **The date of the second Stratford fire** (only the window: before 16 Nov 1595).
6. **Daily weather.** Two documented days; no London weather diary for 1595 was found. Harvest and prices are known only in aggregate.
7. **Plague and mortality figures for 1595.** No closure, and no numbers.
8. **The London and Stratford fair calendar for 1595.** Bartholomew Fair's exact days and the dates of Stratford's two charter fairs were not found. Southwark Fair (Our Lady Fair, early September) appears in no report and was not checked.
9. **Whether Lent playing was formally banned in 1595.** The Rose played ten days into Lent, and no order has been found.
10. **Why the Rose went dark in summer 1595.**
11. **What "K. Richard" at Hoby's was** (play, painting, or something else).
12. **Law-term Sunday adjustments** and the 1595 Michaelmas opening, checked only against Harrison's rule and not against a 1595 plea roll.
13. **An almanac printed for 1595** (it would give the period's own moon tables and feast list). The moon phases here are computed.
