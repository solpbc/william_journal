# 05 · Daily life, body, the news, and the contested and imagined Shakespeare

**Source-book section 5 of the william journal research.** Covers request deliverables 6, 7, 8 and 10 and research areas E, F and G (research-request.md). It reconciles three reports: chatgpt.md, gemini.md and claude.md. Compiled 2026-11-14. Spot-checks were run against the web the same day, and each one is named where it is used.

**Tiers** (from the request): **T1** documented · **T2** scholarly consensus · **T3** contested · **T4** tradition or anecdote · **T5** imagined. **INF** marks the editor's inference, meaning arithmetic or a reasonable reading of T1 or T2 facts. INF is never evidence. Any number labelled INF or T5 is ours to invent.

**Dates** are Julian (Old Style), with the year starting 1 January unless written as 1594/5. In 1595 the Gregorian date is 10 days later. 1 January 1595 was a Wednesday, and Easter fell on 20 April. The editor computed both, and they agree with Claude and Gemini.

**Report keys:** C = ChatGPT · G = Gemini · Cl = Claude · ✔ = spot-checked by the editor this session.

### 1b. Prices and wages

⚠ **Read before using any price.** Under the Assize of Bread, a loaf sold at a fixed price (½d, 1d) and its *weight* changed with the price of grain. In a dearth year **the penny loaf shrinks; it does not get dearer** (✔ LEMDO *Bread and Baking*). So write "the penny loaf is small this summer," never "bread rose to 2d."

**Dearth spikes.** There were four poor harvests running, 1594–97, with the worst in 1596–97 (T2). London ate imported Baltic grain and fared better than the countryside (T2). In June 1595 the city broke out over **fish (12 June, Billingsgate)** and **butter (13 June, Southwark)** (T1, ✔). In Stratford, a malting town, the Corporation's petitions blame malting for the fires and claim the trade is its only living (T1, Bearman 2000). Malt and barley hoarding is the Stratford version of dearth anxiety. Shakespeare himself held 10 quarters of malt in the 1598 survey (T1, Cl).

## 10. Gaps

1. **His 1595 body**: no illness, injury, height or regimen. Every body number is T5.
2. **London weather by day, 1595**: none found. Check Stow's *Annales*, Forman's diary (Halliwell 1849) and the Casebooks for 1596.
3. **London burial totals, 1595**: the weekly bills reportedly ran to December 1595, but no figures were located (Guildhall, LMA).
4. **Wheat and bread series**: rely on Hoskins (1964) or the Thorold Rogers / Beveridge price tables directly. The current figures are second-hand.
5. **Lodging rent and household costs** for a single man in St Helen's: Marsh (2021) may hold them; not extracted.
6. **Watermen's fares in 1595 exactly**: the regulation exists (EMLoT 2205); the fare table was not read.
7. **Travel**: horse-hire cost, carrier fares and the inn names on the Oxford and Banbury roads in 1595.
8. **Stratford fair and market days** beyond Thursday; the 1595 fire's exact day.
9. **His company's 1595 summer**: tour or not (REED Patrons and Performances).
10. **The martial-law proclamation**: the Hughes and Larkin number and date.
11. **Imagined works not in the reports** (Shaw, Bond, *Doctor Who*, Jude Morgan): not assessed.
12. **Bookstall imprints for 1595** (Sidney, Spenser, Daniel): added by the editor; verify in EEBO.
13. **Forman's weather quote**: confirm the year.
