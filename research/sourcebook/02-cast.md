# 02 · The cast: everyone around Shakespeare in 1595

**Source book section 2** (request deliverable 2, research area D, plus William himself) · reconciled 2026-11-14 from the three reports in `../`: chatgpt.md (**C**), gemini.md (**G**), claude.md (**Cl**). Tier definitions are in the research request.

**How to read this section**

- **Tiers.** T1 documented · T2 scholarly consensus · T3 contested · T4 tradition or anecdote · T5 imagined. A split such as "T1/T3" means the record is T1 and the reading of it is T3. No tier here is higher than the best report gave, and several are lower.
- **Ages** run through the year: "30 → 31 (Apr)" means 30 on 1 January 1595 and 31 from the birthday or baptism month. "bapt." is a baptism date, and birth was usually a few days earlier. Dates are Julian (Old Style). A record dated January to 24 March "1594" belongs to our 1595 and is written 1594/5.
- **Reports column.** Which reports name the person. A person only one report names is marked, for example "Cl only". "+ added" means none of the three named them; added from a checked source, and kept to a minimum.
- **✔ web** marks a fact checked this session against an outside source (the list is in § 6). Two reports agreeing is not counted as verification.
- **Grief care.** Hamnet is ten and alive for all of 1595. His burial date is recorded once below because the record holds it. Nothing built on this file foreshadows it.

### 2c. The company: the Lord Chamberlain's Men

Who the 1595 sharers were cannot be read off any 1595 document. The one T1 anchor is the three payees. The rest is reconstructed from later lists (the 1599 Globe lease, the 1603 patent, cast lists) and is T2. The usual reconstruction of eight sharers is Shakespeare, Burbage, Kemp, Heminges, Phillips, Pope, Bryan and one more. Cowley, Sly and Condell belong to the company's later records.
